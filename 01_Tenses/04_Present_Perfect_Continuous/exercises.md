# Bài tập thực hành — Present Perfect Continuous (B1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài). Chú ý ngữ cảnh để phân biệt tinh tế giữa **Present Perfect Continuous** và **Present Perfect Simple**. Không xem trước file `answers.md` khi đang làm bài.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức `have/has + (not) + been + V-ing` & Quy tắc chính tả thêm `-ing`.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Present Perfect Continuous** (chú ý thể khẳng định, phủ định hoặc nghi vấn):

1. Maya _________________________ (revise) her dissertation chapters since six o'clock this morning.
2. It _________________________ (rain) continuously for almost twelve hours, so the streets are flooded.
3. I'm worried about Dan; he _________________________ (work) overtime every single evening this month.
4. The audit committee _________________________ (investigate) the missing funds since last Tuesday.
5. Why _________________________ you _________________________ (procrastinate) over this simple application form all week?
6. Honestly, they _________________________ (not / concentrate) on the briefing because everyone is checking their phones.
7. How long _________________________ your brother _________________________ (commute) between Hai Phong and Hanoi?

---

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Động từ trạng thái / Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & Mục 4 — Các cách dùng cốt lõi, quy tắc biến đổi `-ing` và giới hạn của Stative Verbs.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Present Perfect Continuous** hoặc **Present Perfect Simple** (nếu động từ hoặc ngữ cảnh không cho phép dùng thì tiếp diễn):

8. Local air quality _________________________ (deteriorate) steadily over the past five years due to heavy traffic.
9. Look at Sarah — she looks utterly exhausted because she _________________________ (prepare) the annual report all night.
10. The two companies _________________________ (negotiate) the merger terms behind closed doors for nearly three months.
11. My neighbors _________________________ (renovate) their kitchen for weeks, and the drilling noise is driving me crazy.
12. You're completely out of breath! _________________________ you _________________________ (run) up the stairs?
13. Even when the market crashed, Elena _________________________ (persevere) with her small bakery since 2022.
14. We _________________________ (know) about the structural flaw in that bridge for months, yet nothing has been done.
15. Global coffee prices _________________________ (fluctuate) wildly since the severe drought hit South America.

---

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Result vs. Duration, Temporary vs. Permanent, Số lượng vs. Thời lượng.*

Chọn và chia động từ trong ngoặc ở **Present Perfect Continuous** hoặc **Present Perfect Simple** cho phù hợp nhất với sắc thái ngữ cảnh:

16. The language learners _________________________ (make) remarkable progress ever since they joined the speaking club, and they _________________________ (already / master) over 400 idioms.
17. Tom _________________________ (work) overtime all week to finish the prototype, and he _________________________ (finally / complete) the final test this afternoon.
18. For the past two months, Linh _________________________ (stay) at a friend's studio as a temporary arrangement, while her family _________________________ (own) their permanent home in Da Nang for thirty years.
19. These severe headaches _________________________ (persist) for ten days; how many painkillers _________________________ you _________________________ (take) since Monday?
20. I _________________________ (revise) Module 4 all afternoon, and I _________________________ (answer) thirty practice questions so far.
21. Unpaid invoices _________________________ (accumulate) on the manager's desk since April because the finance team _________________________ (be) short-staffed.
22. Why are your eyes so red? _________________________ you _________________________ (stare) at the computer screen to the point of exhaustion again?
23. Look! Someone _________________________ (break) the laboratory window, and glass is scattered all over the floor.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Bẫy đề thi về dấu hiệu vừa ngừng, Stative Verbs, Số lượng & Bị động.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (**A, B, C** hoặc **D**):

24. "You look soaked and out of breath!" — "Yes, I _______ in the pouring rain for twenty minutes because my umbrella broke."
   - A. walked
   - B. am walking
   - C. have been walking
   - D. have walked

25. Detective Evans _______ the cyberattack since dawn, yet he _______ a single reliable clue so far.
   - A. has been investigating / hasn't found
   - B. has investigated / hasn't been finding
   - C. is investigating / didn't find
   - D. has been investigating / wasn't finding

26. Our procurement team _______ with three overseas suppliers all morning, and they _______ two preliminary contracts.
   - A. negotiated / have been signing
   - B. has been negotiating / have signed
   - C. has negotiated / have been signing
   - D. is negotiating / signed

27. Interest rates _______ unpredictably for months, which _______ first-time homebuyers hesitant to borrow.
   - A. are fluctuating / has been making
   - B. fluctuated / made
   - C. have been fluctuating / has made
   - D. have been fluctuating / has been making

28. Why are you so exhausted? You look as if you _______ heavy boxes upstairs for hours.
   - A. have carried
   - B. have been carrying
   - C. carried
   - D. are carrying

29. Which sentence naturally suggests a **temporary** situation focusing on **duration** rather than a permanent state?
   - A. Dr. Tran has taught linguistics at this university since 1998.
   - B. Dr. Tran has been teaching a guest seminar in Kyoto for the last three weeks.
   - C. Dr. Tran has published twelve monographs on syntax.
   - D. Dr. Tran has belonged to the academic board for two decades.

30. Dust and clutter _______ in the storage room for years, so the whole archive _______ a thorough clean.
   - A. have been accumulating / needs
   - B. have accumulated / has been needing
   - C. are accumulating / needed
   - D. accumulated / is needing

31. Because the rural road _______ rapidly since the floods, heavy trucks are no longer allowed to cross the bridge.
   - A. has been deteriorated
   - B. is deteriorating
   - C. has been deteriorating
   - D. deteriorates

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 & Mục 5 — Lỗi sai kinh điển và bẫy Vietlish.*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** liên quan đến thì và ngữ pháp trọng tâm. Hãy chỉ ra phần sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng:

32. My mentor has been owning that vintage bookstore near the central station since 2010.
33. We are waiting for the technician for over an hour, and I still cannot concentrate on my work.
34. How many research papers have you been reading since the semester began last September?
35. How long has your sister commuting by train to the industrial park every weekday?
36. Even though the technical glitches have been persisting for days, nobody has been reporting them to IT yet.
37. Our interns have been making a great progress on the marketing campaign over the past fortnight.
38. The historic opera house has been renovating since January, so all performances have moved elsewhere.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc chuyển đổi từ Quá khứ đơn (`started / began`) sang Hiện tại hoàn thành tiếp diễn.*

Viết lại mỗi câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ gợi ý hoặc bắt đầu bằng cụm từ cho sẵn:

39. The contractors started renovating the old library five months ago, and they are still working on it.
   $\rightarrow$ The contractors ______________________________________________________________________.

40. When did the auditors begin investigating the regional branch's expenses?
   $\rightarrow$ How long __________________________________________________________________________?

41. The last time Kevin worked overtime was three weeks ago. *(Lưu ý bẫy phủ định!)*
   $\rightarrow$ Kevin hasn't ________________________________________________________________________.

42. She began persevering with her Japanese studies in 2023, and she still studies every evening.
   $\rightarrow$ She _____________________________________________________________________ since 2023.

43. My daily commute took forty minutes when I started this job in May, and it still takes the same today.
   $\rightarrow$ I ____________________________________________________________ forty minutes a day since May.

44. The trainees started making steady progress two months ago, and the improvement is still continuing.
   $\rightarrow$ The trainees _________________________________________________________________________.

## Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 5 & Mục 6 — Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt & Ứng dụng trong giao tiếp / công việc / IELTS.*

### A. Hoàn thành đoạn văn ngắn (Câu 45–47)
Chia các động từ trong ngoặc vào đúng thì (**Present Perfect Continuous** hoặc **Present Perfect Simple**) trong email cập nhật tiến độ sau:

> *Hi Alex,*
>
> *Just a quick update before Friday's meeting. Since Monday, I **(45)** _________________________ (revise) the client proposal based on the feedback you sent me. Our junior designer **(46)** _________________________ (negotiate) with the print shop about paper quality all morning, though we **(47)** _________________________ (procrastinate) a little on choosing the final cover color! Let's decide together at 3 p.m.*
>
> *Best,*
> *Minh*

### B. Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 48–50)
Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh, sử dụng **Present Perfect Continuous** và các từ vựng trọng tâm của bài:

48. Trông cậu thở hổn hển quá! Nãy giờ cậu dọn dẹp tầng hầm liên tục phải không?
49. Kể từ khi tốt nghiệp đại học, anh ấy vẫn luôn kiên trì theo đuổi con đường nghiên cứu y khoa. *(Gợi ý: pursue)*
50. Trong thập kỷ qua, chất lượng đất nông nghiệp trong khu vực này đã và đang xấu đi nghiêm trọng do tình trạng ô nhiễm kéo dài. *(Gợi ý: deteriorate, persistent)*
