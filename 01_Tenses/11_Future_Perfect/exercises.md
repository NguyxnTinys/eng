## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn & Bị động cơ bản.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở **Thì Tương lai hoàn thành (Future Perfect)** (chú ý thể khẳng định, phủ định, nghi vấn và bị động nếu có).

1. By 6 p.m. today, the analysts _________________________ (compile) all the quarterly sales figures for the director.
2. Unfortunately, the construction crew _________________________ (not / renovate) the south wing before the autumn semester starts.
3. _________________________ (you / finalize) the travel itinerary by the time the client calls tomorrow morning?
4. How many mock contracts _________________________ (the trainees / negotiate) by the end of this intensive workshop?
5. By next Friday, all updated safety protocols _________________________ (implement) across every regional warehouse.
6. The municipal council _________________________ (not / allocate) the emergency funds before the fiscal review concludes.
7. Who _________________________ (submit) the final manuscript to the publisher by midnight?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng, Stative Verbs & Thể Bị động
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Các cách dùng chi tiết, Động từ trạng thái & Bị động B2–C1.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc vào chỗ trống sao cho phù hợp về nghĩa và cấu trúc (sử dụng Future Perfect chủ động/bị động, hoặc Present Simple/Present Perfect cho mệnh đề phụ thuộc nếu cần).

8. By the time the new aviation hub _________________________ (commence) operations next spring, the airline _________________________ (undergo) a complete brand overhaul.
9. Don't bother knocking on Professor Tran's office; it's 4:15 p.m., so she _________________________ (depart) for the airport by now.
10. When Mr. Binh retires this December, he _________________________ (own) the family logistics firm for thirty-five years.
11. By 2035, clean solar power _________________________ (compensate) for nearly half of the country's industrial electricity demand.
12. _________________________ (the revised curriculum / approve) by the Ministry of Education before the academic year opens?
13. Look at how exhausted the engineers look—they _________________________ (resolve) the core server bug by now, judging by the green status lights.
14. By the end of this five-year scholarship, Nadia _________________________ (accumulate) more than 2,000 hours of clinical research experience.
15. If our sales team maintains this momentum, they _________________________ (exceed) the annual production quota well before November.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Phân biệt Future Perfect với Future Simple, Future Continuous, Future Perfect Continuous & `by` vs. `until`.*

Chọn thì thích hợp nhất cho động từ trong ngoặc (`Future Simple`, `Future Continuous`, `Future Perfect`, hoặc `Future Perfect Continuous`).

16. Don't call me between 7 and 8 p.m. tonight; I _________________________ (negotiate) a sensitive licensing deal with our Tokyo partners.
17. Call me after 8:30 p.m. instead; by then, I _________________________ (finish) the video call and can talk freely.
18. By the time the rescue boat arrives at dawn, the stranded hikers _________________________ (wait) on the ridge for nearly nine hours.
19. We must pace our spending carefully so that we _________________________ (not / exhaust) our project budget before Phase 2 even begins.
20. The archival team _________________________ (stay) in the vault until 9 p.m. tonight to catalog the remaining manuscripts.
21. By next Tuesday, Dr. Hoa _________________________ (know) her research mentor for exactly a decade.
22. I promise I _________________________ (forward) the signed agreement to your legal department as soon as I receive it.
23. By the end of this month, the IT department _________________________ (streamline) fifteen internal workflows—and they aim to finish five more in May.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 — Ngoại lệ mệnh đề thời gian, Suy đoán hiện tại & Bẫy đề thi.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).

24. By the time the external auditors _____ on Monday, the accounting staff _____ every single discrepancy.
    - A. will arrive / will have resolved
    - B. arrive / will have resolved
    - C. have arrived / resolve
    - D. arrived / will resolve

25. It's already 11:45 p.m. and the theater lights are off; the closing performance _____ by now.
    - A. will finish
    - B. is finishing
    - C. will have finished
    - D. will be finishing

26. By 2029, the old railway station _____ into a zero-emission cultural center for local residents.
    - A. will have converted
    - B. will have been converting
    - C. will convert
    - D. will have been converted

27. The contractors won't have cleared the construction debris _____ Friday afternoon, so please postpone the inspection.
    - A. until
    - B. for
    - C. since
    - D. during

28. By the end of the quarter, our logistics unit _____ over 40,000 parcels without a single damaged shipment.
    - A. will have been delivering
    - B. will have delivered
    - C. delivers
    - D. will be delivered

29. _____ the board of directors _____ the merger terms before the press conference starts?
    - A. Will / have finalized
    - B. Will / be finalized
    - C. Have / finalized
    - D. Will / finalize

30. You shouldn't expect a reply from Elena today; she _____ for Frankfurt two hours ago and her flight _____ by now.
    - A. left / will depart
    - B. has left / departs
    - C. left / will have departed
    - D. will have left / departed

31. By the time the new policy takes effect, employees _____ through three mandatory compliance workshops.
    - A. will have been gone
    - B. will go
