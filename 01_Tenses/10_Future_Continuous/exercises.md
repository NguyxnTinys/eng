# Bài tập Thực hành — Thì Tương lai tiếp diễn (Future Continuous) (B1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn làm bài:**
> - Tổng cộng **50 câu hỏi** chia thành **7 dạng bài** từ cơ bản đến nâng cao.
> - Trước mỗi phần có mục *🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết* giúp bạn đối chiếu nhanh với `theory.md` trước khi làm.
> - Đáp án và giải thích chi tiết nằm ở file `answers.md`.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (`S + will + be + V-ing`, phủ định `won't be + V-ing`, nghi vấn `Will + S + be + V-ing?` và quy tắc chính tả thêm `-ing`).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Tương lai tiếp diễn (Future Continuous):**

1. At 9 a.m. tomorrow, our CEO __________ (facilitate) a strategic meeting at the headquarters.
2. This time next week, I __________ (not / commute) to the city center because I have taken annual leave.
3. __________ you __________ (use) the conference room between 2 p.m. and 4 p.m. this Friday?
4. At 11 o'clock tonight, the flight to Tokyo __________ (take) off as scheduled.
5. Where __________ the delegation __________ (stay) during the three-day summit in Geneva?
6. Don't call him at noon; he __________ (board) the express train to Manchester then.
7. She __________ (not / attend) the seminar tomorrow afternoon since she has an urgent appointment.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & 4 — Các cách dùng cốt lõi (mốc tương lai, lịch trình tự nhiên, hỏi lịch sự, dự đoán hiện tại) và Ngoại lệ động từ trạng thái (Stative verbs).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Tương lai tiếp diễn (`will be + V-ing`) hoặc Tương lai đơn (`will + V`) sao cho đúng ngữ pháp và ngữ nghĩa:**

8. At this time next month, the new software __________ (belong) to the entire engineering department.
9. Excuse me, __________ you __________ (pass) the reception desk on your way out? Could you drop off these keys?
10. From 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. tomorrow, our technical staff __________ (supervise) the installation simultaneously across three floors.
11. Please note that the central database __________ (undergo) routine maintenance this Saturday night.
12. By next summer, Sarah __________ (know) how to negotiate international contracts fluently.
13. Look at the clock — it's 7:45 a.m., so David __________ (commute) to work right now.
14. According to the tentative itinerary, we __________ (rely) on local guides throughout the mountain expedition.
15. At midnight tonight, the security team __________ (have) a brief meeting to review the new protocol.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & 4 — Phân biệt `will + V` (quyết định tức thời / sự kiện đơn lẻ), `be going to + V` (dự định sẵn), `will be + V-ing` (đang diễn ra / lịch trình tự nhiên / hỏi lịch sự) và `will have + V3/ed` (hoàn tất trước mốc).*

**Chọn dạng động từ thích hợp nhất trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu:**

16. "I've left my charger at home." — "Don't worry, I **(will lend / will be lending)** you mine right now."
17. At 10 a.m. tomorrow, we **(will implement / will be implementing)** the updated policy, so expect some system delays.
18. **(Will you use / Will you be using)** your company car tonight? I need to pick up a client from the airport.
19. By the end of this quarter, the company **(will accommodate / will have accommodated)** over 500 new remote employees.
20. When the keynote speaker **(arrives / will arrive)** at the venue tomorrow, journalists will be waiting outside the main hall.
21. The annual conference **(starts / will be starting)** at exactly 8:30 a.m. tomorrow morning — don't be late!
22. During the upcoming transition, employees **(will be adapting / will adapt)** to the hybrid model for several months.
23. Look at those dark clouds! It **(will be raining / is going to rain)** before we reach the headquarters.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Bẫy đề thi B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Bẫy đề thi (mệnh đề thời gian không dùng `will`, phân biệt sắc thái câu hỏi, động từ trạng thái, giới từ `at` vs `by`).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):**

24. When the inspectors ______ the warehouse next Tuesday, our staff ______ the new safety measures.
    A. will visit / will be demonstrating
    B. visit / will be demonstrating
    C. visit / demonstrate
    D. are visiting / will demonstrate

25. ______ your tablet later this evening? If not, could I borrow it to review the tentative itinerary?
    A. Do you use
    B. Will you use
    C. Will you be using
    D. Are you used to using

26. At this time tomorrow, the board members ______ the revised budget, so they won't be available for phone calls.
    A. will negotiate
    B. will be negotiating
    C. will have negotiated
    D. are negotiated

27. Please apologize for any inconvenience in advance, as the elevator ______ routine maintenance all morning tomorrow.
    A. will be undergoing
    B. will undergo
    C. undergoes
    D. will have undergone

28. At 6 p.m. next Friday, I ______ the company for ten years, yet I ______ still ______ my daily commute by train.
    A. will be working / will / enjoy
    B. will have worked / will / be enjoying
    C. will work / am / enjoying
    D. will be working / will / be enjoying

29. You shouldn't bother calling Emma at 9 a.m. — she ______ her weekly team briefing right now.
    A. will be leading
    B. leads
    C. is going to lead
    D. will have led

30. While the technicians ______ the main server tomorrow afternoon, the backup system ______ uninterrupted service.
    A. will upgrade / will be ensuring
    B. upgrade / will be ensuring
    C. are upgrading / will ensure
    D. will be upgrading / ensure

31. By the time the new policy takes effect, most departments ______ smooth operations, though some teams ______ still ______ their workflows.
    A. will be establishing / will / adjust
    B. will have established / will / be adjusting
    C. establish / will / be adjusting
    D. will establish / are / adjusting

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Các lỗi sai kinh điển của người học Việt Nam (quên `be`, sai dạng `V-ing`, dùng `will` trong mệnh đề `when/while`, chia tiếp diễn cho Stative verbs).*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây có MỘT lỗi sai ở các phần được đánh dấu (A, B, C, D). Hãy xác định lỗi sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng:**

32. At 8 a.m. tomorrow, our colleagues **(A) will** **(B) attending** an international conference **(C) to discuss** **(D) inevitable** market changes.
33. When the director **(A) will return** from his business trip next week, the team **(B) will be** **(C) implementing** the **(D) proposed** strategy.
34. At this time next year, I **(A) will be** **(B) owning** a small consultancy firm that **(C) helps** startups **(D) negotiate** contracts.
35. **(A) Will** you **(B) be** **(C) travel** to the headquarters tomorrow, or **(D) will** you be working remotely from home?
36. From 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. tomorrow, we **(A) will be** **(B) accomodating** VIP guests while the hosts **(C) are preparing** the **(D) main** hall.
37. At 9 p.m. tonight, the flight attendants **(A) will be** **(B) serving** dinner while passengers **(C) will be watching** movies **(D) on board**.
38. Please don't **(A) panic** — when the system **(B) updates** tonight, our engineers **(C) will be** **(D) supervizing** every stage carefully.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp công thức & sắc thái lịch sự / lịch trình tự nhiên của Future Continuous.*

**Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng gợi ý trong ngoặc:**

39. I want to borrow your microphone this afternoon. First, let me politely check your plan.
    → ______________________________ this afternoon? *(dùng `Will you be...`)*
40. Our plan is to conduct interviews from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. tomorrow morning.
    → At 10 a.m. tomorrow morning, we ______________________________. *(dùng `be conducting`)*
41. It's part of my daily routine to commute past the library, so I can return your book tomorrow.
    → I ______________________________ tomorrow, so I can return your book. *(dùng `be commuting`)*
42. It is inevitable that companies will adopt AI tools continuously over the coming decade.
    → Over the coming decade, companies ______________________________ AI tools. *(dùng `inevitably` + Tương lai tiếp diễn)*
43. Don't phone me at 4 p.m. because I have a scheduled meeting with a colleague at that time.
    → Don't phone me at 4 p.m. because I ______________________________. *(dùng `be meeting`)*
44. The aircraft is scheduled to enter its routine maintenance phase throughout next Monday.
    → Throughout next Monday, the aircraft ______________________________. *(dùng `undergo`)*
