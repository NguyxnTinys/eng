## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức tổng quát & Quy tắc chính tả khi thêm `-ing` (bỏ `-e` câm, `-ie → -y`, gấp đôi phụ âm cuối theo trọng âm, thêm `-k` sau `-c`).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Hiện tại tiếp diễn (Present Continuous).**

1. Quiet, please! The CEO ______________ (demonstrate) the new prototype to the investors right now.
2. Why ______________ (you / lie) on the sofa at 10 a.m.? Aren't you supposed to be at the briefing?
3. Our legal team ______________ (not / negotiate) with that vendor at the moment because the contract expired.
4. Look at the children! They ______________ (picnic) by the lake despite the chilly wind.
5. ______________ (the new intern / begin) her orientation session in Room 302 right now?
6. Watch out! That old wooden shelf ______________ (fall) off the wall — step back!
7. Who ______________ (travel) with the marketing director to the conference in Busan today?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi / Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 5 Cách dùng cốt lõi (đang diễn ra, tạm thời, xu hướng biến đổi, phàn nàn với `always/constantly`, kế hoạch tương lai gần đã chốt) & cấu trúc đặc biệt `be being + adj`.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Hiện tại đơn hoặc Hiện tại tiếp diễn sao cho phù hợp nhất với sắc thái câu.**

8. Normally Dr. Minh teaches in Hanoi, but this semester he ______________ (lecture) as a visiting professor in Da Nang.
9. Thanks to the new filter policy, air quality in the industrial zone ______________ (gradually / improve) month by month.
10. My neighbor ______________ (constantly / drill) holes in the wall at 6 a.m. — it is genuinely irritating!
11. Everything is confirmed: we ______________ (relocate) our regional warehouse to Binh Duong next Monday morning.
12. You ______________ (be) unusually selfish today, Tom! You normally share the lab equipment with everyone.
13. Prices of imported raw materials ______________ (fluctuate) wildly these days due to shipping delays.
14. According to the official timetable, the express train to Hue ______________ (depart) at 7:15 a.m. tomorrow.
15. I ______________ (stay) at a temporary guesthouse near the station until the contractor finishes renovating my apartment.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh Stative Verbs & Động từ đổi nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Stative Verbs không chia tiếp diễn & Bảng Động từ lưỡng tính (think, have, see, taste, smell, feel, look, weigh, appear).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Hiện tại đơn hoặc Hiện tại tiếp diễn dựa vào nghĩa trạng thái (Stative) hay hành động (Dynamic).**

16. This new software package ______________ (consist) of four core modules and a cloud backup tool.
17. Wait a second — the chef ______________ (taste) the mushroom soup right now to see if it needs more pepper.
18. I ______________ (think) that expanding into rural markets without proper logistics is too risky.
19. We ______________ (think) of hiring two part-time translators for the upcoming summit next month.
20. Don't disturb Ms. Linh right now; she ______________ (have) an urgent video call with our Tokyo partners.
21. Although this applicant ______________ (possess) impressive coding certificates, he lacks team leadership experience.
22. How much ______________ (this industrial printer / weigh)? We need to check if the elevator can carry it.
23. Look! Why ______________ (the customs officer / smell) my coffee beans? — Wait, he ______________ (appear) to be checking for hidden items!

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi (bẫy `currently` vs `actually`, `now` + stative, `always` phàn nàn, `be being + adj`, vị trí trạng từ).*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).**

24. "Have you finalized your summer internship yet?" — "Not yet, I ______ several offers from tech startups at the moment."
    A. consider
    B. am considering
    C. am considered
    D. considers

25. Public perception of electric buses ______ steadily as charging stations become more accessible across the city.
    A. shifts
    B. is shifting
    C. is shifted
    D. shift

26. Sorry, I can't join you for lunch today. I ______ the dentist at 12:30 p.m. — I booked the slot last week.
    A. see
    B. am seeing
    C. saw
    D. have seen

27. Why ______ to your team members like that? You are usually so calm and supportive.
    A. are you being so rude
    B. are you so rude
    C. do you be so rude
    D. were you being so rude

28. That herbal tea ______ slightly bitter at first, doesn't it?
    A. is tasting
    B. taste
    C. tastes
    D. has tasted

29. My younger brother ______ his headphones on the subway; this is the third pair he's lost this year!
    A. always leaves
    B. is always leaving
    C. always is leaving
    D. is leaving always

30. Our engineering department ______ two senior architects because the current workload ______ everyone.
    A. is currently recruiting / is overwhelming
    B. currently recruits / overwhelms
    C. is currently recruiting / overwhelms
    D. recruits currently / is overwhelming

31. Although I ______ a hectic time with the new project this week, I ______ what you mean about prioritizing quality over speed.
    A. am having / am seeing
    B. have / see
    C. am having / see
    D. have / am seeing

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Các lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay gặp (thiếu `be`, chia tiếp diễn với stative verb, sai quy tắc chính tả `-ing`, dùng sai `be being` với tính từ cảm xúc thụ động).*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây có MỘT lỗi sai ở phần được gạch chân (A, B, C hoặc D). Hãy xác định chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng.**

32. Because the main bridge (A) **is under repair**, commuters (B) **are currently** (C) **rely on** ferry boats to cross the river (D) **this month**.
33. Look at the lab results: the patient's condition (A) **deteriorates** rapidly right now, so the doctors (B) **are preparing** (C) **for** an emergency (D) **operation**.
34. Why (A) **is** your little sister (B) **being** so (C) **hungry** right now? Didn't she (D) **have** breakfast an hour ago?
35. The quality inspector (A) **weighs** the incoming shipment (B) **at the loading dock** while the manager (C) **is checking** the (D) **invoices**.
36. I (A) **am belonging** to a local cycling club, and we (B) **are organizing** a charity ride (C) **across** the peninsula (D) **this Sunday**.
37. Look at the warehouse worker: he (A) **is tieing** the heavy crates together (B) **while** his colleague (C) **is loading** them onto the truck (D) **carefully**.
38. Nowadays, more young graduates (A) **are prefering** flexible remote roles (B) **over** rigid office hours, even if the salary (C) **seems** slightly (D) **lower**.
