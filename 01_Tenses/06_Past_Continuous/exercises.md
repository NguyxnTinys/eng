# Bài tập thực hành: Thì Quá khứ tiếp diễn (Past Continuous) (A2–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài). Đừng xem `answers.md` trước khi tự làm xong để đánh giá chính xác mức độ nắm vững kiến thức của bạn.

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức Khẳng định (`was/were + V-ing`), Phủ định (`wasn't/weren't + V-ing`), Nghi vấn Yes/No và Wh-.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở **thì Quá khứ tiếp diễn (Past Continuous)**:

1. At 8 p.m. last night, Maya __________ (prepare) her slides for an urgent deadline.
2. We __________ (not / commute) by train at that hour because the line was under maintenance.
3. __________ (they / negotiate) the final terms with the supplier when you arrived?
4. Everyone in the office __________ (work) quietly under a calm atmosphere at 10 a.m. yesterday.
5. Why __________ (he / hesitate) to sign the temporary agreement during the meeting?
6. The two departments __________ (not / collaborate) well at that stage of the project.
7. Who __________ (make) all that loud noise outside the library at midnight?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng chi tiết, Quy tắc chính tả & Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.2 & Mục 3 & Mục 4.2 — Quy tắc thêm `-ing`, các cách dùng cốt lõi và ngoại lệ Stative Verbs.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Past Continuous** hoặc **Past Simple** (nếu gặp động từ trạng thái/ngoại lệ hoặc ngữ cảnh bắt buộc):

8. While the children __________ (lie) on the grass and watching the clouds, a breeze gently cooled the park.
9. At 7:30 a.m. yesterday, the workers __________ (begin) to clear the debris after the storm.
10. When I walked into the room, I __________ (know) immediately that something had gone wrong.
11. My former colleague __________ (constantly / make) an excuse whenever we asked him to stay late.
12. At this time last month, our team __________ (plan) a temporary schedule for the summer camp.
13. I __________ (wonder) if you could spare five minutes to review my proposal right now.
14. During the recession, the company __________ (gradually / lose) its market share before new investors stepped in.
15. Back in 2020, my uncle __________ (own) two small cafés in the centre of Da Nang.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.2, 3.3 & Mục 4.1 — Hành động xen vào (`when`/`while`), hành động song song, hành động nối tiếp và cảnh báo dấu hiệu.*

Chia các động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Past Continuous** hoặc **Past Simple** sao cho đúng ngữ cảnh:

16. We __________ (discuss) the budget when a sudden power outage __________ (hit) the entire floor.
17. While I __________ (wait) for the bus, I accidentally __________ (overhear) two colleagues talking about the incident.
18. Yesterday afternoon, my brother __________ (revise) for his biology test while I __________ (draft) a report.
19. When the fire alarm __________ (ring), all the employees immediately __________ (leave) the building through the stairs.
20. The detective __________ (investigate) a suspicious clue in the hallway when the lights __________ (go) out.
21. While the technician __________ (fix) the network simultaneously, the receptionist __________ (update) the clients by phone.
22. Yesterday evening, I __________ (watch) a documentary about wildlife from 8 p.m. to 9 p.m., and then I went to bed.
23. She __________ (be) in the middle of explaining the background of the project when a visitor __________ (interrupt) her.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Bẫy đề thi (`during` vs `while`, chủ ngữ phức, Stative Verbs, bối cảnh truyện, lời nói lịch sự).*

Chọn **một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D)**:

24. __________ I was commuting to the city centre, I witnessed a minor traffic incident near the bridge.
    - A. During
    - B. While
    - C. For
    - D. Meanwhile

25. The project leader, along with two junior engineers, __________ the system failure at 11 p.m. last night.
    - A. were investigating
    - B. investigated
    - C. was investigating
    - D. are investigating

26. He read the novel so intently last night that he completely __________ track of time and missed his last train.
    - A. was losing
    - B. lost
    - C. loses
    - D. had been losing

27. "Good morning, Professor. I __________ if I could submit my essay tomorrow morning instead of today."
    - A. wonder
    - B. was wondering
    - C. have wondered
    - D. am wondered

28. Loud construction noise outside __________ me while I was trying to concentrate on the financial figures.
    - A. was distracting
    - B. distracts
    - C. distracted
    - D. has distracted

29. At that moment, nobody __________ why the old warehouse had suddenly collapsed.
    - A. was understanding
    - B. were understanding
    - C. understood
    - D. had been understanding

30. Rain __________ down heavily and a cold wind __________ through the valley when the hikers finally spotted a shelter.
    - A. poured / blew
    - B. was pouring / was blowing
    - C. poured / was blowing
    - D. was pouring / blew

31. Neither the managers nor the assistant __________ to the speaker when the microphone stopped working.
    - A. was listening
    - B. were listening
    - C. listened
    - D. are listening

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
