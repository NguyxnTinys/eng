## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức cơ bản (`to be`: was/were & động từ thường: V2/V-ed, did/didn't + V-inf).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple)**.

1. The board of directors _______________ (be) very satisfied with the quarterly report yesterday.
2. Why _______________ (be) the international flights _______________ (delay) at the airport last night? *(Gợi ý: câu bị động với to be)*
3. Our team _______________ (not / collaborate) with external agencies on that project in 2023.
4. _______________ (you / announce) the new recruitment policy during the meeting this morning?
5. There _______________ (be) several unexpected technical issues before we launched the website.
6. She _______________ (not / hesitate) to sign the agreement once she read the final draft.
7. Who _______________ (resolve) the customer complaint while the supervisor was away?

## Part 2: Quy tắc biến đổi chính tả, Bất quy tắc & Phát âm `-ed` (Câu 8–15)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Quy tắc gấp đôi phụ âm, đổi `-y`, động từ bất quy tắc & Phát âm đuôi `-ed` (`/t/`, `/d/`, `/ɪd/`).*

**Câu 8–11:** Chia dạng quá khứ đơn đúng của động từ trong ngoặc (chú ý chính tả và bất quy tắc).

8. Under difficult financial circumstances, the startup _______________ (overcome) fierce competition and survived its first year.
9. The candidate openly _______________ (admit) that he had very little experience, and he later _______________ (regret) not preparing better.
10. Over the past decade, the healthcare sector _______________ (undergo) a major transformation in 2019 before the pandemic hit.
11. Because heavy rain _______________ (occur) throughout the morning, the organizers _______________ (postpone) the outdoor concert until Sunday.

**Câu 12–15:** Chọn từ (A, B, C hoặc D) có phần đuôi **`-ed`** phát âm **KHÁC** với các từ còn lại.

13. A. `hesitated` &nbsp;&nbsp; B. `implemented` &nbsp;&nbsp; C. `established` &nbsp;&nbsp; D. `negotiated`
14. A. `delayed` &nbsp;&nbsp; B. `occurred` &nbsp;&nbsp; C. `postponed` &nbsp;&nbsp; D. `committed`
15. A. `demonstrated` &nbsp;&nbsp; B. `collaborated` &nbsp;&nbsp; C. `admitted` &nbsp;&nbsp; D. `studied`

## Part 3: Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (hành động chấm dứt hẳn vs. còn tiếp diễn/xen vào).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì thích hợp (**Past Simple**, **Past Continuous**, hoặc **Present Perfect**) dựa vào ngữ cảnh.

16. My sister _______________ (live) in Berlin for three years from 2018 to 2021, and now she lives in Munich.
17. We _______________ (collaborate) on this research paper since we _______________ (meet) at the conference last autumn.
18. While the engineers _______________ (demonstrate) the new software, the main server suddenly _______________ (crash).
19. I _______________ (not / see) David this morning—maybe he is working from home today. *(Nói vào lúc 10:00 sáng)*
20. Yesterday evening, I _______________ (lock) the office door, _______________ (walk) to the station, and _______________ (catch) the last train home.
21. Up to now, the company _______________ (launch) three successful products, whereas last year it _______________ (launch) only one.
22. When I was a teenager, I often _______________ (spend) weekends reading science magazines in the local library.
23. Look! Someone _______________ (leave) the laboratory lights on, even though the last researcher _______________ (leave) two hours ago.

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D — Gài bẫy B1–C1 (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi (`found/founded`, `It's time`, `occur`, `did + V2`, câu giả định).*

Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (**A, B, C** hoặc **D**).

24. This non-profit organization _______ in 1998 to support underprivileged children in rural areas.
    A. found &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. founded &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. was found &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. was founded

25. It is high time the city council _______ stricter regulations on industrial waste before the consequences worsen.
    A. implement &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. implemented &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. has implemented &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. will implement

26. How many errors _______ during the final inspection last Friday?
    A. did occur &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. occurred &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. were occurred &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. had been occurred

27. The marketing director didn't approve the campaign because the proposal _______ enough data to support its budget.
    A. hadn't &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. wasn't have &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. didn't have &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. didn't had

28. If the negotiators _______ a compromise today, the union wouldn't have to call a strike tomorrow.
    A. reach &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. reached &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. would reach &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. have reached

29. Why _______ so reluctant to negotiate with the supplier during yesterday's call?
    A. did you be &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. were you &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. did you were &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. are you

30. The sudden market crash was an _______ consequence after the bank _______ to regulate risky loans.
    A. inevitable / failed &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. inevitably / fails &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. inevitable / was failed &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. avoiding / failed

31. Neither of the two interns _______ aware of the crucial deadline until the manager sent a reminder.
    A. were &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; B. are &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; C. was &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; D. did

## Part 5: Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction) (Câu 32–38)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay gặp.*

Mỗi câu sau có **một lỗi sai** ở một trong bốn phần được đánh dấu **(A), (B), (C), (D)**. Hãy **chọn chữ cái chứa lỗi sai** và **viết lại phần sửa đúng**.

32. **Did** (A) the manager **approved** (B) the new policy immediately **after** (C) the committee **reviewed** (D) it?
33. The severe accident **was occurred** (A) **at** (B) the crossroads **because** (C) the traffic lights **stopped** (D) working.
34. Although the team **faced** (A) severe **consequences** (B), they **never** (C) **regreted** (D) their honest decision.
35. When I **first** (A) **read** (B) the contract, it **costed** (C) our department **far** (D) more than expected.
36. Yesterday evening, the airport **was** (A) crowded **because** (B) a snowstorm **delay** (C) almost every **departure** (D).
37. **Did** (A) your colleagues **nervous** (B) when they **underwent** (C) the annual performance **review** (D)?
38. I wish our team **have** (A) more time right now to **resolve** (B) the technical **glitches** (C) before the **launch** (D).
