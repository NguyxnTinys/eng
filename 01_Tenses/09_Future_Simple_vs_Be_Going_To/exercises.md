# Bài tập thực hành: Thì Tương lai đơn (Will) & Tương lai gần (Be going to)

**Hướng dẫn chung:** Hoàn thành đầy đủ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 phần). Không nhìn đáp án trước khi làm xong. Chú ý đọc kỹ ngữ cảnh để phân biệt quyết định tức thời, kế hoạch có sẵn, dự đoán có/không có bằng chứng, lịch trình cố định và bẫy mệnh đề thời gian.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 (Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết của `will` và `be going to`).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc theo thì gợi ý (`will` hoặc `be going to`) để hoàn thành đúng cấu trúc câu.

1. Our HR department (`be going to` / relocate) _________________________ to the new office tower next month.
2. Don't worry; I (`will` / not / hesitate) _________________________ to ask for help if the workload gets too heavy.
3. (`will` / they / implement) _________________________ the new safety regulations before the end of this quarter?
4. We (`be going to` / not / invest) _________________________ any more money in that outdated software.
5. When (`will` / the manager / confirm) _________________________ the final deadline for the design team?
6. Look at the dark grey clouds! (`be going to` / it / rain) _________________________ heavily in a few minutes?
7. I promise I (`will` / reassure) _________________________ the clients about the delivery time right after lunch.

---

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 (Cách dùng chi tiết của `will`, `be going to`, `Shall I/we` và quy tắc mệnh đề thời gian).*

Hoàn thành câu bằng cách sử dụng `will`, `won't`, `shall` hoặc `be going to` (hoặc Hiện tại đơn nếu rơi vào ngoại lệ mệnh đề thời gian/điều kiện).

8. **A:** "My laptop just froze and I haven't saved the itinerary!" — **B:** "Stay calm, I _________________________ (help) you recover the file right away."
9. Look at that old wooden bridge shaking under the truck—it _________________________ (collapse) any second now!
10. Stop interrupting the speaker, or I _________________________ (ask) you to leave the conference room!
11. _________________________ I _________________________ (send) you an email confirmation once the payment goes through?
12. I have already bought paint and brushes because I _________________________ (redecorate) my study room this Saturday.
13. Experts believe that remote work _________________________ (probably / remain) popular for decades to come.
14. As soon as the director _________________________ (arrive) tomorrow morning, we will negotiate the contract terms.
15. Do not worry about your secret—I _________________________ (not / mention) your intention to quit to anyone.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & 4 cách diễn đạt Tương lai
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3C & 4A (So sánh `Will` vs `Be going to` vs `Present Continuous` vs `Present Simple` & Dấu hiệu nhận biết).*

Chọn dạng đúng nhất của động từ trong ngoặc (`will + V0`, `be going to + V0`, `Present Continuous`, hoặc `Present Simple`) dựa trên ngữ cảnh cụ thể.

16. According to the official timetable, the express train to Hue _________________________ (depart) at 6:30 a.m. sharp tomorrow.
17. **A:** "Why did you take Friday off?" — **B:** "I _________________________ (meet) Dr. Minh at 10 a.m. I booked the appointment two weeks ago."
18. **A:** "We don't have enough chairs for the guests." — **B:** "Really? I _________________________ (bring) some extra ones from the lobby now."
19. With no rainfall recorded in the past two months, a severe water shortage _________________________ (occur) across the province.
20. Someday, Mai _________________________ (learn) French, though she hasn't signed up for any course yet.
21. Let's hurry up! The final exam _________________________ (start) at 8:00 a.m., and the traffic looks terrible.
22. I'm not sure who will win the election, but I think the younger candidate _________________________ (get) more support from students.
23. Look at the latest weather forecast: temperatures _________________________ (drop) to 8°C tonight, so bring a thick coat!

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4B (Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi).*

Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).

24. Look at how exhausted the marathon runner looks in the last 200 meters; I think he _______ before reaching the finish line.
    - A. will faint
    - B. is going to faint
    - C. faints
    - D. shall faint
25. We won't sign the partnership agreement until the supplier _______ written confirmation of the quality standards.
    - A. will provide
    - B. is going to provide
    - C. provides
    - D. provided
26. If unforeseen technical problems arise during the launch, the IT team _______ a backup plan immediately.
    - A. is implementing
    - B. implements
    - C. will implement
    - D. was going to implement
27. **A:** "Have you decided where to spend your bonus?" — **B:** "Yes, I _______ it in a sustainable energy fund."
    - A. will invest
    - B. am going to invest
    - C. invest
    - D. invested
28. Despite the manager's commitment to finish early, the heavy workload means the team _______ meet the deadline tonight.
    - A. won't be able to
    - B. won't can
    - C. aren't going to can
    - D. don't able to
29. _______ we postpone the weekly briefing until Thursday so everyone has time to read the report?
    - A. Will
    - B. Are
    - C. Shall
    - D. Do
30. Because of rising production costs, price increases across the retail sector will become _______ next year.
    - A. inevitable
    - B. be inevitable
    - C. going to be inevitable
    - D. inevitably
31. **A:** "I forgot to pack my charger for the business trip!" — **B:** "No problem, I _______ you my spare one."
    - A. am lending
    - B. am going to lend
    - C. lend
    - D. will lend

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4B (Lỗi sai kinh điển & Bẫy ngữ pháp).*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** nằm ở một trong bốn phần gạch chân `(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)`, `(D)`. Hãy xác định chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng.

32. As soon as she **(A) will receive** the email **(B) confirmation**, she **(C) will book** the hotel rooms **(D) for** the guests.
33. Look at the **(A) crowded** schedule on his desk; he **(B) will definitely** **(C) missing** his flight **(D) this evening**.
34. My parents **(A) going to** **(B) relocate** to a quieter coastal town **(C) after** they **(D) retire** next spring.
35. Don't worry about **(A) the** missing files—I **(B) am going to** search the archive **(C) right now** and send them **(D) to** you.
36. Our company **(A) will to** **(B) implement** a greener policy **(C) in order to** build a **(D) sustainable** workplace.
37. Wait a minute! Those boxes **(A) are stacked** too high; they **(B) will fall** onto the floor **(C) in** just a **(D) few** seconds!
38. The international flight from London **(A) will arrives** at Terminal 2 **(B) at** 7:45 a.m. tomorrow **(C) according to** the airport **(D) schedule**.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc diễn đạt tương lai (ý định, sắp xếp, lời hứa, dự báo).*

Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ/cụm từ gợi ý trong ngoặc.

39. David intends to negotiate a flexible working schedule with his boss next Monday. *(use `going`)*
    $\rightarrow$ David _________________________________________________________________________.
40. We have arranged to meet the overseas investors at the airport at 4 p.m. tomorrow. *(use `meeting`)*
    $\rightarrow$ We ___________________________________________________________________________.
41. Nam promises not to postpone the project again. *(use `won't`)*
    $\rightarrow$ Nam promises that he ________________________________________________________.
42. Look at the huge traffic jam ahead; it is obvious that we are going to be late for the seminar. *(use `obviously` / `going`)*
    $\rightarrow$ Look at the huge traffic jam ahead; we ____________________________________________.
43. The university library's official schedule is to close at 9:30 p.m. on weekdays. *(use `closes`)*
    $\rightarrow$ The university library ___________________________________________________________.
44. Would you like me to carry those heavy boxes to the storage room for you? *(use `Shall`)*
    $\rightarrow$ ______________________________________________________________________________?
