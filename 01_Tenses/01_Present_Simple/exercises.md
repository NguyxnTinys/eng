## Part 1 (Câu 1–45): Nhận diện & Chia/Điền dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)

### A. Động từ `to be` (`am / is / are` — Khẳng định, Phủ định & Nghi vấn) (Câu 1–12)
*Hướng dẫn: Chia động từ `to be` trong ngoặc ở Thì Hiện tại đơn sao cho phù hợp với chủ ngữ và thể câu (khẳng định, phủ định hoặc nghi vấn).*

1. She _______ (be) always **punctual** for morning client briefings.
2. I _______ (not / be) familiar with the new training **curriculum** yet.
3. _______ (be) these updated **itineraries** ready for the overseas delegates?
4. The system _______ (not / be) stable enough to handle peak traffic today.
5. Why _______ (be) the regional train late almost every Monday?
6. Everyone in our department _______ (be) responsible for protecting client data.
7. _______ (be) you satisfied with your current **productivity** level at work?
8. These quarterly figures _______ (not / be) **consistent** with the financial report.
9. The number of employees working remotely **on a regular basis** _______ (be) still quite small.
10. A number of experienced engineers _______ (be) involved in this calibration project.
11. Where _______ (be) the safety guidelines for the laboratory equipment?
12. Clean drinking water _______ (be) essential for every living organism on Earth.

### C. Động từ thường ở Thể Phủ định & Nghi vấn (`don't / doesn't`, `Do / Does`, Wh-) (Câu 27–38)

31. _______ (this package / **consist**) of five online modules as stated in the brochure?

35. Why _______ (exchange rates / **fluctuate**) so sharply before major policy announcements?

37. Most travelers _______ (not / **take**) local customs **for granted** when they visit historic towns.
38. How often _______ (the engineering team / inspect) the backup generators?
