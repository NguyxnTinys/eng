# Bài tập thực hành — Thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (Present Perfect) (A2–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn làm bài**: Hoàn thành đầy đủ 50 câu hỏi dưới đây (từ Part 1 đến Part 7). Chú ý ngữ cảnh, mốc thời gian và các cấu trúc bẫy trước khi kiểm tra đáp án tại `answers.md`.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (chia `have/has + V3/ed` và động từ bất quy tắc).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (Present Perfect).**

1. Our engineering team _______________ (accomplish) three major milestones since the start of this year.
2. She _______________ (not / resolve) the customer complaint yet because she is waiting for the manager's reply.
3. How long _______________ you _______________ (know) the new director of marketing?
4. We _______________ (already / implement) the updated security policy across all regional offices.
5. _______________ the board of directors _______________ (decide) to relocate the headquarters yet?
6. Over the past five years, Mr. Hoang _______________ (overcome) countless financial difficulties to keep his business running.
7. My colleagues _______________ (never / hesitate) to share their knowledge whenever I need help.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng cốt lõi
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (`just / already / yet`, `for / since`, `ever / never`, `gone vs been`, `It's the first time...`).*

**Điền vào chỗ trống từ thích hợp trong hộp (`just`, `already`, `yet`, `for`, `since`, `ever`, `never`, `gone`, `been`) hoặc chia động từ đúng vào chỗ trống.**

8. Don't call the technician—I have _______________ fixed the printer myself, and it's working fine now.
9. Have you finished analyzing the quarterly report _______________? Everyone is waiting for your summary.
10. The company has accumulated more than ten thousand loyal users _______________ it launched the mobile app in 2023.
11. My parents have lived in that quiet coastal town _______________ nearly twenty-five years.
12. "Where is David? I need him to sign this document." — "He has _______________ to the bank and won't be back until 3 p.m."
13. I have _______________ to Singapore twice for business conferences, and I really admire how efficient the city is.
14. It's the first time our research group _______________ (make) a breakthrough in renewable battery technology.
15. This is the most remarkable improvement I _______________ (ever / see) in your presentation skills.

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (Present Perfect) hoặc Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple).**

18. When _______________ your company _______________ (relocate) its manufacturing plant to Binh Duong?
19. Ms. Lan _______________ (work) as a senior consultant for ten years before she _______________ (subsequently / open) her own firm in 2021.
20. Look at the office! Someone _______________ (leave) the air conditioner on all night, and the room is freezing right now.
21. A: "Guess what! Our team _______________ (just / win) the national innovation award!" — B: "Congratulations! How _______________ you _______________ (celebrate) it?"
22. I _______________ (drink) four cups of coffee today, and I still have three more meetings this afternoon.
23. The manager _______________ (postpone) the product launch just now because of an unexpected delivery delay.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A / B / C / D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

**Chọn phương án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) để hoàn thành mỗi câu sau.**

24. Since the management _______________ the flexible working policy, employee productivity _______________ significantly.
    - A. has implemented / increased
    - B. implemented / has increased
    - C. implemented / increased
    - D. implements / has increased

25. It is nearly six months since we last _______________ a contract with a European partner.
    - A. have negotiated
    - B. negotiate
    - C. negotiated
    - D. had negotiated

26. Dr. Linh isn't at her desk right now; she _______________ to the laboratory to examine the new samples.
    - A. has been
    - B. has gone
    - C. went
    - D. goes

27. Have you _______________ to Tokyo? I'm planning a trip there next month and need some hotel recommendations.
    - A. ever gone
    - B. been ever
    - C. ever been
    - D. gone ever

28. That was the first time the young entrepreneur _______________ an audience of more than two thousand investors.
    - A. has addressed
    - B. addressed
    - C. had addressed
    - D. addresses

29. Although several shortcomings remain, the new software has contributed greatly to _______________ manual data errors.
    - A. reduce
    - B. reducing
    - C. reduced
    - D. having reduce

30. The startup _______________ unprecedented growth in recent years, which has attracted several venture capital funds.
    - A. experienced
    - B. experiences
    - C. has experienced
    - D. is experiencing

31. How long _______________ this electric vehicle? You seem very familiar with all of its features.
    - A. have you bought
    - B. did you buy
    - C. have you owned
    - D. do you own
