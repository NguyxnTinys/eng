## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn) & Quy tắc thêm `-ing`.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Future Perfect Continuous.**

1. By next Friday, the engineers ________________________ (collaborate) on this clean-energy prototype for eight months.
2. At 10 p.m. tonight, I ________________________ (compile) the quarterly sales data for nearly five hours straight.
3. By the end of this term, how long ____________ Dr. Nam ________________________ (dedicate) his evenings to mentoring exchange students?
4. When the inspector arrives at noon, the technicians ________________________ (not / monitor) the backup server for very long—only about twenty minutes.
5. By 2030, the company ________________________ (expand) its logistics network across Southeast Asia for over a decade.
6. ____________ the construction crew ________________________ (renovate) the old city library for two full years by the time it reopens?
7. By sunrise tomorrow, the detectives ________________________ (investigate) the cyberattack uninterruptedly for twelve hours.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc Stative Verbs
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (1, 2, 3) & Ngoại lệ Động từ trạng thái (Stative Verbs).*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc `by... for...`, `How long...`, chuyển đổi từ mốc quá khứ/hiện tại sang mốc tương lai.*

**Viết lại các câu sau sử dụng từ gợi ý sao cho nghĩa không đổi, bắt buộc dùng thì Future Perfect Continuous (trừ trường hợp Stative Verb).**

39. Professor Minh started lecturing at this university in September 2018, and he plans to continue until at least 2028.
    → By September 2028, Professor Minh ______________________________________________________.

40. Our engineering division began testing the solar battery six months ago, and the trial ends next month.
    → By the time the trial ends next month, our engineering division ______________________________.

41. When did the city council start renovating the central bridge, given that next Friday marks two years of continuous work?
    → How long ________________________________________________________________ by next Friday?

42. Clara will get home at 9 p.m., after driving nonstop since 4 p.m., which explains why her shoulders will ache.
    → Clara's shoulders will ache when she gets home at 9 p.m. because __________________________.

43. We bought this eco-friendly warehouse in 2020, and we intend to keep it well beyond 2030.
    → By 2030, we __________________________________________________________________________.

44. The bilateral trade talks started on Monday morning and are still going on as Friday evening approaches.
    → By Friday evening, the diplomats ________________________________________________________.
