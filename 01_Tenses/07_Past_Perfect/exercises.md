# Bài tập thực hành Unit 07: Thì Quá khứ hoàn thành (Past Perfect) (B1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây theo 7 dạng bài từ cơ bản đến nâng cao. Không tra đáp án trước khi làm xong để kiểm tra chính xác khả năng nắm bắt lý thuyết và tránh bẫy đề thi.

---

## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (`S + had + V3/ed`, phủ định, nghi vấn, dạng `'d had` và động từ bất quy tắc).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Quá khứ hoàn thành (Past Perfect):**

1. By the time the inspector arrived, the factory workers ____________ (evacuate) the entire eastern wing.
2. Why ____________ (the manager / postpone) the press conference before the official statement came out?
3. She realized too late that she ____________ (not / compile) the latest sales figures for the board meeting.
4. When I checked my wallet at the gate, I discovered I ____________ (forget) my boarding pass at the hotel reception.
5. ____________ (you / ever / negotiate) such a complex contract prior to joining this firm?
6. Before the ambulance got there, the patient ____________ (lie) unconscious on the floor for nearly twenty minutes.
7. He mentioned that he ____________ (have) serious doubts about the timeline long before the project began.

---

## Part 2: Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt (Câu 8–15)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (`before/after/by the time`, mốc thời gian quá khứ, kỳ vọng chưa thành, `It was the first time`, câu điều kiện loại 3).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc (dùng Past Perfect hoặc dạng phù hợp theo cấu trúc câu):**

8. We ____________ (intend) to reserve the riverside venue for the annual gala, yet another organization booked it first.
9. By the end of 2021, the startup ____________ (implement) an unprecedented automated tracking system across twelve warehouses.
10. It was the second time that week that the automated billing portal ____________ (malfunction) during peak hours.
11. If the technician ____________ (acknowledge) the warning light earlier, the engine failure could have been prevented.
12. Up until yesterday's trial, the defendant ____________ (show) no remorse whatsoever for the financial losses he caused.
13. She ____________ (hope) to submit the grant application ahead of the deadline, but a sudden family emergency delayed her.
14. By the time the auditors uncovered the discrepancy, the former director ____________ (already / depart) for overseas.
15. After both delegations ____________ (negotiate) behind closed doors for ten hours, they finally signed a compromise.

---

## Part 3: Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Phân biệt Past Perfect với Past Simple và Past Continuous; tránh bẫy dùng máy móc.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì phù hợp nhất (Past Simple, Past Continuous, hoặc Past Perfect):**

16. Last night, I ____________ (lock) the front door, ____________ (turn) off the porch light, and ____________ (head) upstairs to bed.
17. When we got to the departure lounge, the passengers ____________ (already / board), so we had to sprint to Gate 14.
18. While the analysts ____________ (review) the quarterly spreadsheets, they noticed someone ____________ (alter) the totals the day before.
19. Prior to the merger last autumn, the two rival banks ____________ (compete) fiercely for nearly two decades.
20. Because he ____________ (oversleep) that morning, he missed the briefing and ____________ (receive) a formal warning.
21. When the fire alarm went off, everyone ____________ (run) toward the emergency exits immediately.
22. I wish I ____________ (anticipate) the sudden drop in demand before we ordered extra inventory last quarter.
23. When the CEO entered the conference room, the team ____________ (already / resolve) the dispute, so the atmosphere was calm.

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D — Gài bẫy B1–C1 (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi (đảo ngữ `No sooner/Hardly`, `had had`, `prior to`, chuỗi hành động).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):**

24. Hardly had the software update been rolled out ________ users began reporting severe login malfunctions.
    - A. than
    - B. when
    - C. then
    - D. that

25. No sooner ________ the compromise than the opposition party issued a statement criticizing the terms.
    - A. the committee had announced
    - B. did the committee announce
    - C. had the committee announced
    - D. the committee announced

26. She told the HR representative that she ________ a similar administrative role before moving to Singapore.
    - A. has held
    - B. was holding
    - C. had held
    - D. holds

27. By the time the rescue helicopter reached the remote valley, the hikers ________ shelter inside an abandoned cabin.
    - A. have already found
    - B. already found
    - C. were already finding
    - D. had already found

28. Prior to ________ the final draft, the research team had double-checked every single footnote for discrepancies.
    - A. submit
    - B. submitted
    - C. submitting
    - D. they had submitted

29. We ________ to launch the product in May, ________ unprecedented supply-chain bottlenecks forced us to postpone it until August.
    - A. had expected / but
    - B. expected / so
    - C. had expected / therefore
    - D. have expected / yet

30. Scarcely had the concert started ________ a sudden power outage plunged the entire venue into darkness.
    - A. than
    - B. when
    - C. until
    - D. while

31. That was by far the most detailed report the senior editor ________ throughout his thirty-year career.
    - A. ever read
    - B. has ever read
    - C. had ever read
    - D. was ever reading

## Part 5: Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction) (Câu 32–38)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai thường gặp của người học Việt Nam và các bẫy về chia động từ / cặp liên từ.*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây có MỘT lỗi sai về ngữ pháp hoặc cấu trúc liên quan đến Past Perfect. Hãy xác định phần sai và viết lại cho đúng:**

32. No sooner had the airline acknowledged the baggage delay **when** hundreds of angry passengers demanded compensation.
33. By 2018, the municipal government **has implemented** strict emission rules that subsequently reduced air pollution by 30%.
34. She realized she **had forgot** to attach the compiled spreadsheet only after she had clicked the "Send" button.
35. Before he **had joined** our consultancy in 2020, he had worked as a financial auditor in Frankfurt for six years.
36. Hardly **the firefighters had evacuated** the residents when the roof of the warehouse collapsed.
37. It was the first time the young violinist **performed** on such a prestigious international stage.
38. He confessed that he **hadn't never anticipated** how fiercely the local community would oppose the construction plan.

## Part 6: Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa (Câu 39–44)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc (`Hardly...when`, `No sooner...than`, `By the time`, `Before/After`, kỳ vọng chưa thành).*

**Viết lại mỗi câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ gợi ý hoặc phần đầu câu cho sẵn:**

39. The IT department resolved the server malfunction, and immediately afterward, traffic surged back to normal.
    $\rightarrow$ **No sooner** ________________________________________________________________.

40. First, the team compiled all the survey data. Then, they submitted the findings to the ministry.
    $\rightarrow$ **Before** ___________________________________________________________________.

41. The train departed from Platform 3. A few minutes later, we rushed into the station.
    $\rightarrow$ **By the time** _______________________________________________________________.

42. She had scarcely unpacked her suitcase when the hotel staff notified her of a room upgrade.
    $\rightarrow$ **Hardly** ____________________________________________________________________.

43. We planned to host the workshop outdoors, though heavy rain forced us to move it indoors. *(Dùng `intend`)*
    $\rightarrow$ We __________________________________________________________________________.

44. The two CEOs negotiated the merger privately before they made a joint public announcement.
    $\rightarrow$ **After** _____________________________________________________________________.

## Part 7: Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 45–50)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Ứng dụng đời sống (Email công việc) & Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt (tránh lỗi Vietlish).*

**A. Hoàn thành đoạn trích email công việc dưới đây bằng cách chia đúng động từ trong ngoặc (Câu 45–47):**

> Dear Mr. Tanaka,
> Thank you for your patience regarding the delay in our Q3 delivery. Prior to your inquiry on Wednesday, our logistics unit **(45)** ____________ (already / identify) a minor discrepancy in the customs paperwork. Although we **(46)** ____________ (hope) to clear the shipment before the weekend, an unexpected inspection at the port postponed the release by 48 hours. **Subsequently**, as soon as our legal team **(47)** ____________ (provide) the missing certificates, the goods were dispatched immediately.

**B. Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh sử dụng thì Quá khứ hoàn thành và từ vựng đã học trong bài (Câu 48–50):**

48. Vào lúc tôi nhận ra mình đã ngủ quên, chuyến tàu đi Oxford đã khởi hành được mười lăm phút rồi.
49. Tôi vừa mới đặt xong địa điểm tổ chức hội thảo thì khách hàng đột ngột đề nghị hoãn sự kiện lại. *(Dùng cấu trúc đảo ngữ `No sooner...` hoặc `Hardly...`)*
50. Trước năm 2022, họ chưa bao giờ chứng kiến một cuộc khủng hoảng tài chính có quy mô chưa từng có tiền lệ như vậy.
