## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn & quy tắc `-ing`).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì **Past Perfect Continuous** (`had (not) been + V-ing`).

1. By the time the auditors arrived, the accounting department ___________________ (compile) the annual figures for nearly three weeks.
2. When the power finally came back on, the engineers ___________________ (try) to resolve the server outage since midnight.
3. She looked completely overwhelmed because she ___________________ (not / sleep) properly in the days leading up to the conference.
4. How long ___________________ the two delegations ___________________ (negotiate) before they finally reached a compromise?
5. ___________________ the research team ___________________ (investigate) the software vulnerability long before the data breach occurred?
6. The demonstrators ___________________ (lie) down across the main entrance for over an hour before the police cleared the street.
7. I ___________________ (not / pay) close attention during the safety briefing, so I had no idea what to do when the alarm rang.

## Part 3: Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Câu 16–23)

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở **Past Perfect Continuous**, **Past Perfect**, hoặc **Past Continuous** phù hợp nhất với ngữ cảnh.

17. When the rescue helicopter finally arrived, the stranded hikers ___________________ (wait) in freezing temperatures for nearly nine hours.
18. By the time we got to the venue, the keynote speaker ___________________ (already / finish) her presentation, and guests were heading to the lobby.
19. The pavement was covered in deep puddles because torrential rain ___________________ (pour) down without interruption until just before dawn.
20. While I ___________________ (compile) the quarterly expense report yesterday morning, the system suddenly froze and erased my unsaved notes.
21. The two rivals ___________________ (compete) fiercely for market dominance for years before the merger put an end to their rivalry.
22. When we checked the laboratory at 6 p.m., Dr. Vance looked triumphant because he ___________________ (discover) the missing catalyst at last.
23. Currency exchange rates ___________________ (fluctuate) unpredictably for months before the central bank intervened to stabilize the market.

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1) (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi, Ngoại lệ & Mốc thời gian.*

Chọn đáp án chính xác nhất (**A**, **B**, **C**, hoặc **D**).

24. Before the treaty was signed, diplomats from both nations _______ behind closed doors for nearly eighteen months.
    - A. were negotiating
    - B. had been negotiating
    - C. have been negotiating
    - D. would negotiate

25. Look at her eyes—she looks completely exhausted! She _______ to meet that tight deadline since 6 a.m.
    - A. had been working
    - B. was working
    - C. has been working
    - D. had worked

26. By the time the marathon runner crossed the finish line, she _______ over forty-two kilometers under the scorching sun.
    - A. had been running
    - B. was running
    - C. had run
    - D. has run

27. The antique clock stopped working yesterday even though my grandfather _______ it carefully for thirty years before giving it to me.
    - A. had been owning
    - B. was owning
    - C. had owned
    - D. has owned

28. Tom admitted during the interview that he _______ under immense strain for months prior to his decision to resign.
    - A. has been operating
    - B. was operating
    - C. had been operating
    - D. operates

29. How long _______ when the persistent power interruption forced the factory to halt production?
    - A. the machines had been running
    - B. had the machines been running
    - C. were the machines running
    - D. have the machines been running

30. If the pilot _______ closely to the weather warnings during the flight, the emergency landing might have been avoided.
    - A. had been listening
    - B. was listening
    - C. has been listening
    - D. listened

31. Toxic waste _______ in the riverbed for decades before independent inspectors uncovered the environmental violation.
    - A. is accumulating
    - B. accumulated
    - C. has been accumulating
    - D. had been accumulating

## Part 6: Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa (Câu 39–44)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc (Reported Speech, Nguyên nhân–Kết quả, Thời lượng trước mốc quá khứ, Câu điều kiện/ước).*

Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng gợi ý trong ngoặc và thì **Past Perfect Continuous** (hoặc cấu trúc tương thích).

39. "I have been compiling these survey results since early morning," Maya told her supervisor.
    → Maya told her supervisor that ___________________________________________________.
40. They started negotiating the trade deal in March. They finally reached a compromise in November. (for eight months / before)
    → They _________________________________________________________________________.
41. David felt overwhelming fatigue yesterday because he drove non-stop for seven hours. (had)
    → David felt overwhelming fatigue yesterday because _______________________________.
42. I regret not paying attention while the consultant was explaining the risk assessment. (wish)
    → I _____________________________________________________________________________.
43. The marathon training lasted six months; then she suffered a serious knee injury. (for / when)
    → She ___________________________________________________________________________.
44. "Were you waiting outside in the cold for long before the doors opened?" the receptionist asked me.
    → The receptionist asked me ______________________________________________________.
