## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Mixed 3–2, Mixed 2–3 và câu nghi vấn hỗn hợp).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc theo đúng cấu trúc **Mixed Conditional** phù hợp với mốc thời gian của từng vế.

1. If we (allocate) __________ sufficient funding to the project last quarter, we wouldn't be facing such severe setbacks now.
2. If Sarah (be) __________ more punctual by nature, she wouldn't have missed the crucial client briefing yesterday morning.
3. What (you / do) __________ right now if you (resign) __________ from that toxic company two years ago?
4. If David (not / make) __________ such a reckless investment in 2023, he (not / struggle) __________ to pay off his debts at the moment.
5. (you / accept) __________ the promotion in Singapore last month if you (speak) __________ fluent Mandarin?
6. If our department (not / be) __________ so short-staffed at present, we (could / complete) __________ the audit before yesterday's deadline.
7. Why (she / still / feel) __________ so exhausted today if she (not / stay) __________ up all night revising the contract?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi Đảo ngữ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết & Quy tắc biến đổi (Đảo ngữ Loại 1, Loại 2, Loại 3 và quy tắc vị trí `not`).*

Hoàn thành các câu **đảo ngữ câu điều kiện** dưới đây bằng cách điền dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc (tuyệt đối không dùng `If`).

8. (Should / she / hesitate) __________ to sign the agreement tomorrow, please invite the legal team to explain the terms.
9. (Were / I / be) __________ in your circumstances, I would never resign without securing another offer first.
10. (Had / the committee / not / intervene) __________ immediately last week, the dispute would have damaged the firm's reputation.
11. (Were / the board / approve) __________ the proposal today, we could mitigate most of our operational risks this year.
12. (Should / any employee / fail) __________ to comply with the safety regulations, management will issue a formal warning.
13. (Had / they / persist) __________ with the negotiation yesterday, they (might / reach) __________ a compromise by now.
14. (Were / it / not / for) __________ her fluent Japanese, our team wouldn't have closed the deal in Tokyo last Friday.
15. (Had / he / not / be) __________ so reluctant to seek advice last year, where (his business / be) __________ today?

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết & Phân biệt ngữ cảnh (Mixed vs Câu điều kiện thuần; Trạng thái cố hữu vs Hành động quá khứ).*

Chọn dạng đúng trong ngoặc đơn để câu phản ánh đúng logic thời gian và ngữ cảnh.

16. If Lisa **(didn't have / hadn't had)** a fear of public speaking, she would have delivered the keynote speech at last week's conference.
17. **(Were / Had)** the weather been calmer yesterday afternoon, the rescue helicopter could have landed safely.
18. If I **(took / had taken)** that intensive course last summer, I **(would speak / would have spoken)** French much more fluently now.
19. **(Should / Were)** the client request a revised timeline next Monday, we will have to postpone the product launch.
20. If he **(weren't / hadn't been)** so careless at that exact moment during the experiment, the glass beaker wouldn't have shattered.
21. **(Had / Were)** I to know his true intentions now, I wouldn't trust him with our confidential records.
22. If Mark **(wasn't / hadn't been)** an experienced negotiator, he wouldn't have secured such a favourable deal last month.
23. **(Had it not been for / Were it not for)** the firefighters' rapid response last night, the warehouse wouldn't still be standing today.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

Chọn đáp án chính xác nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).

24. __________ the manager's diligent supervision, the project would not be running so smoothly today.
    A. Were it not for
    B. Had it not been for
    C. Should it not be for
    D. If it wasn't

25. __________ to reconsider our offer, we would be delighted to allocate additional resources to your branch.
    A. Were your company
    B. Should your company
    C. Had your company
    D. Did your company

26. __________ the safety warnings earlier, the technicians wouldn't be dealing with serious consequences now.
    A. Hadn't they ignored
    B. Were they not to ignore
    C. Had they not ignored
    D. Should they not ignore

27. __________ any unexpected complications arise during the flight, the crew will follow emergency procedures.
    A. Were
    B. Had
    C. Unless
    D. Should

28. If Emma __________ more qualifications in data analytics, she __________ for that senior role last November.
    A. held / would have applied
    B. had held / would apply
    C. holds / would have applied
    D. were holding / applied

29. __________ so recklessly on the icy road yesterday, he wouldn't be recovering in hospital at the moment.
    A. Were he not driving
    B. Hadn't he driven
    C. Had he not driven
    D. Should he not drive

30. How would the negotiations have turned out last week if the CEO __________ less reluctant to compromise by nature?
    A. would have been
    B. were
    C. would be
    D. has been

31. __________ your advice at the start of my career, I doubt I would possess the confidence I have today.
    A. Were I not to follow
    B. Had I not followed
    C. Should I not follow
    D. If I didn't follow

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai kinh điển & Bẫy viết tắt / chia động từ trong đảo ngữ.*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai ngữ pháp**. Hãy xác định phần sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng.

32. Hadn't the doctors intervened promptly, the patient's condition would have deteriorated rapidly.
33. Should the supplier postpones the delivery again, our factory will suffer severe financial consequences.
34. Were I to know about the traffic jam yesterday morning, I would have taken the train instead.
35. If she hadn't been so lazy as a student, she would have earned her degree and would have a better qualification last year.
36. Did the government enforce stricter environmental laws, corporations wouldn't jeopardise public health so blatantly.
37. Had we not wasted half the budget in March, we would have been able to expand the team right now.
38. Weren't he to comply with the court order, he would immediately face severe penalties.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa

    $\rightarrow$ Had it _____________________________________________________________.

    $\rightarrow$ Were he ____________________________________________________________.
43. If the authorities raised taxes on fuel next month, commuters would protest strongly.
    $\rightarrow$ Were the authorities _________________________________________________.

    $\rightarrow$ If I _________________________________________________________________.

## Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt & Ứng dụng đời sống.*

### A. Hoàn thành email công việc sau (Câu 45–47) bằng cách chia đúng dạng từ trong ngoặc (có thể dùng đảo ngữ hoặc câu điều kiện hỗn hợp phù hợp):

> Dear Mr. Tan,
>
> Thank you for your patience regarding the delayed shipment. **(45) (Had / our logistics partner / notify)** __________ us about the port congestion last Tuesday, we **(46) (not / deal)** __________ with this backlog today. To prevent further disruption, we are offering an immediate air-freight alternative. **(47) (Should / your team / agree)** __________ to this option by 5 p.m., we will absorb all additional shipping costs.
>
> Best regards,
> Minh Anh

### B. Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh (Câu 48–50), bắt buộc sử dụng cấu trúc **Mixed Conditional** hoặc **Đảo ngữ Câu Điều kiện** phù hợp:

48. Nếu hồi năm ngoái anh ấy không đưa ra quyết định liều lĩnh như vậy, thì bây giờ uy tín của công ty đã không bị ảnh hưởng rồi. *(Gợi ý: reckless, reputation — dùng đảo ngữ `Had`)*
49. Nếu bản tính cô ấy không quá miễn cưỡng thay đổi như vậy, thì tuần trước cô ấy đã nắm bắt cơ hội quý giá đó rồi. *(Gợi ý: reluctant, seize)*
50. Trong trường hợp bạn cần thêm bất kỳ thông tin nào về bằng cấp của ứng viên, xin đừng ngần ngại liên hệ với tôi. *(Gợi ý: qualification, hesitate — dùng đảo ngữ `Should`)*
