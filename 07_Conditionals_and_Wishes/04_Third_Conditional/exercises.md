## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia động từ cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức Khẳng định, Phủ định và Nghi vấn của Câu Điều kiện Loại 3.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng của Câu Điều kiện Loại 3:**

1. If I (know) ____________ about the severe traffic jam yesterday, I wouldn't have regretted taking the highway.
2. She (avoid) ____________ that expensive mistake if she had listened to her mentor's advice.
3. If Tom (back up) ____________ his thesis on a cloud drive last semester, he wouldn't have lost three months of research.
4. ____________ you (miss out on) ____________ the scholarship if you had submitted your application a day late?
5. They wouldn't have faced a heavy fine if the accountant (not / overlook) ____________ the tax deadline.
6. How much money ____________ the company (save) ____________ if the managers hadn't underestimated the shipping costs?
7. If the driver (not / be) ____________ so irresponsible last night, the passengers wouldn't have been injured.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Modal Verbs, Bị động, Tiếp diễn & Cấu trúc mở rộng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.2 & Mục 3 — Sắc thái `would/could/might`, thể Bị động, Tiếp diễn, Đảo ngữ và `But for / Without`.*

**Hoàn thành câu sử dụng từ gợi ý trong ngoặc sao cho đúng ngữ pháp và sắc thái nghĩa:**

8. If the IT team had patched the software earlier, the security breach ____________ (could / prevent — thể bị động).
9. He ____________ (might / stay) safe during the storm if he had taken proper safety precautions beforehand.
10. If the committee had investigated the complaint thoroughly at that time, they ____________ (might / uncover) the truth.
11. But for the mediator's help, the two sides ____________ (could / not / negotiate) a fair agreement last week.
12. Had the CEO ____________ (acknowledge) the problem sooner, the investors wouldn't have panicked last month.
13. If the system ____________ (compromise — thể bị động) by malware, the entire network would have shut down immediately.
14. If she ____________ (not / work — nhấn mạnh quá trình liên tục) overtime all week before the launch, she wouldn't have missed that crucial detail.
15. If the planners had anticipated the sudden flood, the bridge ____________ (not / damage — thể bị động) so severely.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt Câu Điều kiện Loại 2 vs Loại 3
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.1 & 4.2 — Phân biệt giả định trái thực tế ở hiện tại (Loại 2) và quá khứ đã khép lại (Loại 3).*

**Đọc kỹ ngữ cảnh thời gian của mỗi câu và chia động từ trong ngoặc theo đúng Loại 2 hoặc Loại 3:**

16. The project failed last quarter. If the team (consider) ____________ the potential consequences beforehand, they (not / lose) ____________ that major client.
17. I'm not a millionaire right now, so I can't donate that much. If I (have) ____________ that kind of money today, I (build) ____________ a shelter for stray animals.
18. The wildfire spread rapidly in 2022 and caused devastating damage. If firefighters (intervene) ____________ earlier, fewer forests (burn) ____________ down.
19. Stop making such reckless decisions! If you (be) ____________ more cautious in general, your parents (not / worry) ____________ about you every single day.
20. When we arrived at the museum last Sunday, it was closed. If we (check) ____________ the opening hours first, we (not / waste) ____________ two hours on the bus.
21. She speaks Japanese poorly, so she can't apply for the Tokyo office next month. If she (speak) ____________ it fluently, she (get) ____________ the promotion easily.
22. Back in 2019, he turned down a job offer in Singapore. If he (accept) ____________ it back then, he (gain) ____________ invaluable international experience during that period.
23. Honestly, I don't know how to fix this engine at the moment. If I (be) ____________ a mechanic, I (repair) ____________ it for you right away.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.3 — Các bẫy đề thi kinh điển (bẫy `'d`, đảo ngữ phủ định, điều kiện ẩn, `wouldn't have had`).*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):**

24. If I ______ about the networking workshop last Friday, I ______ on such a rare chance to meet the recruiters.
    A. had known / wouldn't have missed out
    B. would know / hadn't missed out
    C. knew / wouldn't have missed out
    D. had known / wouldn't miss out

25. In hindsight, ______ the early warning signs, the crew could have avoided the collision.
    A. hadn't the captain ignored
    B. had the captain not ignored
    C. if the captain didn't ignore
    D. would the captain not ignore

26. ______ the government's delayed response in 2020, the economic impact wouldn't have been so detrimental to small businesses.
    A. If it weren't for
    B. Were it not for
    C. Had it not been for
    D. Unless it hadn't been for

27. If the technician ______ the faulty cable during yesterday's inspection, the factory ______ a power outage last night.
    A. replaced / wouldn't have
    B. would have replaced / hadn't had
    C. had replaced / wouldn't have
    D. had replaced / wouldn't have had

28. Had the board of directors ______ the employees' concerns earlier, the strike ______ peacefully.
    A. acknowledged / could have been resolved
    B. acknowledged / must have resolved
    C. been acknowledged / could resolve
    D. acknowledge / would be resolved

29. Nobody ______ the storm's sudden shift in direction, but even if meteorologists ______ it, evacuation would still have been difficult.
    A. had anticipated / anticipated
    B. anticipated / had anticipated
    C. anticipated / would anticipate
    D. would anticipate / had anticipated

30. They reinforced the dam just in time last month; otherwise, the heavy rains ______ the town's entire water supply.
    A. would compromise
    B. had compromised
    C. would have compromised
    D. compromised

31. Emergency teams arrived within ten minutes; ______ slower, the flooding would have been far harder to mitigate.
    A. if they were
    B. were they
    C. had they been
    D. if they would have been

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.3 & Mục 5 — Lỗi sai kinh điển và thói quen dịch Vietlish.*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây có một phần in đậm bị sai ngữ pháp `[A]`, `[B]`, `[C]` hoặc `[D]`. Hãy xác định chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng:**

32. If he [A] **would have told** me the truth [B] **at the meeting** yesterday, I [C] **would have defended** his decision [D] **without hesitation**.
33. She [A] **could have avoid** [B] **paying** the late fee if she [C] **had transferred** the rent [D] **before Friday**.
34. If the police [A] **didn't investigate** the warehouse [B] **promptly** last week, the stolen goods [C] **would have been shipped** [D] **abroad**.
35. If [A] **I'd have overlooked** that [B] **minor** calculation error, the bridge design [C] **might have collapsed** during [D] **the 2021 stress test**.
36. [A] **Hadn't she reacted** so calmly [B] **during the crisis**, a [C] **crucial** piece of evidence [D] **would have been lost**.
37. If the manager [A] **wasn't** so [B] **irresponsible** during last year's audit, the department [C] **wouldn't have suffered** such a [D] **severe** penalty.
38. He [A] **wouldn't have crash** his car [B] **into the barrier** if he [C] **hadn't been driving** so [D] **recklessly** in the fog.

## Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 5 & Mục 6 — Ứng dụng vào ngữ cảnh đời sống và tránh lỗi Vietlish khi dịch.*

### A. Hoàn thành đoạn văn (Câu 45–47)
**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng của Câu Điều kiện Loại 3 để hoàn thành đoạn văn kể chuyện sau:**

Last month, Minh suffered a major setback just hours before a big presentation. A sudden power outage wiped his laptop's hard drive, and because he hadn't saved his files online, he lost two weeks of work. Looking back, if he **(45. back up)** ________________ his portfolio on a cloud server, he **(46. not / face)** ________________ such devastating consequences. Moreover, if he had tested his backup battery earlier, he might have avoided the panic altogether. His colleague reminded him that if the client **(47. not / agree)** ________________ to extend the deadline by 24 hours, Minh would have lost his biggest contract of the year.

### B. Dịch Việt – Anh ứng dụng (Câu 48–50)
**Dịch các câu sau sang tiếng Anh, sử dụng Câu Điều kiện Loại 3 (và từ gợi ý trong ngoặc):**

48. Nếu hôm qua tôi nghe lời khuyên của bạn, tôi đã không phải hối tiếc vì quyết định vội vàng đó. *(Gợi ý: `regret`)*
49. Ngẫm lại thì, giá như ban giám đốc không bỏ sót khoản chi phí đó, dự án đã có thể mang lại lợi nhuận hồi năm ngoái. *(Gợi ý: `in hindsight`, `overlook`)*
50. Nếu chính quyền địa phương can thiệp sớm hơn, sự chậm trễ đó đã không gây tổn hại nghiêm trọng đến ngành du lịch. *(Gợi ý: `intervene`, `detrimental`)*
