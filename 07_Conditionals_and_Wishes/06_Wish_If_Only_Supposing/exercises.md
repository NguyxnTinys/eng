# Bài tập thực hành: Câu Ước (Wish / If only) & Liên từ Điều kiện nâng cao (B1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn**: Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài). Không xem file đáp án trước khi tự làm xong để rèn phản xạ né bẫy ngữ pháp thực chiến.

---

## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia động từ cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn với Wish / If only / Hope).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng nhất:

1. I really **regret** leaving my hometown; I wish I __________ (live) closer to my parents right now.
2. Why do you wish you __________ (not / say) those harsh words to your manager during yesterday's meeting?
3. If only the neighbors __________ (stop) playing loud music late at night so I could focus on my work!
4. We sincerely hope that both sides __________ (reach) a fair **compromise** at tomorrow's discussion.
5. Do you ever wish you __________ (can / speak) Spanish fluently when traveling around South America?
6. She wishes she __________ (not / be) so shy whenever she has to present her ideas to senior directors.
7. I wish I __________ (have) enough time next month to join your volunteering trip to the highlands.

## Part 2: Áp dụng Cách dùng, Quy tắc biến đổi & Liên từ điều kiện (Câu 8–15)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết, Quy tắc biến đổi `were / had V3` & Cấu trúc `Provided that`, `As long as`, `Without / But for`.*

Điền dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc hoặc hoàn thành chỗ trống bằng từ/cụm thích hợp theo gợi ý:

8. If only the timeline __________ (be) more **feasible** under our current **circumstances**, we wouldn't feel so stressed.
9. **Without** your **indispensable** technical support, our team __________ (not / launch) the platform on schedule last week.
10. You are allowed to work remotely **provided that** you __________ (attend) all mandatory Monday briefings.
11. **But for** his determination to **overcome** every obstacle, the startup __________ (not / exist) today.
12. **Supposing** the supplier __________ (refuse) to **negotiate** a lower price yesterday, what would you have done?
13. I wish my brother __________ (not / interfere) in my career decisions every single time we talk.
14. They agreed to **implement** the reform **on condition that** the board __________ (stipulate) a clear evaluation period first.
15. If only I __________ (acknowledge) that **shortcoming** earlier so we could have avoided this **consequence**!

## Part 3: Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Phân biệt `Wish` vs `Hope`, `In case` vs `If`, `As long as` vs `Unless`, `Without` vs `If`.*

Chọn từ hoặc cụm từ thích hợp trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu:

16. Let's transfer the backup files to an external drive (**in case / if**) the system crashes unexpectedly.
17. I (**wish / hope**) our flight isn't delayed tonight because I have an early interview tomorrow morning.
18. You may borrow my car for the weekend (**as long as / in case**) you return it with a full tank of petrol.
19. (**Without / Unless**) strict quality control, faulty products would soon damage the brand's reputation.
20. Please call the reception desk immediately (**in case / if**) any **unforeseen** problems occur during your stay.
21. I (**wish / hope**) I knew how to fix this software bug myself instead of waiting for the IT team.
22. (**Supposing / On condition that**) you lost your passport abroad, who would you contact first?
23. (**In case / In case of**) a power failure, the emergency generators will activate automatically within five seconds.

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D — Gài bẫy B1–C1 (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & 6 Bẫy đề thi kinh điển.*

Chọn phương án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):

24. I wish I __________ so much when the opportunity to lead the overseas branch came up last year.
    - A. didn't hesitate
    - B. wouldn't hesitate
    - C. hadn't hesitated
    - D. haven't hesitated

25. The CEO promised not to **postpone** the annual bonus **provided that** the sales department __________ its quarterly target.
    - A. will fulfill
    - B. fulfills
    - C. fulfilled
    - D. would fulfill

26. I wish I __________ attend the design conference in Tokyo next week, but my visa hasn't been approved yet.
    - A. would
    - B. could
    - C. will
    - D. had

27. __________ your timely **precaution**, the warehouse would have suffered severe water damage during the storm.
    - A. If not
    - B. Unless
    - C. But for
    - D. Provided that

28. Always keep a **contingency** fund aside __________ an unexpected medical expense arises.
    - A. as long as
    - B. on condition that
    - C. in case
    - D. if only

29. I really hope the client __________ our revised proposal by Friday so we can start production.
    - A. would approve
    - B. approved
    - C. approves
    - D. had approved

30. She seems so **reluctant** to speak up; if only she __________ more confident in front of the investors right now!
    - A. were
    - B. would be
    - C. had been
    - D. is

31. **Supposing** nobody __________ to **resign** after the merger, how many new positions would the company need to create?
    - A. decides
    - B. decided
    - C. would decide
    - D. has decided

## Part 5: Tìm và sửa lỗi sai — Error Identification & Correction (Câu 32–38)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi & Các lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay gặp.*

Mỗi câu sau có 4 phần gạch chân/đánh dấu **(A), (B), (C), (D)**. Hãy xác định **1 phần sai** và **viết lại cho đúng**:

32. I **(A) wish** I **(B) would be** more **(C) punctual** when I **(D) go** to work tomorrow morning.
33. **(A) But for** the team **(B) worked** overtime last weekend, we **(C) wouldn't have met** the client's **(D) strict** deadline.
34. Take a printed copy of your boarding pass **(A) if** the airline app **(B) fails** to load **(C) at** the security **(D) gate**.
35. We will sign the lease **(A) on condition that** the landlord **(B) will repair** the leaking roof **(C) before** we move **(D) in**.
36. I **(A) hope** my roommate **(B) wouldn't leave** wet towels **(C) on** the sofa every single **(D) evening**.
37. If only I **(A) hadn't turned** down that scholarship offer **(B) three years ago**; my career **(C) would have been** completely different **(D) now**.
38. **(A) Providing that** you **(B) don't hesitate** to ask for help, you will easily **(C) overcoming** every **(D) shortcoming** in your draft.

## Part 7: Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 45–50)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ứng dụng đời sống & Liên hệ tư duy Tiếng Việt (tránh lỗi Vietlish).*

### A. Hoàn thành đoạn email công việc sau (Câu 45–47)
Điền **1–3 từ** thích hợp (từ nhóm cấu trúc đã học hoặc chia động từ trong ngoặc) vào mỗi chỗ trống:

> *Hi Linh,*
>
> *Thank you for sending over the risk assessment for next week's product launch. I **wish** our marketing vendor **(45) __________ (be)** more responsive right now, because waiting for their final banners is slowing us down. However, I **hope** they **(46) __________ (deliver)** all visual assets by Thursday afternoon.*
>
> *We can still proceed with the launch on Friday **provided that** the technical team completes the server check tomorrow. Also, please prepare a short press statement **(47) __________** any **unforeseen** technical glitch occurs during the livestream. **Without** your thorough preparation, this launch would be far riskier.*
>
> *Best,*  
> *Nam*
