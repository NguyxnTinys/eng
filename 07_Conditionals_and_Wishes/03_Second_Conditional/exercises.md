## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia động từ cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc theo đúng cấu trúc Câu điều kiện Loại 2:

1. If our company __________ (have) a bigger budget, we __________ (expand) into three new markets this year.
2. If I __________ (not / commute) two hours every day, I __________ (have) much more time to exercise.
3. What __________ you __________ (do) if your manager __________ (ask) you to handle an overwhelming workload?
4. If she __________ (be) fluent in Japanese, she __________ (apply) for the senior translator position immediately.
5. __________ you __________ (accept) a remote job if the salary __________ (be) slightly lower than your current one?
6. If they __________ (not / hesitate) to invest early, their startup __________ (be) much stronger right now.
7. Where __________ he __________ (live) if he __________ (decide) to relocate to another country?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Quy tắc `were` cho mọi ngôi, biến thể `could / might`, cấu trúc `were to + V` và lời khuyên lịch sự*

Hoàn thành các câu sau sử dụng biến thể phù hợp (`were`, `could`, `might`, hoặc `were to + V-inf`):

8. If I __________ (be) you, I wouldn't resign without securing another offer first.
9. If the city council __________ (implement) stricter traffic rules next year, air pollution __________ (decrease) significantly. *(Dùng cấu trúc giả định trang trọng `were to + V` ở mệnh đề If)*
10. If Minh __________ (speak) more clearly during presentations, clients __________ (understand) his perspective easily. *(Dùng modal chỉ khả năng)*
11. If we postponed the launch until Friday, the design team __________ (finish) the final prototype in time, though it's not guaranteed. *(Dùng modal chỉ xác suất không chắc chắn)*
12. It __________ (be) much more efficient if everyone __________ (set) clear priorities before starting the project.
13. If the new director __________ (be) more open to compromise, negotiations wouldn't stall like this.
14. Suppose the factory __________ (face) a sudden shortage of raw materials tomorrow—how would you keep production running? *(Dùng `were to + V`)*
15. If Maya __________ (not / be) so afraid of failure, she __________ (start) her own business right now. *(Dùng `were` ở mệnh đề If)*

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Loại 1 vs Loại 2)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy thực tế vs giả định*

Đọc kỹ ngữ cảnh thực tế trong mỗi câu và chia động từ theo **Loại 1** (có khả năng xảy ra) hoặc **Loại 2** (trái thực tế hiện tại / khó xảy ra):

16. Look at the dark clouds! If it __________ (rain) this afternoon, we __________ (postpone) the outdoor team-building event.
17. I'm really sorry I can't help you with the report now. If I __________ (not / have) such a tight deadline, I __________ (review) it for you.
18. Don't worry about the interview tomorrow. If you __________ (prepare) your answers carefully tonight, you __________ (feel) confident.
19. Housing in this district is terribly expensive. If apartments here __________ (be) more affordable, many young teachers __________ (rent) them.
20. She is currently working part-time while finishing her degree. If she __________ (have) a full-time schedule right now, she __________ (not / be) able to keep up with her classes.
21. Let's check the budget first. If we __________ (save) enough money by next month, we __________ (buy) two new laptops for the office.
22. Nobody actually expects aliens to land on Earth tomorrow, of course. Still, if they __________ (arrive) tomorrow, how __________ governments __________ (react)?
23. Actually, I'm not the CEO of this company. If I __________ (run) this organization, I __________ (invest) far more in staff training.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–B2)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi & Ngoại lệ*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):

24. If I __________ more fluent in German, I wouldn't need an interpreter for next week's meeting.
    - A. am
    - B. would be
    - C. were
    - D. had been

25. Many employees __________ resign if the management __________ to cut annual bonuses without notice.
    - A. will / decided
    - B. might / were
    - C. would / would decide
    - D. might / were to decide

26. __________ you relocate to Da Nang if the company covered all your moving expenses?
    - A. Will
    - B. Do
    - C. Would
    - D. Were

27. If he __________ so much about every small detail, we could reach a compromise much faster.
    - A. didn't hesitate
    - B. wouldn't hesitate
    - C. doesn't hesitate
    - D. wasn't hesitate

28. What would you prioritize first if you __________ a substantial grant for your research?
    - A. receive
    - B. received
    - C. would receive
    - D. were receive

29. If I __________ you, I __________ the deadline before agreeing to take on any additional tasks.
    - A. was / will negotiate
    - B. am / would negotiate
    - C. were / would negotiate
    - D. were / negotiated

30. The local transport system would be far more efficient if the city __________ a new metro line.
    - A. built
    - B. builds
    - C. would build
    - D. has built

31. If we __________ work remotely three days a week, our daily commute costs __________ drop dramatically.
    - A. can / would
    - B. could / will
    - C. could / would
    - D. were / could
