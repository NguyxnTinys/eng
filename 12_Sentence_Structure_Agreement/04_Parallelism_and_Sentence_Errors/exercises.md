# Bài tập thực hành: Cấu trúc Song song (Parallelism) & Lỗi Câu thường gặp (B2–C1)

**Tổng số câu:** 50 câu (đánh số liên tục `1.` – `50.`), chia thành 7 dạng bài bám sát từng mảng lý thuyết và bẫy đề thi.

## Part 1: Nhận diện & Hoàn thành Cấu trúc Song song cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2A — Công thức Song song theo từ loại, cụm từ trong câu khẳng định, phủ định và nghi vấn.*

Hoàn thành mỗi câu sau bằng cách chọn hoặc chia dạng đúng của từ/cụm từ trong ngoặc sao cho đảm bảo tính song song (**parallelism**).

1. A good manager must remain calm, patient, and __________ (objectivity) even during a sudden crisis.
2. The consulting firm plans to review our budget, __________ (evaluation) our current workflow, and suggest practical improvements.
3. Neither the preliminary findings nor the final report __________ (confirm / confirmed / confirming) any link between the two variables, nor did the researchers hide or __________ (exaggerated) the error rate.
4. Did the committee decide to __________ (allocation) more funds to research or __________ (postponing) the project until next quarter?
5. How can small businesses remain __________ (resilience), adapt to digital trends, and __________ (expansion) into international markets without overspending?
6. The editorial lacked clarity, __________ (consistent), and persuasive evidence, so it failed to convince the board.
7. She prefers working independently in the morning, __________ (attend) brief team meetings at noon, and writing reports in the late afternoon.

## Part 2: Quy tắc Song song nâng cao — Tương quan, So sánh & Lược bỏ (Câu 8–15)

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Gài bẫy B2–C1) (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

Chọn **một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D)** cho mỗi câu sau.

24. Having completed the preliminary audit, __________.
    - A. several accounting errors were immediately identified
    - B. the report was submitted to the chief financial officer
    - C. the external auditors presented their key recommendations
    - D. it became clear that the budget needed revision

25. The government launched new tax incentives; __________, many manufacturers remained reluctant to relocate their factories.
    - A. therefore
    - B. consequently
    - C. however
    - D. in addition

26. Which of the following sentences is **grammatically correct and free of ambiguity**?
    - A. The journalist interviewed the scientist who discovered the vaccine in a tailored suit.
    - B. Wearing a tailored suit, the scientist who discovered the vaccine was interviewed by the journalist.
    - C. The journalist in a tailored suit interviewed the scientist who discovered the vaccine.
    - D. Interviewing the scientist, a tailored suit was worn by the journalist.

27. Effective public speaking requires careful preparation, clear articulation, and __________.
    - A. you must maintain eye contact with the audience
    - B. maintaining steady eye contact with the audience
    - C. steady eye contact with the audience
    - D. to keep eye contact steadily with the audience

28. __________, the software update failed to resolve the security vulnerability.
    - A. Although it was tested thoroughly by engineers
    - B. Because engineers tested it thoroughly before launch

29. Which sentence contains a **Comma Splice**?
    - A. While the strategy was ambitious, its execution lacked consistency.

    - C. The strategy was ambitious; however, its execution lacked consistency.

30. Not only __________ clear guidelines, but the handbook also illustrates common pitfalls with real examples.
    - A. the handbook provides

## Part 5: Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction) (Câu 32–38)

## Part 6: Viết lại câu & Sửa lỗi câu giữ nguyên nghĩa (Câu 39–44)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp công thức sửa Fragment, Run-on, Comma Splice, Modifier Error và Parallelism.*

Viết lại hoặc kết hợp các câu sau theo gợi ý trong ngoặc để sửa triệt để lỗi ngữ pháp mà vẫn giữ nguyên nghĩa gốc.

39. **Câu gốc (Comma Splice):** The company allocated a larger budget to cybersecurity, nevertheless, hackers still breached the main database.
    → *(Sửa lại bằng cách sử dụng dấu chấm phẩy `;` chuẩn học thuật).*

40. **Câu gốc (Dangling Modifier):** After reviewing all twenty applications, the most qualified candidate was selected for the role.
    → *(Viết lại câu bắt đầu bằng `After reviewing all twenty applications, the hiring panel...`).*

41. **Câu gốc (Faulty Parallelism):** A persuasive essay should be well-researched, have a clear structure, and its tone must remain objective.
    → *(Viết lại câu dùng 3 tính từ hoặc 3 cụm tính từ song song sau `should be...`).*

42. **Câu gốc (Sentence Fragment):** The city council plans to renovate the public library. Which has served local residents for nearly a century.
    → *(Kết hợp thành một câu phức chuẩn ngữ pháp).*

43. **Câu gốc (Run-on Sentence):** Urban green spaces help mitigate summer heat they also improve residents' mental well-being.
    → *(Kết hợp thành một câu sử dụng cấu trúc `Not only ... but also` có đảo ngữ).*

    → *(Đặt lại vị trí từ `only` cho đúng ý nghĩa mong muốn).*
