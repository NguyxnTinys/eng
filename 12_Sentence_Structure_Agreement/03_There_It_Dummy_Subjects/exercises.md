# Bài tập thực hành: Chủ ngữ giả It & Cấu trúc There is/are (A1–B2)

**Hướng dẫn làm bài:** Hoàn thành đầy đủ 50 câu hỏi dưới đây theo yêu cầu từng phần. Xem lại phần Lý thuyết tương ứng trước khi làm nếu cần. Không xem đáp án trước khi hoàn thành.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–8): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 (Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết — chia `There + be` qua các thì, quy tắc tiệm cận, chủ ngữ giả `It` cơ bản).*

**Chia dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc hoặc điền `It` / `There` thích hợp vào chỗ trống.**

1. Excuse me, _________ (be) there any reliable accommodation near the university campus?
2. There _________ (be) a modern laptop and two spare monitors on the manager's desk right now.
3. Yesterday morning, there _________ (be) severe traffic congestion on the main bridge because of an accident.
4. _________ (be) there going to be a press conference after the trade talks finish tomorrow?
5. Since 2020, there _________ (be) considerable improvements in the city's public infrastructure.
6. According to the weather forecast, _________ is extremely humid outside today, and _________ is a chance of heavy rain tonight.
7. It usually _________ (take) my brother forty-five minutes to commute to the industrial zone.
8. There _________ (not / be) enough reliable buses in this town when I lived here ten years ago.

## Part 2 (Câu 9–16): Áp dụng cấu trúc nâng cao & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 (Cách dùng chi tiết — `There seems/appears to be`, `It is + adj + for/of sb + to V`, `It takes`, `It is no use/good + V-ing`).*

**Điền giới từ (`for` / `of`) hoặc chia dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc.**

9. It was very considerate _________ you to warn me about the roadworks before I left home.
10. It is crucial _________ every employee to read the updated safety regulations carefully.
11. It is no use _________ (persuade) David to change his plan once he has made up his mind.
12. There _________ (seem) to be several major obstacles to completing the highway on schedule.
13. At the moment, there _________ (appear) to be a serious shortage of qualified nurses in rural clinics.
14. It is no good _________ (complain) about the demanding schedule if you never talk to your supervisor.
15. How long did it take the engineers _________ (repair) the damaged power infrastructure?
16. It was completely unreasonable _________ the client to demand a full refund without any evidence.

## Part 3 (Câu 17–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Bảng phân biệt `There` vs `It`, cảnh báo dùng máy móc & các cấu trúc dễ nhầm).*

**Điền `It`, `There`, hoặc chia dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc sao cho phù hợp với ngữ cảnh.**

17. _________ is about fifteen kilometres from my apartment to the airport, so _________ isn't enough time to walk there.
18. _________ seems to be a misunderstanding between the two departments regarding the delivery date.
19. _________ seems that nobody checked the weather forecast before scheduling the outdoor event.
20. It is definitely not worth _________ (negotiate) with a partner who refuses to share reliable figures.
21. It was extremely generous _________ our neighbours to let us use their garage during the renovation.
22. Look at those dark clouds! _________ is getting windy already, and _________ is going to be a thunderstorm soon.
23. There _________ (be) plenty of efficient ways to save energy, and there _________ (be) also a new smart-home app you can try.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–B2)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 (Các bẫy đề thi kinh điển).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) cho mỗi câu sau.**

24. Over the past few months, _________ a noticeable shortage of affordable accommodation in the capital.
    - A. there was
    - B. there have been
    - C. there has been
    - D. it has been

25. It was very _________ of Sarah to stay late and help the new intern finish the presentation.
    - A. crucial
    - B. considerate
    - C. essential
    - D. exhausting

26. How long _________ to negotiate an agreement that satisfies both the union and the management?
    - A. it will take
    - B. will it take
    - C. will it takes
    - D. takes it

27. When my grandfather was young, _________ a small wooden ferry crossing this river every hour.
    - A. there used to be
    - B. there used to have
    - C. it used to be
    - D. there was used to be

28. _________ over a cancelled flight; let's check whether there is another airline available.
    - A. It is no use to worry
    - B. There is no use worrying
    - C. It is no good worrying
    - D. It is pointless worry

29. _________ an unexpected obstacle in the budget approval process, which might delay our launch.
    - A. It seems to be
    - B. There seems to be
    - C. There seems that
    - D. It is seeming to be

30. In recent years, there _________ a growing number of commuters who choose cycling to avoid traffic congestion.
    - A. is
    - B. has been
    - C. was
    - D. have been

31. _________ was quite exhausting to drive in heavy rain because _________ was severe congestion on every bridge.
    - A. It / it
    - B. There / there
    - C. It / there
    - D. There / it

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 & Mục 5 (Lỗi sai kinh điển & Lỗi tư duy Vietlish).*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây chứa MỘT lỗi sai ngữ pháp liên quan đến `There` hoặc `It`. Hãy chỉ ra phần sai và sửa lại cho đúng.**

32. In my hometown, there have many traditional markets and efficient bus routes for tourists.
33. There are a reliable colour printer and three laptops in the meeting room for the workshop.
34. It was really considerate for him to check the weather forecast before our mountain hike.
35. It is no use to persuade an unreasonable customer who refuses to listen to any explanation.
36. It is a huge shortage of skilled software testers in the local job market this year.
37. It took the legal team nearly three weeks negotiate the new infrastructure contract.
38. There seems to be several inevitable delays due to the humid weather and strong winds.

## Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
