## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức cơ bản (Động từ tri giác + O + V-bare / V-ing; Thể phủ định và nghi vấn của phân từ).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng (`V-bare`, `V-ing`, `Not V-ing`, hoặc `V3/ed / Not V3/ed`):

1. I watched the mechanic ______ (repair) the entire engine from start to finish so I could learn how to do it myself.
2. As I walked past the conference room, I overheard my colleagues ______ (negotiate) a new deadline with the supplier.
3. Did you see anyone ______ (enter) the restricted laboratory before the alarm went off?
4. She didn't notice the weather ______ (deteriorate) rapidly while she was working indoors.
5. ______ (not / know) the exact budget figures, why did you promise to submit the financial report today?
6. ______ (overwhelm) by the sudden workload, several junior employees requested a two-day extension.
7. ______ (not / give) enough time to prepare, the candidates struggled to answer the panel's questions.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng, Câu bị động & Phân từ hoàn thành
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (Bị động của động từ tri giác `be seen to V / V-ing`, Phân từ hoàn thành `Having V3 / Having been V3`, Rút gọn sau liên từ).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng thích hợp nhất (có thể cần `to-V`, `V-ing`, `V3/ed`, `Having V3`, hoặc `Having been V3`):

8. The suspect was seen ______ (leave) the warehouse shortly after midnight, just before the police arrived.
9. When I looked out the window, I saw a suspicious man ______ (climb) over our neighbor's fence.
10. ______ (implement) the new inventory software successfully, the IT department received warm praise from the board.
11. ______ (abandon) for decades before the city bought it, the historic theater required complete structural restoration.
12. I felt my hands ______ (tremble) uncontrollably as I stepped onto the stage to deliver my speech.
13. Once ______ (complete), the new bridge will significantly shorten the daily commute for thousands of residents.
14. The injured hiker was heard ______ (call) for help from the bottom of the misty valley.
15. ______ (not / receive) any official confirmation yet, we decided to postpone the product launch until next month.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi (Bẫy Dangling Participle, Bị động tri giác, Sai vị trí `not`, Nhầm `see/notice + that`).*

24. Walking along the riverbank at dusk, ______.
    A. the city lights were seen reflecting on the water
    B. a flock of birds flew right over our heads
    C. we caught a glimpse of several boats returning to the harbor
    D. the cool breeze made us feel completely refreshed

25. ______ repeatedly by the client about the delayed shipment, the support team finally escalated the issue.
    A. Having questioned
    B. Having been questioned
    C. Questioning
    D. To question

26. The CEO was overheard ______ that the company would postpone its international expansion plan.
    A. say
    B. to saying
    C. saying
    D. said

27. ______ the crucial deadline, the team had to work throughout the weekend to avoid financial penalties.
    A. Not having met
    B. Having not met
    C. Didn't meet
    D. Not met

28. I noticed from her tense expression ______ she was reluctant to accept the compromise.
    A. to be
    B. that
    C. being
    D. been

29. ______ from the outside, the abandoned factory looks completely silent, yet heavy machinery operates inside.
    A. Seeing
    B. Having seen
    C. Seen
    D. To see

30. The emergency exit was found ______ by heavy wooden crates, which posed a serious safety hazard.
    A. blocking
    B. to block
    C. blocked
    D. having blocked

31. ______ in a soundproof booth for six consecutive hours, ______.
    A. Working / the interpreter felt her concentration deteriorate
    B. Worked / the interpreter felt her concentration deteriorate
    C. Working / the interpreter's concentration began to deteriorate
    D. Having worked / it was hard for the interpreter to stay focused
