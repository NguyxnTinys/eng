## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Chủ ngữ & Tân ngữ)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 (Công thức & Quy tắc biến đổi) & Mục 3.1 (Làm chủ ngữ/bổ ngữ).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng Danh động từ (V-ing) thích hợp (lưu ý dạng phủ định nếu có `not` và quy tắc chính tả).**

1. ____________ (commute) long distances every morning often drains employees' energy before work even begins.
2. One effective way to prevent severe burnout is ____________ (allocate) at least thirty minutes daily for physical exercise.
3. The team leader suggested ____________ (not / postpone) the product review despite the minor technical glitch.
4. ____________ (lie) on a resume can permanently damage a candidate's professional reputation.
5. She admitted ____________ (panic) slightly when the server crashed ten minutes before the live presentation.
6. ____________ (not / sleep) well for several consecutive nights inevitably lowers your productivity at work.
7. My main responsibility this month is ____________ (coordinate) with overseas partners and ____________ (draft) the budget.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Động từ theo sau bởi V-ing & Sau giới từ (bao gồm `to` là giới từ)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.2 (Động từ + V-ing) & Mục 3.3 (Giới từ + V-ing, đặc biệt bẫy giới từ `to`).*

**Hoàn thành các câu sau bằng dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc.**

8. We are really looking forward to ____________ (negotiate) the final partnership agreement in person next Tuesday.
9. The former accountant finally confessed to ____________ (alter) the quarterly financial figures to hide the loss.
10. If you want to finish the report by 5 p.m., you must avoid ____________ (procrastinate) on minor formatting details.
11. Several local residents strongly objected to ____________ (convert) the public park into a commercial parking lot.
12. It took the new intern nearly a month to get accustomed to ____________ (collaborate) across different time zones.
13. Rather than ____________ (complain) about the tight schedule, she focused on ____________ (adapt) her workflow.
14. The supplier offered a 15% discount to compensate for ____________ (deliver) the raw materials three days late.
15. Do you mind ____________ (wait) outside for a moment while the committee finishes reviewing your application?

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi).*

**Chọn phương án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) cho mỗi câu sau.**

    A. you to spend  
    B. your spending  
    C. you spent  
    D. your to spend  

25. It's no good ____________ to convince him; he has already made up his mind to resign.
    A. try  
    B. to try  
    C. trying  
    D. to trying  

26. The HR manager suggested ____________ a short break every two hours to maintain concentration.
    A. taking  
    B. to take  
    C. us to take  
    D. take  

27. ____________ late at night and skipping breakfast ____________ harmful to your digestive system.
    A. Eating / is  
    B. Eat / are  
    C. Eating / are  
    D. To eating / is  

28. In addition to ____________ fluent in French, the candidate is committed to ____________ in rural communities.
    A. be / work  
    B. being / working  
    C. being / work  
    D. be / working  

29. Although the new software looks promising, it took our staff three weeks ____________ how to operate it smoothly.
    A. learning  
    B. to learn  
    C. learn  
    D. to learning  

30. I really couldn't resist ____________ a slice of that homemade chocolate cake, even though I'm on a diet.
    A. to have  
    B. have  
    C. having  
    D. to having  

31. She apologized for ____________ able to attend the workshop because of an unexpected family emergency.
    A. not being  
    B. being not
