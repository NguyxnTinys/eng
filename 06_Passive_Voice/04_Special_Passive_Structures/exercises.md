# Bài tập thực hành: Các cấu trúc Bị động đặc biệt (B2–C1)

**Tổng số câu:** 50 câu (chia làm 7 phần bám sát từng mảng lý thuyết).

---

### Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 (Công thức cơ bản cho 5 nhóm cấu trúc).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng bị động phù hợp để hoàn thành các câu sau.

1. It _______________ (estimate) that over two million commuters use the metro line every week.
2. Nobody enjoys _______________ (humiliate) in front of their teammates during a meeting.
3. Why didn't the logistics staff _______________ (reimburse) for their overnight meal costs?
4. The central heating system in this old dormitory needs _______________ (inspect) before winter arrives.
5. Were the newly hired engineers _______________ (grant) full access to the security **credentials** yesterday?
6. The board of directors refused _______________ (mislead) by incomplete financial figures.
7. How did the delivery van _______________ (get / stick) in the flooded tunnel during rush hour?

---

### Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi / Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & 4 (Bị động kép lệch thì, động từ 2 tân ngữ kèm `to/for`, `need/want/require + V-ing`, ngoại lệ `explain/let`).*

Hoàn thành câu bằng dạng đúng của động từ (hoặc thêm giới từ cần thiết nếu có).

8. The former treasurer is alleged _______________ (embezzle) public funds five years before the audit. *(dùng `embezzle`)*
9. Substantial grants were allocated _______________ rural hospitals to upgrade their emergency wards. *(điền giới từ thích hợp)*
10. The outdated IT infrastructure urgently requires _______________ (overhaul) to prevent data breaches.
11. Employees are strictly forbidden to enter the lab without _______________ (issue) temporary passes first.
12. The new safety protocol was clearly explained _______________ the night-shift supervisors yesterday. *(điền giới từ thích hợp)*
13. Factory apprentices shouldn't _______________ (make / work) overtime without extra pay.
14. Several historic villas along the boulevard are expected _______________ (renovate) by next autumn.
15. The suspect is rumoured _______________ (hide) in a remote mountain village at this very moment.

---

### Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Phân biệt `need + V-ing` vs `need + to V`, `to V` vs `to have V3`, `being V3` vs `having been V3`, `get + V3` vs `be + V3`).*

Chọn dạng đúng trong ngoặc đơn để phù hợp với ngữ cảnh của câu.

16. The rooftop solar panels need **(to clean / cleaning)** because heavy dust has reduced their efficiency.
17. Before leaving the site, the safety officer needs **(to inspect / inspecting)** every single fire exit.
18. The whistleblower is suspected **(to leak / to have leaked)** the confidential memo last month.
19. Although she rarely talks about it, she resented **(not being invited / not to be invited)** to the strategy retreat.
20. The CEO publicly acknowledged **(having been warned / to have warned)** about the supply chain risk weeks earlier.
21. Shakespeare's plays **(get widely known / are widely known)** across almost every continent.
22. Two freelance journalists **(got detained / were detaining)** at the checkpoint for carrying expired press passes.
23. These fragile glass crates need **(to handle / to be handled)** with extreme care during loading.

---

### Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Bẫy đề thi kinh điển).*

Chọn đáp án chính xác nhất (`A`, `B`, `C` hoặc `D`).

24. The missing artworks are believed _______ out of the country shortly after the museum closed in 2019.
    - A. to be smuggled
    - B. to have smuggled
    - C. to have been smuggled
    - D. being smuggled

25. _______ for the flight cancellation, the passengers were offered hotel vouchers and meal coupons.
    - A. To compensate
    - B. Having been compensated
    - C. Having compensated
    - D. Being compensating

26. The company's regional office is reported _______ to an industrial park outside the city next quarter.
    - A. to be relocated
    - B. to have relocated
    - C. relocating
    - D. being relocated

27. _______ when the new performance-based bonus scheme takes effect?
    - A. Got the staff promoted
    - B. Did the staff got promoted
    - C. Did the staff get promoted
    - D. Were the staff get promoted

28. Your winter coat looks rather dusty; it really wants _______.
    - A. dry-clean
    - B. being dry-cleaned
    - C. dry-cleaning
    - D. to dry-clean

29. During the crisis, small business owners feared _______ by predatory lenders.
    - A. to exploit
    - B. being exploited
    - C. exploiting
    - D. to have exploited

30. The scholarship _______ last Friday after the committee reviewed all applicants' academic **credentials**.
    - A. was awarded to Mai
    - B. awarded Mai
    - C. was awarded Mai
    - D. was awarding to Mai

31. Under the new labour law, interns are not allowed _______ unpaid overtime on public holidays.
    - A. to be worked
    - B. working
    - C. to work
    - D. being worked

---

### Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Các lỗi sai thường gặp).*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT** lỗi sai ngữ pháp. Hãy chỉ ra phần sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng.

32. The newly appointed interns were explained the safety procedures during Monday's orientation session.
33. Several abandoned warehouses near the harbour need being demolished before construction begins.
34. The former director is rumoured to emigrate to New Zealand two years ago to avoid prosecution.
35. Did the injured cyclist got compensated fairly by the insurance company after the accident?
36. Many seasonal workers complained about not informing about the sudden shift changes.
37. The neglected bridge requires to repair immediately if we want to prevent serious traffic accidents.
38. The suspect pretended to not be involved in the scheme, even though his login credentials were traced.

### Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp 5 nhóm cấu trúc bị động đặc biệt.*

Viết lại mỗi câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng gợi ý hoặc từ khóa trong ngoặc.

39. People allege that the pharmaceutical firm concealed negative clinical trial results.
    -> The pharmaceutical firm ____________________________________________________.

40. The ministry granted a special research permit to our university team. *(Bắt đầu bằng `Our university team`)*
    -> Our university team ______________________________________________________.

41. It is believed that the ancient manuscripts were neglected for centuries before their discovery.
    -> The ancient manuscripts ___________________________________________________.

42. Someone ought to overhaul the city's drainage system before the monsoon starts. *(Dùng `needs`)*
    -> The city's drainage system ________________________________________________.

43. Nobody likes it when people prosecute them unfairly based on unverified rumours. *(Dùng `being`)*
    -> Nobody likes ____________________________________________________________.

44. Investigators suspect that the accountant was working with an outside hacker at that time.
    -> The accountant __________________________________________________________.

### Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 5 & 6 (Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt & Ứng dụng đời sống).*

#### A. Điền dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc để hoàn thành đoạn email/báo cáo nội bộ (Câu 45–47)

> **Subject:** Post-Audit Action Plan
>
> Dear Team,
>
> Following last week's external audit, our department's archiving software **(45)** _______________ outdated and urgently needs **(46)** _______________. In addition, extra funding will be **(47)** _______________ to the compliance unit so that staff can verify client **credentials** more accurately and avoid **being misled** by forged documents.

45. (45) _______________ (`acknowledge / be`)
46. (46) _______________ (`upgrade`)
47. (47) _______________ (`allocate`)

#### B. Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh, áp dụng cấu trúc bị động đặc biệt (Câu 48–50)

48. Người ta ước tính rằng khoảng 200 công nhân có nguy cơ bị bóc lột hoặc không được hoàn trả tiền làm thêm giờ. *(Gợi ý: `estimate`, `risk`, `exploit`, `reimburse`)*
49. Giám đốc điều hành bị cáo buộc là đã làm bẽ mặt một nhân viên cấp dưới ngay trước khi ông ấy bị điều chuyển sang chi nhánh khác. *(Gợi ý: `allege`, `humiliate`, `relocate`)*
50. Sau khi máy điều hòa được kiểm tra và sửa chữa xong, cô ấy cuối cùng cũng được thăng chức lên trưởng nhóm vận hành. *(Gợi ý: `need/require` hoặc `get + V3`, `inspect`, `promote`)*
