# Bài tập Thực hành: Câu Bị động (Hoàn thành, Tương lai & Khuyết thiếu) (B1–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn**: Hoàn thành đầy đủ 50 câu hỏi dưới đây (từ Part 1 đến Part 7). Đọc kỹ phần nhắc lại lý thuyết ở đầu mỗi phần trước khi làm bài.

---

## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn).*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng Bị động thích hợp theo thì / động từ khuyết thiếu được gợi ý hoặc ngữ cảnh rõ ràng.**

1. Since the beginning of the year, three new policies ________________________ (implement) to protect user privacy.
2. When we arrived at the museum, we were told that the historic wing ________________________ (renovate) the previous month.
3. All damaged goods ________________________ (compensate) by the shipping company next week.
4. Look at the official announcement on the board! The final exam ________________________ (going to / postpone) until Monday.
5. By the end of this month, the new security software ________________________ (install) on every employee's laptop.
6. Confidential files ________________________ (must / not / leave) unattended on your desk at any time.
7. ________________________ the revised proposal ________________________ (submit) to the director yet?

## Part 2: Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt (Câu 8–15)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Cách dùng chi tiết & Mục 2.2–2.3 — Câu hỏi bị động, Trạng từ, Phrasal verbs.*

**Điền dạng bị động đúng của động từ trong ngoặc (chú ý vị trí trạng từ, giới từ và động từ khuyết thiếu quá khứ).**

8. The technical issue ________________________ (already / resolve) before the morning shift started.
9. Why ________________________ the residents ________________________ (not / evacuate) before the floodwaters reached the town?
10. These safety checks ________________________ (should / carry out) last week, not today! Now it's too late.
11. The broken window suggests that the warehouse ________________________ (must / break into) late last night.
12. Those extra chairs ________________________ (need not / buy)—twenty seats had already been reserved for our guests!
13. Who ________________________ this urgent transaction ________________________ (will / authorize) by?
14. Several complaints about customer service ________________________ (look into) by the management team recently.
15. The guidelines ________________________ (ought to / clarify) during yesterday's briefing so that nobody felt confused.

## Part 3: Phân biệt Ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & 4.1 — Phân biệt Chủ động vs Bị động và các mốc thời gian.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng Chủ động hoặc Bị động cho đúng với ngữ cảnh và dấu hiệu thời gian.**

16. By the time the inspector gets here tomorrow afternoon, all mandatory safety checks ________________________ (complete).
17. Don't panic! A similar system error ________________________ (occur) twice last year, and our IT team fixed it quickly.
18. Once your identity ________________________ (verify), your suspended account will be reactivated automatically.
19. By 2020, more than 60% of the region's transport infrastructure ________________________ (modernize).
20. The preliminary results ________________________ (may / publish) tomorrow, depending on whether the lab finishes the tests tonight.
21. That email ________________________ (can't / send) by our bank—it is clearly a phishing attempt!
22. Extra funds ________________________ (have to / allocate) to the healthcare sector in the upcoming budget; otherwise, hospitals will face shortages.
23. Look! The stage lights are flickering and the crew is packing up—the outdoor concert ________________________ (going to / call off).

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm Chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 — Các bẫy đề thi kinh điển (B1–C1).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) để hoàn thành mỗi câu sau.**

24. By the time the new director takes office in July, the restructuring plan ________.
    A. will be finalized
    B. will have been finalized
    C. has been finalized
    D. is going to finalize

25. Due to human negligence, the confidential database ________ during last night's cyberattack.
    A. might have been compromised
    B. might be compromised
    C. might have compromised
    D. should be compromised

26. A major power outage ________ across the industrial zone just before midnight yesterday.
    A. was occurred
    B. has been occurred
    C. occurred
    D. had been occurred

27. ________ will the allocated funds be distributed to the local communities?
    A. By who
    B. Whom
    C. By whom
    D. Who by

28. The construction project ________ finished by now, yet the workers haven't even laid the foundation.
    A. should have been
    B. must have been
    C. needn't have been
    D. will have been

29. All applicants will receive an email confirmation as soon as their documents ________.
    A. will be verified
    B. will have been verified
    C. have been verified
    D. had been verified

30. The emergency exit ________ blocked; that's why the inspector issued a heavy fine.
    A. shouldn't have been
    B. couldn't have been
    C. needn't have been
    D. wouldn't be

31. Up to now, no clear explanation ________ for why the satellite connection was suddenly suspended.
    A. has been being given
    B. has been given
    C. had been given
    D. was given

## Part 5: Tìm và Sửa lỗi sai (Câu 32–38)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 — Những lỗi sai người Việt thường mắc phải.*

**Mỗi câu sau có 4 phần được đánh dấu [A], [B], [C], [D]. Hãy xác định phần chứa lỗi sai và viết lại cho đúng.**

32. By next Friday, [A] **all the preliminary reports** [B] **will be reviewed** [C] **and signed** [D] **by the committee**.
33. The stolen paintings [A] **must have stolen** [B] **by someone** [C] **who knew** the museum's [D] **security layout**.
34. [A] **Has** the new software [B] **been install** on all computers [C] **before** the training session [D] **starts**?
35. [A] **It's a pity** that the fire alarm [B] **wasn't tested** earlier; [C] **lives** [D] **could be saved** during last week's fire.
36. [A] **How many** proposals [B] **have been being submitted** [C] **since** the competition [D] **was announced**?
37. You [A] **didn't need to be paid** the registration fee in advance, [B] **so I'm glad** [C] **you waited** until [D] **the rules were clarified**. *(Lưu ý kiểm tra đúng lỗi ngữ pháp nếu có, hoặc xem xét ngữ nghĩa bị động tại [A])*
38. [A] **Had** the passengers [B] **compensated** for the cancelled flight [C] **before** they [D] **left** the airport?
