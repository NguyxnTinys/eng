# Bài tập thực hành: Mạo từ A, An, The & Mạo từ rỗng (A1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài). Kiểm tra phần `answers.md` sau khi làm xong để đối chiếu đáp án và phân tích bẫy ngữ pháp.

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Quy tắc `a / an` theo âm
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức (Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn) & Quy tắc chọn `a/an` theo âm đầu tiên.*

Điền **`a`**, **`an`**, **`the`** hoặc **`Ø`** (nếu không cần mạo từ) vào chỗ trống.

1. It took us nearly ______ hour to reach the conference hall because of heavy traffic.
2. Is she applying to ______ university in the United Kingdom to study economics?
3. That was not ______ honorable decision, even though the committee tried to justify it.
4. Does the research team consider climate change ______ universal threat to coastal cities?
5. She doesn't want to miss ______ unique opportunity to present her findings at the summit.
6. Because of the fracture in her wrist, the doctor ordered ______ X-ray immediately.
7. Turn left at the corner because this is ______ one-way street during rush hours.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Mạo từ rỗng (Zero Article)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.3 — Zero Article (bữa ăn, môn học, thể thao, phương tiện `by bus`, chức năng vs. tòa nhà).*

Chọn phương án đúng trong ngoặc đơn.

16. Many office workers choose to commute **(by bus / by the bus)** to reduce urban emissions.
17. Yesterday I left my umbrella **(on bus / on the bus)** while rushing to the meeting.
18. We usually have **(breakfast / a breakfast)** at 7 a.m., unless we have an early flight.
19. The hotel chef prepared **(delicious breakfast / a delicious breakfast)** for all the international guests.
20. At this prestigious institution, **(mathematics / the mathematics)** is compulsory in the national curriculum.
21. After the car accident, he remained **(in hospital / in the hospital)** for three weeks for rehabilitation.
22. Every Sunday afternoon, her relatives visit her at **(hospital / the hospital)** near the central park.
23. Although he loves playing **(football / the football)** in a local tournament, his sister prefers playing **(violin / the violin)**. *(Chọn 2 vị trí)*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A, B, C, D (Bẫy đề thi B1–C1)

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (`A`, `B`, `C` hoặc `D`).

24. Protecting marine ______ is essential if we want coastal ecosystems to recover.
    - A. a biodiversity
    - B. the biodiversity
    - C. biodiversity
    - D. an biodiversity

25. To distinguish between facts and opinions, the delegates finally reached ______ consensus on carbon taxes.
    - A. a
    - B. an
    - C. the
    - D. Ø

26. ______ public infrastructure develops, the faster local startups emerge as regional leaders.
    - A. Better
    - B. A better
    - C. The better
    - D. Best

    - A. Ø
    - B. an
    - C. a
    - D. the

28. ______ Lake Baikal in ______ Siberia is the deepest freshwater lake in the world.
    - A. The / Ø
    - B. Ø / Ø
    - C. Ø / the
    - D. The / the

29. ______ people believe that technological progress is an inevitable consequence of globalization.
    - A. The most
    - B. Most of
    - C. Most
    - D. A most

30. She gave ______ honest explanation of why air quality began to deteriorate in the industrial zone.
    - A. a
    - B. an
    - C. the
    - D. Ø

31. While my grandfather still listens to ______ radio every morning, I rarely watch ______ television.
    - A. the / Ø
    - B. Ø / the

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)

36. **(A) The curriculum** at that **(B) prestigious** institution teaches students to distinguish **(C) the history** from **(D) mythology**.
37. Even though he traveled to work **(A) by the train** for **(B) nearly** **(C) a** decade, he never missed **(D) an** important deadline.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc `the` với so sánh nhất, `the + Adj`, so sánh kép và chức năng tổ chức xã hội.*

Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng đúng quy tắc mạo từ.

39. No other desert in the world is larger than the Sahara.
    → The Sahara is ______________________________________________________.

40. Elderly people often need better healthcare services in rural areas. *(Dùng cấu trúc `The + Adj`)*
    → ____________________________________________________________________.

41. As public infrastructure becomes more modern, the city attracts more investors. *(Dùng cấu trúc `The..., the...`)*
    → ____________________________________________________________________.

42. He was admitted to the medical center for surgery and rehabilitation. *(Dùng cụm `in/to hospital` chỉ chức năng)*
    → He went ____________________________________________________________.

43. She has the ability to play the cello very skillfully. *(Dùng `can play`)*
    → ____________________________________________________________________.

44. Nobody had ever achieved such an unprecedented breakthrough before Dr. Minh did. *(Dùng `the first`)*
    → Dr. Minh was _______________________________________________________.
