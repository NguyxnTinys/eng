## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Điền dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 (Công thức cơ bản: Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn & Vai trò hạn định từ / đại từ).*

Điền **một từ thích hợp** vào mỗi chỗ trống (`each`, `every`, `both`, `either`, `neither`, `all`, `whole`, `no`, `none`, `another`, `other`).

1. **__________** participant in tomorrow's workshop must submit a registration form in advance.
2. There are two equally viable alternatives, so you can choose **__________** option you prefer.
3. I reviewed **__________** of the two proposals carefully, and **__________** one meets our mandatory criteria. *(Điền 1 cặp từ phù hợp cho 2 chỗ trống, hoặc 2 từ phân cách bằng dấu `/`)*
4. **__________** consensus was reached after three hours of debate, so the committee adjourned the meeting.
5. Do **__________** of the applicants have the required experience to collaborate with our overseas partners?
6. We spent the **__________** afternoon trying to comprehend the updated tax regulations.
7. Five experts assessed the prototype, yet **__________** of them found a serious flaw in its design.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Hòa hợp Chủ–Vị & Cấu trúc `+ of` / Quy tắc biến đổi
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.1 & 2.2 (Quy tắc `+ of`, chia động từ số ít/số nhiều, danh từ đếm được/không đếm được).*

Chọn hoặc chia dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc (hoặc thêm `of` / `one of` khi cần thiết).

8. Each of the applicants **__________** (be) interviewed individually by the panel yesterday.
9. Neither the project manager nor the team leaders **__________** (have) agreed to implement the policy yet.
10. Almost **__________** (every / each) obstacle we encountered last quarter has now been resolved.
11. **__________** (Every / Every one) of the committee members signed the final agreement.
12. All of the funding allocated to the rural schools **__________** (be) distributed transparently last month.
13. Neither criterion **__________** (seem) flexible enough to cover exceptional cases.
14. Both of **__________** (we) believe that the training program is mutually beneficial.
15. The committee will require **__________** (another / other) three weeks to evaluate the remaining submissions.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 (Phân biệt `each` vs `every`, `all` vs `whole`, `other`/`another`/`the other`/`others`/`the others`, `none` vs `no`/`neither`).*

Chọn từ đúng nhất trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu theo đúng ngữ cảnh.

16. The audit team visits our regional office **__________** (each / every) two months to inspect the records.
17. Two candidates were shortlisted for the director role, and **__________** (neither / none) had previously worked abroad.
18. Some participants prefer to tackle complex tasks individually, whereas **__________** (other / others / the others) thrive when they collaborate.
19. Ten applications arrived before the deadline: four were rejected immediately, and **__________** (others / the others / another) were forwarded to the committee.
20. **__________** (All / The whole) city was paralyzed by the sudden transport strike yesterday.
21. She held a heavy folder in **__________** (each / every) hand as she entered the boardroom.
22. Is there any **__________** (other / another / others) alternative we can implement if the budget is cut?
23. "How many eligible candidates showed up for the briefing?" — "**__________** (No / None / Neither). All six of them requested to reschedule."

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 (Các lỗi sai kinh điển của người Việt).*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** liên quan đến từ phân phối hoặc sự hòa hợp ngữ pháp đi kèm. Hãy chỉ ra phần sai và viết lại cho đúng.

32. `Every of the candidates` had to `submit` a `detailed portfolio` before the `mandatory deadline`.
33. `Both of the applicants` `did not meet` the `language criterion`, so the HR team `had to` reopen the vacancy. *(Ngữ cảnh: Cả hai ứng viên đều trượt tiêu chí ngôn ngữ)*
34. Could you please `provide` `an another` example so that `all participants` can `comprehend` the rule?
35. `Whole the committee` spent `over four hours` trying to `reach a consensus` on how to `allocate` the grant.
36. `Many others participants` complained that the `assessment process` was `neither transparent` `nor fair`.
37. `Every criteria` in this `evaluation sheet` `carries` equal weight, with `no exception`.
38. `Each of the five departments` `have submitted` their `quarterly budget` to `the finance director`.
