## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Điền lượng từ cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Quy tắc kết hợp với danh từ đếm được / không đếm được và câu (+), (−), (?).*

**Điền một lượng từ phù hợp vào chỗ trống từ nhóm gợi ý: `some`, `any`, `much`, `many`, `a lot of`.**

1. The company plans to allocate __________ funds to upgrade the office next month.
2. We don't need to buy __________ new equipment because the old machines still work well.
3. How __________ luggage are you planning to take on your business trip to Singapore?
4. There are __________ urgent tasks on my desk this morning, so I can't join you for lunch.
5. Did the manager receive __________ complaints from customers about the delayed delivery?
6. She didn't get __________ feedback on her presentation, only a brief email from the director.
7. How __________ colleagues attended the workshop on time management yesterday?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng, Ngoại lệ & Quy tắc vị trí
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Ngoại lệ của `some`/`any`, vị trí `enough`, và phân biệt `too much`/`too many`.*

**Hoàn thành các câu sau bằng cách chọn từ/cụm từ đúng trong ngoặc.**

8. Would you like (some / any) tea while you wait for the HR manager?
9. (Some / Any) employee who misses the safety training must attend the session next Friday.
10. There are (hardly some / hardly any) funds left in the marketing department's account.
11. The meeting room isn't (enough large / large enough) to hold all thirty participants.
12. Modern households often consume (too much / too many) electricity during peak summer hours.
13. I couldn't finish the report because my supervisor gave me (too much / too many) deadlines at once.
14. Could you lend me (some / any) money until payday? I forgot my wallet at home.
15. Do we have (enough time / time enough) to check whether the data is (adequate enough / enough adequate)?

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Cặp dễ nhầm
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & 4 — `a few`/`few`, `a little`/`little`, `most`/`most of`, `plenty of`, `a lot` vs. `a lot of`.*

**Điền dạng đúng phù hợp với ngữ cảnh vào chỗ trống.**

17. We have __________ (`a little / little`) budget remaining—definitely not enough to hire an outside consultant.
18. Because of the power outage, __________ (`a few / few`) workers could finish their tasks on schedule, so productivity dropped sharply.
19. Don't worry, I still have __________ (`a few / few`) good ideas we can pitch at tomorrow's meeting.
20. __________ (`Most / Most of`) the colleagues in my branch felt overwhelmed when the system crashed.
21. __________ (`Most / Most of`) people prefer flexible working hours to a rigid nine-to-five routine.
22. Take your time checking the contract; we have __________ (`plenty of / too much`) time before the courier arrives.
23. __________ (`Quite a few / Few`) consumers now avoid disposable plastic and choose sustainable alternatives instead.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–B2)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Dấu hiệu nhận biết, danh từ bất quy tắc/không đếm được và bẫy đề thi.*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).**

24. We had to cancel the outdoor event because __________ of the sound equipment __________ damaged by the storm.
    - A. many / were
    - B. much / was
    - C. a few / were
    - D. several / was

25. It is difficult to find affordable __________ in the city centre without __________ help from a local agent.
    - A. accommodations / some
    - B. accommodation / any
    - C. accommodations / any
    - D. accommodation / many

26. You shouldn't pack __________ luggage; the airline charges a hefty fee if your bag is __________ heavy.
    - A. too many / too much
    - B. too much / too much
    - C. too much / much too
    - D. so many / much too

27. While there is __________ data available online, experts warn of a growing __________ of clean water in arid regions.
    - A. an abundance of / scarcity
    - B. many / scarcity
    - C. plenty / shortage
    - D. a few of / shortage

28. __________ workers in the factory were exhausted after working overtime for three consecutive weeks.
    - A. Almost
    - B. Most of
    - C. Almost all of the
    - D. The most

29. Is our current supply __________ to cover the winter demand, or do we need to import more?
    - A. enough adequate
    - B. adequate enough
    - C. too adequate
    - D. plenty adequate

30. The new curriculum puts __________ emphasis on rote memorization and __________ attention to critical thinking.
    - A. too many / too little
    - B. too much / too few
    - C. too much / too little
    - D. much too / very little

31. Fortunately, the customer service team received __________ complaints this quarter compared to last year.
    - A. very few
    - B. very little
    - C. much less
    - D. too many
