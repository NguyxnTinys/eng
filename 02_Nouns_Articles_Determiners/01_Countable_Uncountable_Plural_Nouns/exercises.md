# Bài tập thực hành: Danh từ đếm được, không đếm được & Số nhiều (A1–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây theo 7 phần. Hãy chú ý ngữ cảnh, quy tắc biến đổi số nhiều, sự hòa hợp chủ ngữ – động từ và các bẫy từ không đếm được. Đáp án và giải thích chi tiết nằm ở file `answers.md`.

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc cơ bản (Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn với a/an, some/any, much/many, is/are).*

**Điền từ thích hợp trong ngoặc vào chỗ trống để hoàn thành câu đúng ngữ pháp.**

1. Excuse me, is there __________ *(a / an / any)* affordable accommodation near the train station?
2. We don't have __________ *(many / much / a)* time left before the boarding gate closes.
3. How __________ *(much / many)* luggage are you planning to take on your trip to Da Nang?
4. There __________ *(is / are)* some fresh fruit and two bottles of juice in the refrigerator.
5. She didn't ask for __________ *(a / some / any)* permission before publishing the survey results.
6. How __________ *(much / many)* students signed up for the economics seminar this semester?
7. Look! There __________ *(is / are)* a lot of modern equipment in the newly built laboratory.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Quy tắc biến đổi Số nhiều & Ngoại lệ

**Viết dạng SỐ NHIỀU đúng của danh từ trong ngoặc (giữ nguyên nếu danh từ không đổi hình thái).**

9. The chefs sharpened all their kitchen __________ *(knife)* before preparing the banquet.
10. Our biology professor showed us photos of two endangered __________ *(species)* of mountain __________ *(deer)*.
11. The HR department relies on three strict __________ *(criterion)* when evaluating job candidates.
12. All __________ *(roof)* in the historic town were damaged during last night's storm, and many __________ *(leaf)* covered the streets.
13. The economic __________ *(crisis)* of 2008 and 2020 taught policymakers valuable lessons.
14. Both __________ *(analysis)* submitted by the research team pointed to the exact same conclusion.
15. Two __________ *(mouse)* ran past the wooden boxes where the __________ *(child)* kept their toys.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng 1, 2, 3 & Bẫy đề thi (luôn số nhiều, hình thức số nhiều nghĩa số ít, không đếm được kinh điển).*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).**

24. The police __________ currently searching the __________ of the city for the missing suspect.
    - A. is / outskirt
    - B. are / outskirts
    - C. is / outskirts
    - D. are / outskirt

25. Official statistics __________ that youth unemployment has dropped, even though statistics __________ considered a tough university course.
    - A. show / is
    - B. shows / are
    - C. show / are
    - D. shows / is

26. Every passenger is advised to keep their personal __________ safe in unfamiliar __________.
    - A. belonging / surrounding
    - B. belongings / surrounding
    - C. belonging / surroundings
    - D. belongings / surroundings

27. __________ significant progress in the project, the engineers still lacked __________ clear evidence to prove their theory.
    - A. Despite making / a
    - B. Despite making / any
    - C. Although they made / many
    - D. Because of making / an

28. This pair of scissors __________ blunt; could you hand me __________ sharp ones from the drawer?
    - A. is / a
    - B. are / some
    - C. is / the
    - D. are / that

29. A large number of researchers __________ ready to conduct research, while the number of funding grants __________ surprisingly low.
    - A. are / is
    - B. is / are
    - C. are / are
    - D. is / is

30. The breathtaking __________ in Ha Giang attracted tourists, even though heavy __________ was still clearing the mountain roads.
    - A. sceneries / machineries
    - B. scenery / machineries
    - C. scenery / machinery
    - D. sceneries / machinery

31. No __________ how carefully the news __________ verified, rumors still spread rapidly online.
    - A. matter / were
    - B. matter / was
    - C. problem / were
    - D. problem / was
