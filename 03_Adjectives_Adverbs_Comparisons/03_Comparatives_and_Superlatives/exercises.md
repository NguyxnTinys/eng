# Bài tập: So sánh Bằng, Hơn, Nhất & Bất quy tắc (A1–B2)

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.1 Công thức cơ bản (Bằng – Hơn – Nhất) & Phân biệt Tính từ / Trạng từ.*

Chia dạng đúng của tính từ hoặc trạng từ trong ngoặc để hoàn thành các câu sau:

1. This metro line is much __________ (convenient) than driving during rush hour.
2. Is the new apartment as __________ (spacious) as the one you rented last year?
3. Our team didn't work so __________ (efficiently) as we had expected during the launch.
4. Which is __________ (high) mountain in Southeast Asia?
5. My daily commute isn't as __________ (long) as yours, because I live near the station.
6. Does this software run __________ (fast) than the previous version?
7. Among all the candidates, Minh spoke __________ (fluently) in the final interview.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Quy tắc biến đổi & Dạng Bất quy tắc
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.2 Quy tắc biến đổi (tính từ 2 âm tiết `-y/-er/-ow/-le`, trạng từ `-ly`) & Mục 2.3 Bảng bất quy tắc (`good/well`, `bad/badly`, `far`, `little`, `much/many`, `elder/older`).*

Chia dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc (áp dụng đúng quy tắc biến đổi hoặc bất quy tắc):

8. The instructions in this manual are __________ (simple) than the old ones.
9. Could you speak a bit __________ (quietly)? The baby is sleeping in the next room.
10. The street gets __________ (narrow) as you drive toward the old quarter.
11. If you need __________ (far) information about the affordable housing program, please contact our office.
12. Honestly, my cooking is bad, and my brother's is even __________ (bad).
13. My __________ (old) sister works as a senior architect in Singapore.
14. Today I feel much __________ (well) than I did yesterday, so I can attend the meeting.
15. We have __________ (little) time left than we thought, so let's prioritize the main tasks.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt cấu trúc So sánh bằng & So sánh nhất với `in` / `of`
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 Cách dùng chi tiết (`as...as`, `not so/as...as`, `the same...as`, `in` vs `of`).*

Điền **`as`**, **`so`**, **`than`**, **`the same`**, **`in`**, hoặc **`of`** vào mỗi chỗ trống sau (một số câu có thể có hai đáp án đúng):

16. This laptop costs __________ price as the one on display, yet its battery lasts longer.
17. No other river __________ the world is as long as the Nile, though the Amazon carries more water.
18. That was the most demanding project __________ all the assignments we completed this year.
19. Reading a brief summary is not __________ helpful as reading the full research paper.
20. Your pronunciation is much clearer __________ it was six months ago — you have really made progress.
21. Who is the most reliable member __________ your department?
22. He arrived at __________ time as his colleagues despite the heavy rain.
23. She is the most fluent speaker __________ the five finalists.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):

24. Between the two proposals, the board agreed that the second one is __________.
    - A. the more practical
    - B. the most practical
    - C. more practical than
    - D. most practical

25. Even though both phones have comparable features, the silver model is __________ more affordable.
    - A. very
    - B. substantially
    - C. so
    - D. many

26. Nobody in the workshop handles delicate instruments __________ than Mr. Tuan.
    - A. more carefully
    - B. careful
    - C. as carefully
    - D. most carefully

27. My hometown is __________ from the capital city than Da Nang is.
    - A. further
    - B. farther
    - C. the farthest
    - D. farer

28. This year's sales figures are __________ as last year's, despite the economic slowdown.
    - A. the same
    - B. as same
    - C. same
    - D. the same like

29. He works __________ than anyone else on the competitive sales team, yet he rarely complains.
    - A. more hardly
    - B. harder
    - C. hardest
    - D. more hard

30. Of the three candidates, Lan performed __________ under pressure.
    - A. good
    - B. better
    - C. the best
    - D. well

31. The productivity of the morning shift is significantly higher than __________ of the night shift.
    - A. those
    - B. this
    - C. it
    - D. that

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 Bẫy đề thi kinh điển & Lỗi sai thường gặp.*

Mỗi câu sau có **1 phần gạch chân bị sai** (A, B, C hoặc D). Hãy chọn chữ cái tương ứng và **viết lại phương án sửa đúng**:

32. Although the hotel room was **<u>more quieter</u>** (A) than we **<u>had expected</u>** (B), its location was not **<u>as convenient</u>** (C) as the brochure **<u>claimed</u>** (D).
33. My **<u>elder</u>** (A) brother is three years **<u>older</u>** (B) than me, **<u>and</u>** (C) he is also **<u>more taller</u>** (D) than our father.
34. This complicated software **<u>costs</u>** (A) **<u>the same</u>** (B) amount of money **<u>like</u>** (C) the basic version, **<u>which</u>** (D) surprised everyone.
35. **<u>In</u>** (A) all the cities I have **<u>ever visited</u>** (B), Tokyo has **<u>the cleanest</u>** (C) and most reliable public **<u>transport</u>** (D).
36. She speaks English **<u>much</u>** (A) **<u>more fluent</u>** (B) now **<u>than</u>** (C) she did before moving **<u>abroad</u>** (D).
37. Wait until we gather **<u>farther</u>** (A) details before **<u>making</u>** (B) a final decision, as the **<u>current</u>** (C) data seems **<u>incomplete</u>** (D).
38. There are **<u>less</u>** (A) opportunities for **<u>remote</u>** (B) work in this industry **<u>than</u>** (C) there were two years **<u>ago</u>** (D).
