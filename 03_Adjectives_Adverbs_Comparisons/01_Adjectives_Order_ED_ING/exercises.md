## Part 1: Nhận diện vị trí & Điền dạng tính từ cơ bản (Câu 1–7)

**Chọn dạng từ đúng hoặc sắp xếp cụm từ trong ngoặc vào vị trí chính xác để hoàn thành các câu sau:**

6. The instructions for the new machine weren't clear, so the trainees felt ____________. (`frustrate / frustrating / frustrated`)
7. Is there ____________ who can fix this **state-of-the-art** projector before the seminar starts? (`capable anyone / anyone capable`)

## Part 3: Phân biệt ngữ cảnh `-ed` vs `-ing`, `the + adj` & Tính từ đặc biệt (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.3, Mục 3.5 & Mục 4 (Ngoại lệ linking verbs, nhóm `a-`, `the + adj`).*

**Điền dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc hoặc chọn phương án phù hợp nhất với ngữ cảnh:**

17. Many newly hired graduates feel ____________ (`overwhelm / overwhelming / overwhelmed`) by the ____________ (`overwhelm / overwhelming / overwhelmed`) amount of technical documentation.
18. Everyone in the audience was ____________ (`intrigue / intriguing / intrigued`) by the journalist's ____________ (`compel / compelling / compelled`) evidence.
19. The security guard found the two children fast ____________ (`sleep / sleeping / asleep`) in the waiting lounge.
20. In many developed nations, **the elderly** ____________ (`is / are`) entitled to free annual health check-ups.
21. The new community center offers free vocational workshops to help **the unemployed** ____________ (`develop / develops`) digital skills.
22. Look at that puppy! It looks so ____________ (`love / lovely / lovelily`) in its little knitted sweater.
23. Even though the mountain hike was ____________ (`exhaust / exhausting / exhausted`), the breathtaking view at the summit made everyone ____________ (`delight / delightful / delighted`).
