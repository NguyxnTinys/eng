# Bài tập thực hành: Too...to, Enough...to, So...that, Such...that (A2–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Làm trọn vẹn cả **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây theo 7 dạng bài từ cơ bản đến nâng cao. Sau khi làm xong, đối chiếu và đọc giải thích chi tiết trong file `answers.md`.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn).*

**Điền MỘT từ thích hợp (`too`, `enough`, `so`, hoặc `such`) vào mỗi chỗ trống sau:**

1. The **deadline** is ________ **tight** for the team to finish the entire report tonight without making errors.
2. Are you sure our department has ________ **budget** to **negotiate** a contract with an overseas supplier?
3. She was ________ **exhausted** after a twelve-hour shift that she fell asleep on the train during her **commute**.
4. It was ________ an **overwhelming** amount of data that even the senior analyst couldn't process it quickly.
5. Why didn't the candidate get the promotion? — He isn't **qualified** ________ to lead a **sophisticated** engineering project yet.
6. Were there ________ **reliable** volunteers to handle the charity event on such short notice?
7. How could you make ________ a **crucial** decision without consulting the legal team first?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng, Vị trí & Cấu trúc mở rộng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết, vị trí `enough`, `for sb to V`, lượng từ `so` và đảo ngữ `So/Such`.*

**Sắp xếp và chia dạng đúng của các từ gợi ý trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu:**

8. This **affordable** apartment isn't ______________________________ in with two growing children. *(spacious / enough / for / us / live)*
9. The presenter gave ______________________________ that even **reluctant** investors agreed to fund the startup. *(persuasive / such / a / speech)*
10. There were ______________________________ in the first draft that we had to **postpone** the product launch. *(so / many / technical / error)*
11. The project manager had ______________________________ left before the **deadline** that he couldn't **concentrate** on details. *(little / so / time)*
12. Because of **sufficient** preparation, the system wasn't ______________________________ during peak traffic. *(vulnerable / too / crash)*
13. ______________________________ that several interns felt completely **exhausted** by Friday afternoon. *(so / tremendous / was / pressure / the)*
14. ______________________________ that nobody in the office could work **efficiently**. *(such / was / tight / schedule / the)*
15. Do we have ______________________________ a final decision today? *(enough / information / for / the / committee / make)*

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết & Phân biệt (`too` vs `very`, `so` vs `such`, vị trí `enough`).*

**Chọn phương án đúng trong ngoặc dựa vào ngữ cảnh và dấu hiệu ngữ pháp:**

16. The traffic during the morning **commute** is `(too / very / enough)` heavy, yet I still manage to arrive at the office on time.
17. The startup's **budget** is `(too / very / enough)` **tight** to hire a full-time marketing director right now.
18. Thanks to the new automation tool, the staff can process invoices `(efficiently enough / enough efficiently / too efficiently)` to meet every **deadline**.
19. We received `(so / such / very)` **overwhelming** feedback from clients that we decided to expand the service.
20. There is `(so much / such many / so many)` **vulnerable** personal data online that cybersecurity has become **crucial**.
21. She spoke `(so / such / too)` softly that the audience in the back row could barely hear her **persuasive** arguments.
22. Don't **hesitate** to ask for help if the workload becomes `(too / such / enough)` heavy for you to handle alone.
23. They had `(such a lot of / so much / too)` **reliable** suppliers that a single delivery delay caused no **consequence** at all.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (B1–C1)

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi & Liên hệ tư duy Tiếng Việt (Vietlish).*

**Mỗi câu có 4 cụm từ được đánh dấu (A), (B), (C), (D). Hãy xác định chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và viết lại cho đúng:**

32. The coffee was **(A) too hot** **(B) for me** **(C) to drink** **(D) it** right away.
33. She didn't feel **(A) enough confident** **(B) to give** a **(C) persuasive** presentation in front of **(D) the board**.
34. It was **(A) such a** **(B) tremendous** news **(C) that** everyone started cheering **(D) immediately**.
35. They had **(A) such many** **(B) urgent tasks** before the **(C) deadline** that they couldn't **(D) take a break**.
36. Thank you **(A) too much** **(B) for your** **(C) reliable** support during this **(D) overwhelming** week.
37. The mountain trail was **(A) very steep** **(B) that** several **(C) exhausted** hikers decided **(D) to turn back**.
38. The new server is **(A) too** **(B) vulnerable** **(C) that** hackers could access **(D) sufficient** customer data easily.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Chuyển đổi tương đương giữa `too...to`, `enough...to`, `so...that`, `such...that` và Đảo ngữ.*

**Hoàn thành câu thứ hai sao cho nghĩa không đổi so với câu ban đầu:**

39. The **commute** was **too** exhausting for Anna to **concentrate** on her evening class.
    → The **commute** was **so** __________________________________________________.

40. The hotel room wasn't **affordable enough** for us to book for a whole week. *(Dùng `too expensive`)*
    → The hotel room was __________________________________________________.

41. They faced **so many** **overwhelming** obstacles that they had to **postpone** the launch.
    → They faced **such** __________________________________________________.

42. The speaker's argument was **so persuasive** that even **reluctant** members voted yes.
    → It was **such** __________________________________________________.

43. The **consequence** of ignoring cybersecurity was **so serious** that the system became **vulnerable**. *(Dùng đảo ngữ)*
    → **So** __________________________________________________.

44. The storm caused **such tremendous** damage that flights could not operate **efficiently**. *(Dùng đảo ngữ)*
    → **Such** __________________________________________________.

### B. Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 48–50)
