# Bài tập thực hành: Nghĩa vụ, Cấm đoán & Lời khuyên (50 câu)

> [!NOTE]
> Hoàn thành đầy đủ 50 câu dưới đây theo đúng hướng dẫn từng phần. Không xem file đáp án trước khi làm xong.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức cơ bản của `must`, `have to`, `should`, `ought to`, `had better` & `need`.*

**Điền dạng đúng của động từ khuyết thiếu hoặc cụm từ trong ngoặc vào chỗ trống.**

1. All staff members __________ (must / adhere) to the updated safety protocol starting Monday.
2. __________ employees __________ (have to / submit) a medical certificate if they are absent for only one day?
3. You __________ (had better / not / neglect) your health while preparing for the final exams.
4. Everyone __________ (ought / acknowledge) the effort that the volunteers put into this project.
5. __________ we __________ (need / consult) the legal department before signing this temporary contract? *(dùng `need` như động từ thường)*
6. Students under 18 __________ (must / not / enter) the chemical storage area under any **circumstance**.
7. Why __________ she __________ (have to / postpone) her business trip to Singapore yesterday?

---

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi/Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — `must` (chủ quan) vs. `have to` (khách quan), chia thì của `have to`, `had better`, và `should/ought to have V3`.*

**Chọn đáp án hoặc chia dạng đúng nhất trong ngoặc cho từng ngữ cảnh.**

8. My eyesight has gotten worse lately; I really __________ (must / have to) book an appointment with an optometrist soon. *(người nói tự nhận thấy sự cấp thiết)*
9. In this country, wearing a helmet is **compulsory**, so all motorcyclists __________ (must / have to) wear one by law. *(quy định khách quan từ pháp luật)*
10. Because the server crashed last night, the engineers __________ (must / had to) work until 3 a.m. to **rectify** the error.
11. If the traffic worsens, commuters __________ (will must / will have to) leave home thirty minutes earlier next month.
12. You look exhausted! You __________ (shouldn't stay / shouldn't have stayed) up until 2 a.m. playing video games last night.
13. Look at the dark sky—a storm is **imminent**. We __________ ('d better / 'd better to) secure the windows before the wind picks up.
14. Since 2022, small clinics __________ (have had to / have must) follow stricter hygiene inspections every quarter.
15. In general, young professionals __________ (should / had better) build an emergency fund early in their careers. *(lời khuyên chung chung)*

---

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.2 & 3.3 — `mustn't` vs. `don't have to` / `needn't`, và `didn't need to V0` vs. `needn't have V3/ed`.*

**Hoàn thành câu với cấu trúc phù hợp nhất từ gợi ý trong ngoặc.**

16. Interns are **exempt** from the weekend shift, so they __________ (mustn't / don't have to) come to the office on Saturdays.
17. Visitors __________ (mustn't / don't have to) touch the exhibits; doing so triggers the security alarm immediately.
18. Wait—why did you wash all those dishes by hand? You __________ (didn't need to load / needn't have washed) them; we have a dishwasher!
19. The company announced that the training was optional, so I __________ (didn't need to attend / needn't have attended) and spent the afternoon finishing my report instead.
20. **A**: "Must I bring a printed ticket to the conference?" — **B**: "No, you __________. Just show the QR code on your phone." *(mustn't / needn't)*
21. You __________ (mustn't / don't need to) disclose your PIN to anyone, or you will **compromise** your bank account.
22. She __________ (needn't have cooked / didn't need to cook) a huge dinner last night—everyone had already eaten at the airport before arriving!
23. Because the **mandatory** form was already available online, applicants __________ (didn't need to visit / needn't have visited) the municipal office in person.

---

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).**

24. Under no circumstances __________ confidential customer records on an unencrypted USB drive.
    - A. employees must store
    - B. must employees store
    - C. employees don't have to store
    - D. needn't employees store

25. You __________ your decision before rejecting the scholarship; an opportunity like this rarely comes twice.
    - A. ought to reconsider
    - B. ought reconsider
    - C. mustn't reconsider
    - D. had better to reconsider

26. Drivers who __________ the parking **prohibition** outside the emergency exit will face a heavy **penalty**.
    - A. adhere to
    - B. exempt
    - C. violate
    - D. acknowledge

27. We __________ so many extra chairs for the seminar because half of the attendees ended up joining online.
    - A. didn't need to arrange
    - B. needn't have arranged
    - C. mustn't have arranged
    - D. hadn't better arrange

28. You __________ wear formal attire to the casual team lunch on Friday, though you are welcome to if you want.
    - A. mustn't
    - B. shouldn't
    - C. don't have to
    - D. hadn't better

29. The lab technician __________ all safety **precautions** before handling the acid, which is why the minor spill happened.
    - A. should take
    - B. ought to have taken
    - C. needn't have taken
    - D. mustn't take

30. You __________ submit the tax return before midnight, otherwise the system will charge an automatic late fee.
    - A. 'd better
    - B. 'd rather
    - C. needn't
    - D. shouldn't have

31. Because the **redundant** clause was removed from the draft, the legal team __________ rewrite the entire agreement yesterday.
    - A. didn't need to
    - B. needn't have
    - C. mustn't
    - D. hasn't had to

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.3 — Các lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay gặp.*

**Mỗi câu sau có MỘT lỗi sai về ngữ pháp hoặc cách dùng động từ khuyết thiếu. Tìm phần sai và viết lại cho đúng.**

32. Because the flight was delayed by fog, all passengers **must** wait in the departure lounge for four hours yesterday.
33. You **had better to consult** a financial advisor before investing all your savings in a single startup.
34. **A**: "Do I have to bring my original ID card?" — **B**: "No, you **mustn't**; a clear photo on your phone is completely fine."
35. Why did you buy three bottles of milk? You **didn't need to buy** so much—we already have two cartons in the fridge!
36. She **doesn't ought to** speak to her colleagues in such an impatient tone during meetings.
37. If the company fails to **rectify** the defect, it **will must** recall thousands of defective units next quarter.
38. You **didn't had better** **neglect** your visa renewal, or you may face serious legal **consequences**.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp các cấu trúc tương đương về nghĩa vụ, cấm đoán, không cần thiết và lời khuyên.*

**Viết lại mỗi câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ gợi ý trong ngoặc.**

39. It is **mandatory** for all laboratory staff to wear protective goggles inside this zone. *(MUST)*
    → All laboratory staff __________________________________________________.

40. It isn't necessary for you to **acknowledge** automated system notifications. *(NEEDN'T)*
    → You ________________________________________________________________.

41. Parking bicycles in front of the fire exit is **strictly** prohibited. *(MUSTN'T)*
    → You ________________________________________________________________.

42. It was a mistake for him to **postpone** the medical check-up for six months. *(SHOULDN'T)*
    → He _________________________________________________________________.

43. We bought expensive winter boots, but it turned out the weather was mild all week. *(NEEDN'T HAVE)*
    → We _________________________________________________________________.

44. I strongly advise you to back up your files right now, or you might lose your thesis. *(BETTER)*
    → You ________________________________________________________________.

## Part 7 (Câu 45–50): Hoàn thành đoạn văn đời sống & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 5 & 6 — Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt & Ứng dụng đời sống.*

**A. Đọc email thông báo nội bộ dưới đây và điền một động từ khuyết thiếu / cụm từ phù hợp (`must`, `mustn't`, `don't have to`, `should`, `had better`, `needn't have`) vào mỗi chỗ trống (Câu 45–47).**

> Dear Colleagues,
> As we prepare for tomorrow's annual audit to fulfill our regulatory **obligation**, please remember a few key points. First, all department heads **(45)** __________ **submit** their signed expense logs by 4:00 p.m. today; this is a non-negotiable deadline. Second, although printing paper copies used to be required, the auditors confirmed yesterday that digital files are sufficient—so those who already printed 200 pages **(46)** __________ done so! Finally, we **(47)** __________ lock our desks before leaving tonight, or confidential client files could be exposed.

**Điền đáp án tương ứng vào các vị trí đánh số trong email trên:**

45. (45) __________
46. (46) __________
47. (47) __________

**B. Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh, sử dụng đúng cấu trúc ngữ pháp của bài (Câu 48–50).**

48. Ngày hôm qua tôi đã phải tham dự một cuộc họp khẩn cấp, vì vậy tôi không thể hoàn thành báo cáo đúng hạn.
49. Nhân viên tuyệt đối không được chia sẻ mật khẩu nội bộ, nhưng thực tập sinh không phải tham gia ca trực cuối tuần.
50. Lẽ ra bạn không nên bỏ bê những cảnh báo an toàn đó; tốt hơn hết bây giờ chúng ta nên thực hiện mọi biện pháp phòng ngừa trước khi quá muộn.
