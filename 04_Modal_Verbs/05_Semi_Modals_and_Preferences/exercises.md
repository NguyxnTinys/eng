## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Công thức cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn) của `need`, `dare`, `would rather`, `prefer`, `had better`.*

Điền dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc (có thể thêm `to`, `not`, trợ động từ hoặc lùi thì nếu cần):

1. Since wearing protective helmets is obligatory here, every visitor __________ (need / sign) a safety waiver before entering.
2. You __________ (needn't / submit) the printed receipt today; a scanned PDF copy is totally fine.
3. How __________ (you / dare / disclose) our confidential pricing strategy to a rival firm without asking me?
4. Most commuters in this city prefer __________ (take) the metro to __________ (drive) through heavy morning traffic.
5. It's getting dark and the weather is worsening; we __________ (had better / not / postpone) our departure any longer.
6. Personally, I would rather __________ (negotiate) an alternative schedule than __________ (cancel) the workshop altogether.
7. __________ (Need / I / clarify) these redundant figures again, or is the summary clear enough for the board?

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: `need + V-ing`, `didn't need to V` vs `needn't have V3`, `dare sb to V`, `would rather` quá khứ & giả định.*

Chia dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu theo đúng ngữ cảnh:

8. This draft agreement looks vague in several sections; Clause 5 definitely needs __________ (scrutinize) by a senior lawyer.
9. We __________ (needn't / rush) to the airport so early this morning—when we arrived, the airline announced a three-hour delay.
10. Because the company had already confirmed online billing, I __________ (not / need / queue) at the bank yesterday, so I stayed home and finished my report.
11. Back in college, my classmates once dared me __________ (confront) the strict professor about his grading policy.
12. I'm really sorry about the argument; honestly, I would rather __________ (compromise) yesterday than jeopardize our friendship.
13. I'd rather you __________ (not / interfere) in how I prioritize my tasks during this urgent sprint.
14. Looking back on last year's merger, the CEO now wishes and would rather the legal team __________ (clarify) the tax terms earlier.
15. In formal business settings, I would prefer it if guests __________ (not / make) spontaneous changes to the agenda at the last minute.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Phân biệt `prefer` vs `would prefer`, `had better` vs `would rather`, `dare` (thường vs modal), `need` (người vs vật).*

Chọn phương án đúng nhất trong cặp gợi ý trong ngoặc:

16. In general, Mai prefers working independently **(to / than)** collaborating in large, noisy teams.
17. Thank you for the invitation, but I'd prefer **(staying / to stay)** at the hotel tonight rather than go out for dinner.
18. You **(would rather / had better)** reimburse the client before Friday; otherwise, they will file a formal complaint.
19. She is quite reluctant to speak up; she **(dare not / daren't to)** challenge the director in public meetings.
20. The HR department **(needs reviewing / needs to review)** all onboarding manuals before the new interns arrive next Monday.
21. My parents would rather my younger brother **(chooses / chose)** a stable engineering career next year instead of becoming a freelance artist.
22. Look at the time! **(Hadn't we better / Wouldn't we rather)** wrap up the discussion now so we don't miss the last train?
23. Nobody **(dare / dares to)** question the new compliance policy because violations carry heavy penalties. *(Chọn phương án chuẩn ngữ pháp khi đi với đại từ phủ định `Nobody` theo văn phong trang trọng C1 — lưu ý cấu trúc không chia `-s` của modal nếu có thể, hoặc chọn dạng đúng nhất)*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Bẫy đề thi về `would rather + S`, `had better not`, `prefer ... rather than`, `needn't have V3`.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (**A**, **B**, **C**, hoặc **D**):

24. You had better __________ any confidential documents on your desk when you leave the office tonight.
    - A. not to leave
    - B. not leave
    - C. don't leave
    - D. haven't left

25. I'd rather you __________ so much time procrastinating when the project deadline is literally tomorrow morning.
    - A. don't spend
    - B. wouldn't spend
    - C. didn't spend
    - D. haven't spent

26. Most candidates prefer to submit a digital portfolio __________ a heavy printed binder to the interview.
    - A. rather than carry
    - B. to carry
    - C. than carrying
    - D. rather than carrying

27. Look! You __________ all those redundant slides—the client asked for a five-minute verbal briefing only.
    - A. didn't need to prepare
    - B. needn't have prepared
    - C. needn't prepare
    - D. didn't need preparing

28. The newly installed security software needs __________ thoroughly before we store sensitive client records on the server.
    - A. scrutinize
    - B. to scrutinize
    - C. scrutinizing
    - D. scrutinized

29. You'd better check whether the expenses are obligatory or optional, __________?
    - A. wouldn't you
    - B. didn't you
    - C. shouldn't you
    - D. hadn't you

30. She would rather __________ an alternative route yesterday than get stuck in that two-hour highway jam.
    - A. take
    - B. took
    - C. have taken
    - D. had taken

31. Though he looked furious, the intern __________ a word during the tense negotiation.
    - A. dared not say
    - B. dared not to say
    - C. didn't dare saying
    - D. dares not say

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Các lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay mắc (Vietlish & nhầm cấu trúc).*

Mỗi câu dưới đây có **MỘT lỗi sai** ngữ pháp (được in đậm hoặc nằm trong câu). Hãy xác định phần sai và sửa lại cho đúng:

32. He **need** **to clarify** his travel budget **before** the finance team **reimburses** him. *(Chú ý lỗi chia thì/ngôi của động từ chính)*
33. Many young professionals prefer **commute** by bicycle **to** driving cars **in** congested city centers.
34. You **hadn't better** disclose the merger details **until** both boards **officially** sign the **agreement**.
35. I **would rather** you **stay** at the office **until** this urgent proposal **is submitted** tomorrow.
36. She **would prefer** **to postpone** the meeting **than** hold it **without** the key decision-makers.
37. Why did you buy **so much** bottled water? You **didn't need to buy** any—look, the fridge is **already** full of it!
38. My manager **dared** me **confronting** the difficult client **alone**, even though I was **reluctant** to do so.

### A. Hoàn thành đoạn email công việc (Câu 45–47)
Chọn đáp án đúng nhất để điền vào mỗi chỗ trống (45), (46), (47) trong email dưới đây:

> *Subject: Urgent Update on Q3 Budget Review*
>
> *Dear Team,*
> *As the quarterly audit is approaching, I want to remind everyone that submitting receipts is (45) __________ under our new policy, so please upload them by Thursday. However, you (46) __________ spend hours writing lengthy summaries for minor purchases—a brief bullet list is sufficient. Also, since several figures in Section 2 look inconsistent, I would rather the accounting lead (47) __________ them before Friday's meeting so we do not jeopardize our approval timeline.*
> *Best regards,*
> *Hanh Nguyen*

45. A. spontaneous | B. obligatory | C. redundant | D. reluctant
46. A. needn't | B. don't need | C. daren't | D. had better not to
47. A. scrutinizes | B. scrutinize | C. scrutinized | D. to scrutinize

### B. Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 48–50)
Dịch các câu tiếng Việt sau sang tiếng Anh, bắt buộc sử dụng cấu trúc trọng tâm và từ vựng phù hợp trong bài:

48. Bạn tốt hơn hết đừng trì hoãn việc nộp báo cáo nữa, nếu không việc đó có thể gây nguy hại đến cơ hội thăng tiến của bạn.
49. Hầu hết nhân viên thích thương lượng thời gian làm việc linh hoạt hơn là phải đi làm xa mỗi ngày trong giờ cao điểm.
50. Đáng lẽ hôm qua anh không cần phải tiết lộ thông tin mật đó đâu; tôi thà bạn đã tham khảo ý kiến của tôi trước.
