## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (chia đúng dạng động từ nguyên mẫu không `to`, `be V-ing`, phủ định `can't / may not`, và cấu trúc câu hỏi suy đoán).*

**Điền dạng đúng của động từ khuyết thiếu trong ngoặc kết hợp với động từ cho sẵn.**

1. Look at all those boxes outside his office; he _________________________ (must / move) to another department today.
2. That antique watch _________________________ (can't / be) **genuine** — the brand logo is misspelled.
3. Let's check the server room; the cooling fan _________________________ (might / **malfunction**) right now.
4. _________________________ (could / those data) **indicate** an error in our quarterly calculations?
5. Don't **assume** she is ignoring you; she _________________________ (may / not / check) her phone right now.
6. The CEO has been in back-to-back meetings since 7 a.m.; she _________________________ (must / feel) completely **exhausted** now.
7. Where _________________________ (can / Minh / hide) the spare keys? I have looked everywhere in the drawer.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (thang độ chắc chắn 95%+, `should/ought to` theo lẽ thường, `can't/couldn't` thay cho `mustn't`, trạng từ `well/possibly`, động từ trạng thái stative verbs).*

**Chọn hoặc điền modal verb phù hợp nhất (`must`, `can't`, `couldn't`, `should`, `ought to`, `may`, `might`, `could`) kèm dạng đúng của động từ (nếu có).**

8. Nobody answers the doorbell and all the curtains are closed. They _________________________ (be) out at the moment.
9. You _________________________ (not / be) serious! Expecting us to finish a two-week project in one night is completely **implausible**.
10. Our courier service is very **reliable**, so the legal documents _________________________ (arrive) at your office by 3 p.m. today.
11. Be careful when driving along the coast; weather conditions _________________________ (**fluctuate**) dramatically this week.
12. Even though she isn't picking up, she _________________________ (well / **commute**) on the noisy subway right now.
13. Without clear **evidence**, we can only **speculate**; the committee _________________________ (**postpone**) the final vote until next month.
14. That man over there _________________________ (not / be) Dr. Tuan — Dr. Tuan is currently lecturing in Singapore!
15. Judging by his calm tone, he _________________________ (**know**) the **tentative** results already. *(Lưu ý quy tắc stative verb!)*

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết & Phân biệt sắc thái (`must` suy đoán vs bắt buộc; `can't` suy đoán vs năng lực; `couldn't` vs `might not`; `may be` vs `maybe`; `V-inf` vs `be V-ing`).*

**Khoanh tròn hoặc viết lại phương án ĐÚNG trong cặp từ in nghiêng cho mỗi câu.**

16. Listen! Someone is knocking on the back door. It *(must be / must have to be)* the technician we called earlier.
17. *(Maybe / May be)* the recent drop in sales is just a temporary trend rather than a permanent crisis.
18. Don't rely on this **tentative** schedule; the organizers *(couldn't / might not)* confirm the venue until tomorrow morning.
19. Why is the highway completely blocked? — There *(can / could)* be a major traffic **disruption** ahead.
20. She has lived in London for ten years, so she *(must speak / must be speaking)* fluent English.
21. Under normal **circumstances**, a direct flight from Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City *(ought to take / mustn't take)* roughly two hours.
22. Look at the identical typo in both essays — this *(couldn't / may not)* be a pure **coincidence**! It is clearly copied.
23. Based on this logical **deduction** and rising raw material costs, an increase in retail prices next quarter *(must be / must)* **inevitable**.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi (loại trừ `mustn't`, `can` khẳng định đoán việc cụ thể, `Must he...?`, nhầm `couldn't` với `might not`).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).**

24. The marketing team has been preparing this campaign for months, so its launch _______ be very smooth unless an unexpected **malfunction** occurs.
    A. mustn't
    B. should
    C. can't
    D. maybe

25. "_______ that old map be a **plausible** clue to the hidden tunnel?" — "I doubt it; it looks freshly printed."
    A. Must
    B. May
    C. Could
    D. Should

26. There is zero **probability** of meeting Friday's deadline with half the staff sick; finishing on time _______ be **feasible**.
    A. mustn't
    B. may not
    C. can't
    D. shouldn't

27. We _______ a slight delay at customs, so please keep your schedule flexible for tomorrow afternoon.
    A. might anticipate
    B. must anticipating
    C. can anticipate
    D. ought anticipate

28. Look at how dark the sky is getting. A heavy thunderstorm _______ heading straight toward the city right now.
    A. can be
    B. must be
    C. mustn't be
    D. ought be

29. Why hasn't Nam replied to the group chat? — I'm not certain, but he _______ driving through a mountain tunnel with no signal.
    A. can be
    B. mustn't be
    C. could be
    D. should to be

30. Having run three marathons this year, Minh _______ be out of breath after climbing just two flights of stairs — something is wrong.
    A. mustn't
    B. might not
    C. can't
    D. shouldn't to

31. Although the witness sounds sincere, his story _______ completely **genuine**; we still need physical **evidence** to verify it.
    A. couldn't be
    B. may not be
    C. mustn't be
    D. can be
