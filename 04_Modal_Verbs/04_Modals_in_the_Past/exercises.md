## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức cơ bản (`modal + (not) + have + V3/ed`, dạng bị động & tiếp diễn).*

**Điền dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc với cấu trúc `Modal + have V3/ed` (hoặc dạng bị động / tiếp diễn phù hợp):**

1. The office lights are still on, and his coat is here. He (must / stay) _________________________ late to finish the report.
2. Why is everyone staring at me? (Could / I / say) _________________________ something inappropriate during the meeting?
3. She looks completely exhausted; she (must / work) _________________________ on that financial audit all night.
4. That sensitive document (may / send) _________________________ to the wrong client by mistake yesterday afternoon.
5. You (should / not / leave) _________________________ your laptop unattended in a crowded café.
6. How (could / they / overlook) _________________________ such clear evidence during the initial inspection?
7. They (ought / inform) _________________________ the residents in advance before shutting off the water supply.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng từng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Các cách dùng chi tiết (`must`, `can't/couldn't`, `may/might/could`, `should/ought to`, `shouldn't`, `would`).*

**Viết lại phần trong ngoặc bằng một cấu trúc `Modal + have V3/ed` chính xác nhất theo ngữ cảnh:**

8. Look at the broken window and the footprints outside. A burglar (chắc chắn đã đột nhập) _________________________ into the warehouse last night.
9. Mia speaks fluent Japanese, so she (chắc chắn không thể đã gặp khó khăn) _________________________ trouble ordering food in Tokyo.
10. You (lẽ ra có thể gọi điện) _________________________ me to say you'd be late instead of leaving me waiting in the rain! *(trách móc nhẹ)*
11. In hindsight, the management (lẽ ra nên thực hiện) _________________________ stricter precautions before launching the new system.
12. I deeply regret that remark. I (lẽ ra không nên chỉ trích) _________________________ his idea so harshly in front of the whole team.
13. Without your generous support, we (hẳn đã không thể hoàn thành) _________________________ the charity project on schedule.
14. Nobody answered the door when I knocked. They (có lẽ đã ra ngoài) _________________________ for dinner, though I'm not completely sure.
15. Completing a 50-page legal review in thirty minutes wasn't feasible; he (chắc chắn không thể đã đọc) _________________________ every single clause thoroughly.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.6 & Mục 4 — Phân biệt `needn't have V3` vs `didn't need to V`, `may not have V3` vs `can't have V3`, `could have V3` vs `should have V3`.*

**Chọn dạng đúng trong hai lựa chọn trong ngoặc:**

16. I spent two hours ironing all the shirts, only to find out the trip was cancelled. I (**needn't have ironed / didn't need to iron**) them after all.
17. The hotel provided free towels and toiletries, so we (**needn't have packed / didn't need to pack**) our own, which kept our luggage light.
18. Tom hasn't replied to my email yet. He (**can't have checked / may not have checked**) his inbox today because he's in back-to-back workshops.
19. Tom couldn't possibly have read my email before noon because the server was down until 2 p.m. He (**can't have read / may not have read**) it at 10 a.m.
20. Look at how well the presentation turned out! We (**needn't have worried / didn't need to worry**) so much about the Q&A session last night.
21. Luckily, the thunderstorm cleared up right before the ceremony, so the organizers (**needn't have moved / didn't need to move**) the event indoors—it stayed outside as planned.
22. You drove through a red light at high speed! You (**should have killed / could have killed**) a pedestrian!
23. There is zero evidence that she deleted the file deliberately; she (**must have / might have**) hit the delete key by accident.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 — Các bẫy đề thi kinh điển (bẫy `mustn't have`, bẫy nghĩa ẩn, bị động quá khứ).*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C, hoặc D):**

24. "I can't understand how our competitor knew our pricing strategy." — "Someone from inside the company ______ the confidential figures."
    A. mustn't have leaked
    B. must have leaked
    C. would leak
    D. should have leaked

25. He ______ the deadline deliberately; he had a family emergency and was at the hospital all day.
    A. mustn't have missed
    B. shouldn't have missed
    C. couldn't have missed
    D. needn't have missed

26. The streets are completely dry this morning, so it ______ last night as the weather forecast predicted.
    A. can't have rained
    B. may have rained
    C. mustn't have rained
    D. wouldn't have rained

27. "Why did you buy three extra printer cartridges? The supply cabinet is already full!" — "Oh no! I ______ them then."
    A. didn't need to buy
    B. shouldn't buy
    C. mustn't have bought
    D. needn't have bought

28. That noise you heard upstairs ______ a cat knocking over a flowerpot, or maybe just the wind slamming the shutter.
    A. must have been
    B. can't have been
    C. could have been
    D. ought to have been

29. You ______ me before inviting five extra guests to dinner! Now we don't have enough chairs or food.
    A. must have asked
    B. might have asked
    C. can't have asked
    D. needn't have asked

30. Under normal circumstances, getting a refund without a receipt ______ out of the question, but the store manager made an exception for us.
    A. would have been
    B. must have been
    C. shouldn't have been
    D. needn't have been

31. A lot of unnecessary tension ______ if both departments had communicated their expectations clearly from day one.
    A. would avoid
    B. could have avoided
    C. could have been avoided
    D. must have been avoided

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 & 5 — Lỗi sai chia thì sau modal, `mustn't have`, nhầm `have/has/had`, lỗi `ought to` và `didn't need to`.*

**Mỗi câu dưới đây có MỘT lỗi sai về cấu trúc hoặc ngữ nghĩa của Modal Verbs. Hãy xác định phần sai và viết lại cho đúng:**

32. He **mustn't have** stolen the watch because he was having dinner with us at that exact time.
33. She **must has taken** the wrong bus; otherwise, she would have arrived twenty minutes ago.
34. Yesterday evening, you **should stay** at home and rest instead of going out in the pouring rain.
35. Why did you carry that heavy suitcase all the way up the stairs? You **didn't need to carry** it—there's an elevator right behind you!
36. In hindsight, the committee **ought to not have** approved such an irresponsible budget proposal without a second audit.
37. The antique vase **might have broke** while the movers were unloading the truck this morning.
38. Without the quick action of the paramedics, the injured cyclist **wouldn't survive** the accident last Friday.
