## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Điền từ thay thế / lược bỏ cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn).*

Điền **một đến hai từ** thích hợp (`one`, `ones`, `that`, `those`, `so`, `not`, `to`, `neither`, hoặc trợ động từ thích hợp) vào chỗ trống.

1. A: "Do you **anticipate** any delays in signing the contract?"
   B: "I don't think ______, as both parties have already reviewed the **preliminary** terms."
2. I prefer the **concise** summary on page two to the lengthy ______ at the end of the report.
3. A: "Our team hasn't received the **comprehensive** audit results yet."
   B: "______ has mine."
4. She asked me to **clarify** the **discrepancy** in the budget, and I promised I would try ______.
5. A: "Will the **compensation** package cover travel expenses as well?"
   B: "I'm afraid ______. The policy strictly excludes domestic commuting."
6. The **criteria** used for the final round are far more demanding than ______ applied during initial screening.
7. A: "I always **acknowledge** client emails within two hours."
   B: "______ do I; it keeps communication smooth and professional."

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi / Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết (1–5) & Quy tắc đặc biệt.*

Hoàn thành mỗi câu bằng cách sử dụng từ gợi ý hoặc viết dạng rút gọn/thay thế chuẩn xác nhất (tối đa 3 từ).

8. The **efficient** workflow in our branch isn't nearly as smooth as it ______________ (used).
9. Management asked staff to report any **redundant** procedures, and several senior analysts ______________ (do) promptly.
10. The **sustainable** energy output of this plant now exceeds ______________ of all neighboring facilities combined. (thay cho *the energy output*)
11. Please submit any **amendment** to the schedule before noon; ______________ (not), the current timetable will remain fixed.
12. Although the technician tried ______________ (not), he accidentally deleted the backup files.
13. When ______________ (approach) about taking the **initiative** on the merger, Mr. Tan politely declined.
14. The director wanted to **delegate** the quarterly inspection to an external agency, even though the board was **reluctant** ______________ (allow) it.
15. These **preliminary** sketches are promising, though I'd like to inspect some more detailed ______________ before approving the budget.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Phân biệt ngữ cảnh.*

Chọn phương án phù hợp nhất trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu sao cho đúng ngữ pháp và tự nhiên nhất.

16. A: "Wait—you left the **confidential** folder on the reception desk!"
    B: "______________! Thank goodness you spotted it." *(So did I / So I did / Neither did I)*
17. The company hopes to find an **alternative** supplier soon, because the current **discrepancy** in quality cannot ______________ *(be / do so / persist)* much longer—and if it *(does / does so / is)*, we will lose key clients. (Chọn đáp án cho chỗ trống thứ hai: *does / does so / is*)
18. A: "Could the **fluctuating** exchange rates force us to revise the **compensation** plan?"
    B: "I sincerely ______________." *(don't hope so / hope not / hope no)*
19. Unlike the **coherent** structure of your second essay, ______________ of your first draft lacks clear transitions. *(the one / that / those)*
20. She doesn't **allocate** as much time to staff training as she ______________. *(used to / used to be / did so)*
21. A: "Do you have any spare **batteries** for the microphone?"
    B: "No, I don't have ______________." *(ones / any / some)*
22. ______________ **feasible**, the engineering team will install the **alternative** cooling system over the weekend. *(If / When / Though)*
23. My supervisor **believes** the **remedy** will work immediately, and ______________ do most of the engineers. *(so / neither / such)*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi & Ngoại lệ.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D).

24. The **information** provided by the regional office was far more **comprehensive** than ______ from headquarters.
    A. the one
    B. ones
    C. that
    D. those

25. He claimed he didn't understand the new **criteria**, and ______ his colleagues.
    A. neither did
    B. so did
    C. neither understood
    D. did neither

26. ______ once **allocated** to the marketing department, the surplus funds cannot be transferred elsewhere.
    A. If they
    B. Once
    C. While
    D. Unless

27. I know it isn't easy to **acknowledge** a public mistake, and very few leaders are willing ______.
    A. to
    B. doing so
    C. to do
    D. it

28. Prices in the tech sector tend to **fluctuate** wildly; ______, long-term investors remain cautious.
    A. when they are
    B. when doing so
    C. if so
    D. when they do

29. A: "Will we have to draft another **amendment** if the client rejects this **one**?"
    B: "I ______; let's prepare a backup outline just in case."
    A. don't suppose
    B. suspect so
    C. am afraid not
    D. hope yes

30. ______ **reluctant** to **delegate** core responsibilities, the founder eventually realized she could not handle everything alone.
    A. Despite
    B. Although
    C. Because
    D. While she

31. Everyone wants a **sustainable** career, yet few are prepared to take the **initiative** when asked ______.
    A. to be
    B. to do
    C. to
    D. doing

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai kinh điển của người Việt.*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** liên quan đến Lược bỏ hoặc Thay thế (hoặc lỗi ngữ pháp kéo theo). Hãy **chỉ ra phần sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng**.

32. The **efficient** software we installed yesterday is much faster than **old one** we used in 2022.
33. A: "Are the **preliminary** test results likely to delay the launch?" — B: "I **don't hope so**, because our clients are already waiting."
34. **While reviewing** the **compensation** claims, a serious **discrepancy** was discovered by the accountant.
35. The **advice** given by the legal consultant was more practical than **the one** offered by our internal team.
36. She **resembles** her mother in her calm decision-making, and her younger sister **does so** too.
37. The warehouse isn't nearly as well organized as it **used to**, especially after the recent staff cuts.
38. **To keep** the presentation **concise**, the speaker cut out three slides, and his co-presenter **did the same one**.
