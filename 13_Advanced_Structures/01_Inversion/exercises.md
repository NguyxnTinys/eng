## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 1 & 2 (Phân biệt Đảo ngữ Bán phần vs Hoàn toàn; Khẳng định / Phủ định / Nghi vấn).*

**Xác định loại câu / điền dạng đúng của động từ (hoặc trợ động từ + chủ ngữ + động từ) trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu.**

1. Never before __________ (the board / witness) such an unprecedented decline in quarterly revenue.
2. On the eastern ridge __________ (stand) a historic lighthouse overlooking the rocky coastline.
3. Seldom __________ (senior managers / disclose) internal restructuring plans before the annual meeting.
4. Under no circumstances __________ (you / should / compromise) the accuracy of these financial records.
5. __________ (they / anticipate) the devastating consequence of the policy change when they voted for it? *(Câu nghi vấn bình thường)*
6. Out of the dense fog __________ (emerge) two patrol boats heading straight toward the pier.
7. Little __________ (the tourists / realize) that the mountain trail had been closed due to landslides.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Đảo ngữ Trạng từ phủ định, `Only...`, `Not until`, `No sooner / Hardly`
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.1 – 3.4 (Vị trí đảo ngữ ở mệnh đề chính hay mệnh đề phụ, cặp từ nối chuẩn).*

**Hoàn thành các câu sau bằng cách viết lại phần trong ngoặc theo đúng quy tắc đảo ngữ (chia thì phù hợp).**

8. Only after the lead researcher resigned __________ (the institute / uncover) several overlooked errors in the dataset.
9. No sooner __________ (the company / trigger) a price reduction than customer orders began to soar.
10. Not until the emergency funds were allocated __________ (the clinic / manage) to resume normal operations.
11. Not only __________ (the new regulation / reduce) plastic waste, but it also encourages sustainable packaging.
12. Scarcely __________ (the conference / conclude) when reporters surrounded the keynote speaker.
13. Only by persisting through early setbacks __________ (an entrepreneur / can / accomplish) lasting success.
14. In no way __________ (the committee / be) reluctant to support transparent auditing procedures.
15. Only when the final report is verified __________ (we / will / disclose) the exact figures to the public.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh: `So / Such` & Đảo ngữ Nơi chốn / Phương hướng
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.5 – 3.6 & Mục 4 (Đảo ngữ `So/Such`, Danh từ vs Đại từ trong đảo ngữ nơi chốn, Dấu hiệu nhận biết).*

**Điền cụm từ thích hợp nhất (dựa vào gợi ý trong ngoặc) để hoàn thành các câu sau.**

16. So __________ (profound / be) the documentary's message that many viewers remained silent long after the credits rolled.
17. Here __________ (come) the delivery truck with the laboratory equipment we ordered last week!
18. Such __________ (be) the tremendous workload during tax season that accountants rarely left before midnight.
19. Look at the stray cat on the fence—oh, away __________ (it / jump) the moment we opened the window!
20. So __________ (rapidly / electricity prices / soar) that several factories suspended their night shifts.
21. Beneath the ancient oak tree __________ (lie) a rusted metal box containing handwritten letters from the 1940s.
22. Only two applicants __________ (meet) all the technical criteria for the senior engineering role. *(Chú ý bẫy `Only`!)*
23. Not a single word __________ (the witness / utter) during the entire cross-examination.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Bẫy đề thi kinh điển & Ngoại lệ).*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) cho mỗi câu sau.**

24. Hardly had the CEO announced his intention to resign __________ rumors about a merger started circulating.
   - A. than
   - B. when
   - C. then
   - D. that

25. On no account __________ the confidential medical files without written authorization from the director.
   - A. mustn't staff access
   - B. staff must access
   - C. must staff access
   - D. do staff must access

26. Only after the storm had completely subsided __________ to assess the devastating damage to the harbor.
   - A. did local authorities begin
   - B. local authorities began
   - C. began local authorities
   - D. had local authorities begun

27. __________ did the residents anticipate that the construction project would trigger such severe traffic congestion.
   - A. A little
   - B. Little
   - C. Few
   - D. Not many

28. Beside the narrow stone staircase __________ two marble statues dating back to the eighteenth century.
   - A. did stand
   - B. standing
   - C. stood
   - D. were stood

29. Such __________ the public outcry over the budget cuts that the ministry revised its proposal within forty-eight hours.
   - A. was
   - B. were
   - C. did
   - D. had

30. __________ how reluctant the shareholders were to approve the merger until the financial audit was presented.
   - A. Not until did anyone realize
   - B. Little anyone realized
   - C. Seldom did anyone realized
   - D. Little did anyone realize

31. Outside the embassy gate __________ waiting impatiently for an official statement; suddenly, out __________.
   - A. stood dozens of journalists / came she
   - B. dozens of journalists stood / she came
   - C. stood dozens of journalists / she came
   - D. did dozens of journalists stand / came she
