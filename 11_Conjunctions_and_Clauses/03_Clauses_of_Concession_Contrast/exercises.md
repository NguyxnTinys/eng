## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (`Although / Though / Even though` vs `Despite / In spite of`)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức cơ bản (Khẳng định, Phủ định, Nghi vấn).*

Điền **`although`**, **`even though`**, **`though`**, **`despite`**, hoặc **`in spite of`** vào chỗ trống sao cho đúng ngữ pháp và tự nhiên nhất (một số câu có thể có nhiều hơn một lựa chọn hợp lệ; hãy chọn phương án phù hợp nhất với sắc thái câu):

1. __________ the tight budget constraints, the engineering team delivered the prototype ahead of schedule.
2. __________ several board members remained skeptical, the director decided to implement the new strategy.
3. Why did the company reject such a lucrative offer __________ having severe financial difficulties?
4. The software is quite expensive to maintain. It is still a viable option for large corporations, __________.
5. Did the athletes continue their strenuous training __________ the coach had not arrived yet?
6. __________ not receiving substantial funding from the state, the rural clinic managed to stay open.
7. She kept smiling and stayed resilient __________ she had faced terrible adversity throughout the year.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Quy tắc biến đổi & Cấu trúc nâng cao (`Adj/Adv + as/though`, `No matter + Wh-`, `However + adj/adv`)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Quy tắc biến đổi & Cấu trúc đặc biệt B2–C1.*

Hoàn thành mỗi câu bằng cách sử dụng từ gợi ý trong ngoặc và áp dụng cấu trúc nhượng bộ nâng cao:

8. __________ (exhaustive / analysis / be), the committee still requested additional data before voting. *(Dùng cấu trúc đảo `Adj + as/though + S + V`)*
9. __________ (hard / he / try) to allocate more time to his family, his workload kept expanding. *(Dùng `Adv + though + S + V`)*
10. __________ (admire / I / his perseverance), I cannot overlook the major drawbacks of his proposal. *(Dùng cấu trúc `Much as...`)*
11. __________ (ambitious / goal / be), you can achieve it step by step if you remain disciplined. *(Dùng `No matter how...`)*
12. __________ (challenges / you / encounter) in this market, never compromise on product quality. *(Dùng `No matter what...`)*
13. __________ (lucrative / deal / may seem), do not sign any contract before reading the fine print. *(Dùng `However + adj...`)*
14. __________ (child / he / was), he showed remarkable courage when overcoming adversity. *(Dùng cấu trúc đảo danh từ không mạo từ với `as`)*
15. __________ (who / criticize / project), the researchers refused to abandon their viable hypothesis. *(Dùng `No matter who...` hoặc `Whoever...`)*

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (`However` vs `Nevertheless`, `Whereas/While` vs `On the other hand`, `V-ing` làm chủ ngữ vs sau giới từ)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng & Dấu hiệu nhận biết.*

Chọn từ/cụm từ đúng trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu:

16. __________ (Although / Despite) working under intense scrutiny is stressful, it pushes our team to maintain high standards.
17. Traditional classrooms offer face-to-face interaction, __________ (whereas / on the other hand) online courses provide greater flexibility.
18. Inflation continues to fluctuate unpredictably. __________ (Nevertheless, / Whereas,) consumer spending in the luxury sector remains strong.
19. __________ (Despite / Even though) the fact that public transport is prevalent here, many commuters still prefer driving.
20. On the one hand, this merger will cut operational costs significantly. __________ (While, / On the other hand,) it may lead to substantial job losses.
21. __________ (While / Despite) acknowledging the drawbacks of automation, the report emphasizes its long-term economic benefits.
22. The proposal looked promising on paper; __________ (however, / although,) it failed to address key environmental constraints.
23. __________ (In spite of / Although) implementing stricter rules takes time, the management team believes the change is inevitable.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi.*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (**A**, **B**, **C**, hoặc **D**):

24. __________ the project's initial drawbacks, investors agreed to allocate an additional two million dollars to it.
    - A. Although
    - B. Despite of
    - C. In spite of
    - D. Even though

25. Expensive __________ the new equipment may be, it is essential for keeping our factory competitive.
    - A. although
    - B. though
    - C. despite
    - D. however

26. The two sisters have completely opposite personalities: Emma is outgoing and ambitious, __________ Clara is quiet and cautious.
    - A. on the other hand
    - B. however
    - C. despite
    - D. whereas

27. __________ persuasive his presentation was, several stakeholders remained skeptical about the timeline.
    - A. However
    - B. No matter
    - C. Whatever
    - D. Even though

28. The airline promised to improve its service; __________, delays and cancellations remain prevalent during peak seasons.
    - A. although
    - B. nevertheless
    - C. whereas
    - D. in spite of

29. __________ he was reluctant to relocate, did he accept the lucrative position in Singapore?
    - A. Despite
    - B. Because
    - C. Even though
    - D. However

30. No matter __________ we allocate the funds, someone will complain that their department did not receive enough.
    - A. what
    - B. how
    - C. which
    - D. that

31. __________ the fact that oil prices continue to fluctuate, renewable energy firms are attracting substantial investment.
    - A. Although
    - B. While
    - C. Despite
    - D. Nevertheless

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai thường gặp & Liên hệ tư duy tiếng Việt.*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** về cấu trúc nhượng bộ / tương phản / dấu câu. Hãy gạch chân (chỉ rõ) phần sai và viết lại cho đúng:

32. Although the company faced severe financial constraints, but it refused to compromise on employee welfare.
33. Despite of her strenuous efforts to pass the bar exam, she missed the required score by two points.
34. No matter how is the market unpredictable, resilient businesses always find ways to adapt.
35. The government launched an exhaustive campaign to reduce plastic waste, however, public awareness remains low.
36. Unlikely although it may seem, the small startup managed to overcome every obstacle and turn a profit.
37. Despite the new regulation has become prevalent across Europe, local firms are still reluctant to comply.
38. City residents enjoy substantial cultural opportunities, on the other hand, they suffer from higher living costs.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp quy tắc chuyển đổi cấu trúc.*

Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ gợi ý trong ngoặc:

39. Although the weather conditions were adverse, the rescue team reached the summit safely. *(Viết lại dùng **In spite of**)*
40. Despite feeling exhausted after the flight, the CEO attended the board meeting immediately. *(Viết lại dùng **Even though**)*
41. Even though the proposal sounds viable, it still faces intense scrutiny from regulators. *(Viết lại dùng cấu trúc đảo với **as**)*
42. No matter how strenuous the negotiation is, we must not reveal our bottom line. *(Viết lại dùng **However**)*
43. Although he acknowledged his mistake, he was reluctant to apologize publicly. *(Viết lại dùng **Despite the fact that**)*
44. My older brother thrives under pressure, while I prefer a predictable routine. *(Viết lại dùng **Whereas** đứng đầu câu)*
