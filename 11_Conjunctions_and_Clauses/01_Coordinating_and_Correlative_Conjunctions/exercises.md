# Bài tập thực hành: Liên từ Kết hợp (FANBOYS) & Tương quan (50 câu)

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Điền liên từ cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức cơ bản & Bảng tóm tắt FANBOYS / Tương quan.*

Điền một liên từ FANBOYS (`for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so`) hoặc cặp liên từ tương quan (`both...and, either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also, whether...or`) phù hợp nhất vào mỗi chỗ trống.

1. The new logistics software is ______ fast ______ remarkably **reliable** for daily operations.
2. We tried to **negotiate** a lower fee, ______ the vendor refused to change their pricing policy.
3. She cannot decide ______ to accept the overseas offer ______ stay with her current team.
4. You must submit the safety checklist before noon, ______ the inspection will be **postponed** until next week.
5. The startup faced a severe budget **constraint**, ______ it still achieved **substantial** growth in Q4.
6. ______ the project lead ______ the senior engineers were **reluctant** to take on the urgent task; everyone stepped up willingly.
7. Our operating costs started to **exceed** initial forecasts, ______ the finance team revised the quarterly budget.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Hòa hợp Chủ–Vị, Cấu trúc Song song & Đảo ngữ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Quy tắc hòa hợp chủ–vị, Cấu trúc song song & Đảo ngữ.*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc (hoặc viết lại cụm từ trong ngoặc) cho đúng ngữ pháp về **hòa hợp chủ–vị**, **tính song song** hoặc **đảo ngữ**.

8. Both clear communication and **consistent** effort ______ (be) **vital** for building team trust.
9. Neither the CEO nor the regional managers ______ (have) approved the revised **allocate** plan yet. *(Lưu ý: dùng đúng thì Hiện tại hoàn thành với từ trong ngoặc).*
10. Either the marketing interns or the team leader ______ (be) responsible for updating the **priority** list every morning.
11. Not only ______ (the committee / reject) the proposal yesterday, but they also questioned whether it was **feasible**.
12. To **implement** this reform smoothly, we need both to train staff thoroughly and ______ (monitor) their progress weekly.
13. The consultant was neither willing to **compromise** on quality nor ready ______ (ignore) client feedback.
14. They did not **acknowledge** the error in the report, nor ______ (they / offer) any explanation for the delay.
15. Not only the deadlines but also the workload ______ (seem) likely to **overwhelm** new recruits this month.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh, Dấu câu & Chức năng từ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết, Quy tắc dấu phẩy & Dấu hiệu nhận biết.*

Hoàn thành các câu sau bằng cách chọn phương án đúng trong ngoặc hoặc sửa/thêm dấu phẩy (nếu cần). Nếu câu đã đúng dấu câu, ghi **Correct**.

16. We decided to **persist** with the pilot program (for / so) early feedback from test users was encouraging.
17. The team reviewed the contract carefully (and / , and) highlighted three **crucial** clauses. *(Chọn phương án đúng dấu phẩy).*
18. You can **collaborate** with the design unit (or / , or) you can handle the layout independently. *(Chọn phương án đúng dấu phẩy).*
19. Have they **guarantee**d **adequate** funding for the research lab (yet / but)? *(Chọn từ đúng và chia dạng nếu cần).*
20. (**Whether / If**) we expand into Asia **or** focus on Europe depends heavily on next quarter's revenue.
21. The explanation sounded simple, (**yet / so**) few analysts understood how to **implement** it in practice.
22. Check whether the punctuation is correct: *Both the sales team, and the support staff attended the workshop.*
23. Check whether the punctuation is correct: *We need reliable servers, adequate bandwidth, and consistent monitoring.*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng (Bẫy đề thi B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Ngoại lệ & 7 Bẫy đề thi kinh điển.*

Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (**A, B, C** hoặc **D**) để hoàn thành mỗi câu sau.

24. The director asked us to check ______ the proposed timeline was genuinely **feasible** or not.
    - A. if
    - B. whether
    - C. either
    - D. that

25. ______ did the new policy reduce waste, ______ it also lowered electricity bills by 20%.
    - A. Not only / but
    - B. Both / and
    - C. Neither / nor
    - D. Either / or

26. Although the technical **constraint** was serious, ______ the engineers remained calm and **resilient**.
    - A. yet
    - B. but
    - C. so
    - D. Ø (không điền gì)

27. The supplier failed to deliver on time, ______ did they **acknowledge** our repeated messages.
    - A. or
    - B. neither
    - C. nor
    - D. and

28. Because we lacked **adequate** data to support the claim, ______ we had to **postpone** the presentation.
    - A. so
    - B. therefore
    - C. for
    - D. Ø (không điền gì)

29. To win the bid, our proposal must be ______ innovative in design ______ realistic in budget.
    - A. not only / or
    - B. both / and
    - C. neither / or
    - D. whether / nor

30. The manager promised to **allocate** extra hours to the project, ______ the tight schedule still seemed to **overwhelm** the junior staff.
    - A. for
    - B. so
    - C. yet
    - D. nor

31. Neither the client nor the contractors ______ willing to **compromise** on the safety standards.
    - A. is
    - B. was
    - C. are
    - D. has been

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Lỗi sai thường gặp & Quy tắc song song / dấu phẩy.*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** được đánh dấu trong 4 phần **[A], [B], [C], [D]**. Hãy chỉ ra chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và **viết lại phần sửa đúng**.

32. **[A] Both** the founder **[B] as well as** the investors **[C] agreed** that **consistent** quality is **[D] crucial**.
33. The new regulation **[A] didn't** improve efficiency, **[B] neither** **[C] did it** **guarantee** **[D] substantial** savings.
34. **[A] She** wants **[B] either** to **negotiate** a higher salary **[C] or** **[D] finding** a more **reliable** employer.
35. **[A] Because** travel expenses began to **exceed** our limit, **[B] so** the department **[C] decided to** **postpone** **[D] the** conference.
36. The software is **[A] easy** to use, **[B] it** is **[C] not** **feasible** for large companies with **[D] complex** security needs.
37. **[A] Neither** the supervisor **[B] nor** the assistant **[C] were** **reluctant** to **[D] collaborate** with the external auditors.
38. **[A] Not only** the report lacked **[B] adequate** evidence, **[C] but** it also ignored our **[D] top** **priority**.

## Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu & Chuyển đổi cấu trúc
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc FANBOYS & Tương quan.*

Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ/cụm từ gợi ý trong ngoặc.

39. The company didn't **allocate** more funds, and it didn't hire extra staff either. *(Dùng `neither...nor`)*
    → The company ____________________________________________________.

40. Even though the market was unstable, the local business remained **resilient**. *(Dùng `yet`)*
    → The market ____________________________________________________.

41. You must **persist** with daily practice, or your speaking skills will not become **consistent**. *(Dùng `if`)*
    → If ____________________________________________________.

42. The updated plan protects user privacy and speeds up processing at the same time. *(Đưa `Not only` lên đầu câu)*
    → Not only ____________________________________________________.

43. Maybe the director will attend the workshop, or maybe her deputy will. *(Dùng `Either...or`)*
    → Either ____________________________________________________.

44. We decided to **implement** the backup plan because the main server wasn't **reliable**. *(Dùng `so`)*
    → The main server ____________________________________________________.
