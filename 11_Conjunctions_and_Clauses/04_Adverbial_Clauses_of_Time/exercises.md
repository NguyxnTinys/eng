# Bài tập Thực hành: Mệnh đề Trạng ngữ chỉ Thời gian & Phối hợp thì (B1–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài). Kiểm tra kỹ trục thời gian, Nguyên tắc VÀNG (không dùng `will`/`would` trong mệnh đề thời gian) và đối chiếu đáp án chi tiết trong file `answers.md`.

---

## Part 1: Nhận diện & Chia động từ cơ bản — Nguyên tắc VÀNG (Câu 1–7)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 1 & Mục 2 — Không dùng `will`/`would` trong mệnh đề thời gian; giữ trật tự khẳng định/phủ định/nghi vấn đúng.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì thích hợp:**

1. As soon as the committee __________ (finalize) the schedule, we will notify all participants via email.
2. Please do not submit the application form until you __________ (review) every preliminary requirement.
3. __________ (you / resume) the training session after the technical disruption ends?
4. When the autumn semester __________ (commence) next month, first-year students will attend an orientation week.
5. Before she __________ (leave) the office every evening, she always backs up the shared database.
6. We won't implement the new software until the IT team __________ (resolve) the security flaw.
7. What will the project leader do once she __________ (obtain) the board's consensus?

---

## Part 2: Áp dụng Cách dùng & Phối hợp thì (Câu 8–15)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Phối hợp thì Quá khứ (`when`/`while`/`after`/`before`), `By the time`, `Since`, và Hiện tại hoàn thành nhấn mạnh hoàn tất.*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc sao cho đúng ngữ cảnh và trục thời gian:**

8. By the time the conference opens tomorrow morning, the staff __________ (prepare) all the welcome kits.
9. While the analysts __________ (evaluate) the preliminary figures, the server suddenly crashed.
10. Our company __________ (win) four consecutive industry awards since we adopted this comprehensive strategy.
11. By the time the paramedics arrived at the scene, the bystanders __________ (already / move) the injured cyclist to the pavement.
12. After the researchers __________ (obtain) sufficient evidence from the trials, they published their subsequent report.
13. You can go home as soon as you __________ (complete) all the safety checks—not a minute before! *(Nhấn mạnh hoàn tất xong hẳn)*
14. While Mark was drafting the travel itinerary, his assistant __________ (confirm) the hotel reservations on the phone.
15. It has been nearly two years since the two organizations last __________ (collaborate) on a regional campaign.

---

## Part 3: Phân biệt Ngữ cảnh & Chọn Liên từ thời gian (Câu 16–23)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 & Mục 4 — Phân biệt `until` vs `by the time`, `while` vs `when`, `since`, `once`, `as soon as`, `before`, `after`.*

**Chọn liên từ thích hợp nhất trong khung để điền vào mỗi chỗ trống (mỗi từ có thể dùng một lần):**
> `by the time` | `until` | `since` | `while` | `once` | `before` | `as soon as` | `when`

16. We must wait in the conference room __________ the external auditors finish checking the files.
17. __________ the rescue team reached the village, the floodwaters had already damaged most of the bridges.
18. __________ you have obtained a valid visa, you are allowed to book your flight tickets.
19. __________ the presenter was explaining the crucial timeline, several attendees were taking detailed notes.
20. The factory has experienced far fewer disruptions __________ the new backup generator was installed last spring.
21. Prior to signing any legal agreement, always read the terms carefully __________ you commit to a long-term partnership.
22. __________ the alarm rings, drop your work immediately and head toward the nearest emergency exit.
23. I was just stepping into the elevator __________ my supervisor called to postpone the briefing.

## Part 4: Trắc nghiệm Chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D — Gài bẫy B1–C1 (Câu 24–31)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Bẫy mệnh đề danh ngữ với `when`, đảo ngữ `No sooner`/`Hardly`/`Not until`, rút gọn mệnh đề thời gian, và `By the time`.*

**Chọn một đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):**

24. The logistics manager isn't sure when the delayed shipment __________ at the port, so we may have to postpone the launch.
    - A. arrives
    - B. will arrive
    - C. arrived
    - D. had arrived

25. No sooner __________ the comprehensive proposal than the client requested several subsequent revisions.
    - A. we had submitted
    - B. had we submitted
    - C. did we submit
    - D. we submitted

26. __________ thoroughly, the delicate ceramic artifacts will be displayed in the main gallery.
    - A. Once cleaning
    - B. Once having cleaned
    - C. Once cleaned
    - D. Once to clean

27. By the time the current director retires in December, she __________ with this institution for thirty consecutive years.
    - A. will have been collaborating
    - B. has collaborated
    - C. will collaborate
    - D. had been collaborating

28. Hardly had the CEO announced the merger __________ the company's share price surged dramatically.
    - A. than
    - B. until
    - C. since
    - D. when

29. Not until the independent committee __________ the preliminary findings will the ministry release an official statement.
    - A. will verify
    - B. verified
    - C. has verified
    - D. did verify

30. While __________ the updated itinerary, I noticed a crucial scheduling conflict between the two workshops.
    - A. reviewed
    - B. reviewing
    - C. was reviewing
    - D. to review

31. Tell me the exact moment when you __________ ready, and I'll send the courier over to collect the documents.
    - A. will be
    - B. would be
    - C. are
    - D. had been

## Part 5: Tìm và Sửa lỗi sai — Error Identification & Correction (Câu 32–38)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 & Mục 5 — Lỗi dùng `will`/`would` trong MĐTN, lỗi rút gọn sai chủ ngữ, lỗi `until`/`by`, `since`, đảo ngữ `Not until`.*

**Mỗi câu sau có MỘT lỗi sai về cấu trúc hoặc thì liên quan đến mệnh đề thời gian. Hãy chỉ ra phần sai và sửa lại cho đúng:**

32. As soon as the board **will reach** a consensus next Tuesday, the spokesperson **will notify** the press.
33. Since our department **implemented** the automated system last quarter, productivity **increased** by nearly twenty percent.
34. While **walking** across the campus yesterday afternoon, a heavy briefcase **dropped** right in front of me.
35. Not until **did the technicians resolve** the server disruption **could employees** access the client portal.
36. By the time the keynote speaker **arrives** at the hall yesterday, over two hundred guests **had already taken** their seats.
37. Please make sure you **finalize** the budget **until** Friday afternoon so that we can meet the client's deadline.
38. Scarcely **had the plane landed** **than** the passengers **began** unfastening their seatbelts.

## Part 6: Viết lại câu / Chuyển đổi cấu trúc giữ nguyên nghĩa (Câu 39–44)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc `No sooner... than`, `Hardly... when`, `Not until`, `By the time`, `Since`, và rút gọn mệnh đề.*

**Viết lại mỗi câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng từ gợi ý hoặc cấu trúc mở đầu cho sẵn:**

39. Immediately after the team submitted the preliminary draft, the manager spotted two crucial errors.
    → No sooner ____________________________________________________________________.

40. We didn't resume the construction project until we obtained full approval from the city council.
    → Not until _____________________________________________________________________.

41. The power outage occurred right after the live broadcast commenced.
    → Hardly ________________________________________________________________________.

42. Before you implement any major changes to the itinerary, consult all team members. *(Dùng rút gọn hoặc `prior to`)*
    → Prior to ______________________________________________________________________.

43. She started collaborating with the research institute five years ago, and she still works there today.
    → It has been ___________________________________________________________________.

44. First, the engineers will resolve the software bug; after that, we will release the update.
    → By the time we release the update, _____________________________________________.

## Part 7: Hoàn thành đoạn văn & Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 45–50)
