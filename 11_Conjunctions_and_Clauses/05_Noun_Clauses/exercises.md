# Bài tập thực hành: Mệnh đề Danh ngữ (Noun Clauses) (B2–C1)

> **Hướng dẫn:** Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây theo 7 phần. Hãy đọc kỹ từng ngữ cảnh, kiểm tra chức năng của mệnh đề trong câu và cẩn thận với các bẫy trật tự từ, giới từ và thể giả định trước khi chọn đáp án.

---

### Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chọn từ nối cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.1 — Ba loại Mệnh đề Danh ngữ (`that`, `wh-word`, `whether/if`).*

Điền một từ nối phù hợp nhất vào mỗi chỗ trống (`that`, `what`, `whether`, `if`, `how`, `why`, `who`).

1. Experts widely acknowledge __________ early intervention can prevent major financial losses.
2. Nobody could determine __________ had leaked the confidential draft to the press before the official launch.
3. Let's wait until Friday to see __________ the revised timeline is feasible for both departments.
4. Could you explain __________ the new accounting software calculates tax deductions automatically?
5. It soon emerged __________ several crucial pages had been omitted from the final contract.
6. The board hasn't decided __________ they should relocate the headquarters to another city or expand the current office.
7. Do you happen to know __________ the keynote speaker seemed so reluctant to answer that question?

### Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng 4 vị trí trong câu & Quy tắc biến đổi / Ngoại lệ
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.2 & Mục 3 — 4 vị trí chức năng, giả chủ ngữ `It`, rút gọn `to-V` và Thể giả định.*

Hoàn thành câu với dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc hoặc chọn từ/cụm từ đúng trong ngoặc đơn.

8. What the committee decides during tomorrow's closed session __________ (have) far-reaching implications for the project's outcome.
9. It is crucial that every team member __________ (disclose) any potential conflict of interest before signing the agreement.
10. __________ (That / What / If) the company failed to guarantee customer privacy caused a sharp decline in public trust.
11. The primary concern among investors is __________ (if / whether / that) the startup can remain profitable next year.
12. Many local residents are anxious about __________ (that / how / if) the highway expansion will affect noise levels in the area.
13. After hours of debate, the panel still couldn't decide whether __________ (accept / to accept / accepting) the alternative proposal.
14. The director emphasized that the report must be submitted on time and __________ (that / what / Ø) all figures be double-checked.
15. __________ (It / What / That) seems inevitable that remote work will continue to reshape urban office demand.

### Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh (`what` vs `that`, `whether` vs `if`, `whoever/whomever/whatever/whichever`)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3.5 & Mục 4.2, 4.3, 4.5, 4.6 — Phân biệt các từ nối dễ nhầm.*

Chọn từ thích hợp nhất trong ngoặc để hoàn thành câu.

16. Please take into account __________ (what / that) the senior engineer suggested before modifying the prototype.
17. The common misconception __________ (what / that) higher salaries alone guarantee loyalty has been disproved by recent surveys.
18. Analysts are still speculating about __________ (whether / if) the central bank will raise interest rates again.
19. __________ (Whoever / Whomever / No matter who) submits the most feasible marketing plan will lead the campaign next quarter.
20. There are three vacant seats in the front row; you may take __________ (whatever / whichever / however) one you prefer.
21. The consultant will work closely with __________ (whoever / whomever) the board appoints as the new project coordinator.
22. __________ (What / That) matters most to consumers is whether a brand is transparent about its sourcing practices.
23. We are uncertain __________ (if / whether) or not the external auditors will complete their review before the deadline.

### Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A / B / C / D (Gài bẫy B2–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Bẫy trật tự từ, lồng ghép câu hỏi, giới từ và giả định.*

24. Could you clarify __________ so we can adjust the budget accordingly?
    A. how much will the renovation cost
    B. how much the renovation will cost
    C. what will the renovation cost be
    D. how much does the renovation cost

25. __________ the new regulations will stimulate innovation remains controversial among economists.
    A. If
    B. That
    C. Whether
    D. What

26. The spokesperson refused to comment on __________ the CEO had stepped down voluntarily.
    A. that
    B. if
    C. whether
    D. what

27. __________ do you think will be chosen to represent the university at the international summit?
    A. Who
    B. Whom
    C. Whoever
    D. Which

28. The newly released data clearly demonstrates __________ in the past five years.
    A. how have consumer habits shifted
    B. what have consumer habits shifted
    C. how consumer habits have shifted
    D. that have consumer habits shifted

29. The safety inspector insisted that the faulty wiring __________ before the building reopened to the public.
    A. was replaced
    B. is replaced
    C. be replaced
    D. replaces

30. Our final decision depends heavily on __________ during the trial phase.
    A. that the software performs
    B. how reliably the software performs
    C. how reliable does the software perform
    D. whether does the software perform reliably

31. This handbook is extremely useful __________ it explains complex legal clauses in plain English.
    A. in that
    B. about that
    C. for what
    D. except what

### Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 — Các lỗi sai kinh điển khi dùng Mệnh đề Danh ngữ.*

Mỗi câu dưới đây có **MỘT lỗi sai** liên quan đến cấu trúc mệnh đề danh ngữ. Hãy xác định phần sai và viết lại phần sửa đúng.

32. **That** the witness said **during** the hearing **contradicted** the official police **report**.
33. We **are** still **uncertain** about **if** the supplier can deliver the raw materials **on schedule**.
34. **Can** you remind me **where** **did** we store the archived invoices **from** last quarter?
35. **No matter who** wins the regional **championship** will automatically **qualify** for the national **finals**.
36. **He** failed to **justify** his absence **at** the meeting **disappointed** the entire management team.
37. **Give** this security pass to **whomever** **arrives** at the main entrance **first**.
38. **It** was **emerged** later **that** the contractor had **ignored** several safety warnings.

### Part 6 (Câu 39–44): Viết lại câu / Kết hợp câu giữ nguyên nghĩa
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Tổng hợp cấu trúc chuyển đổi câu hỏi gián tiếp, giả chủ ngữ, `the fact that` và `-ever`.*

Viết lại hoặc kết hợp các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, sử dụng gợi ý trong ngoặc (nếu có).

39. "How did the research team interpret those unexpected test results?"
    → The professor asked __________________________________________________.

40. It is essential for the company to be transparent about its carbon emissions. *(Dùng `It is essential that...`)*
    → It is essential that __________________________________________________.

41. The manager was reluctant to assume responsibility. This frustrated his colleagues. *(Kết hợp thành một câu với mệnh đề danh ngữ làm Chủ ngữ, bắt đầu bằng `The fact that...`)*
    → The fact that __________________________________________________.

42. Does the proposed policy actually protect low-income households? That remains uncertain. *(Kết hợp câu, bắt đầu bằng `Whether...`)*
    → Whether __________________________________________________.

43. Anyone who breaks the confidentiality agreement will face legal consequences. *(Viết lại bắt đầu bằng `Whoever...`)*
    → Whoever __________________________________________________.

44. I don't know the specific thing that motivated her to change careers so suddenly. *(Dùng `what`)*
    → I don't know __________________________________________________.
