### Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản (`at / on / in`, `to / toward`, quy tắc cơ bản)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Công thức & Cách dùng `at / on / in` và `to / toward` (bao gồm quy tắc `home` và phương tiện).*

Điền giới từ thích hợp (`at`, `on`, `in`, `to`, `toward`, hoặc viết `∅` nếu không cần giới từ) vào chỗ trống:

1. Our new branch office is located _______ 74 Oakwood **Avenue**, right next to the city library.
2. Please leave the heavy maintenance equipment _______ the **basement**, not in the main lobby.
3. Because of the heavy rain, the commuters decided not to stand outside; instead, they waited _______ the train station.
4. Is the human resources department located _______ the third floor of this building?
5. After finishing her overtime shift at 8 p.m., Linh drove straight _______ home without stopping anywhere.
6. The stray dog walked slowly _______ the security guard at the main **entrance**, wagging its tail cautiously.
7. Why did you leave your laptop _______ a taxi during your morning **commute**?

### Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc đặc biệt (`arrive at/in`, `above/over`, `below/under`, `between/among`, `into/onto`)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Cách dùng chi tiết 3.1–3.5 (trục thẳng đứng, nhóm/cặp, chuyển động vào trong/lên trên).*

Chọn hoặc điền giới từ chính xác nhất vào mỗi chỗ trống:

8. By the time the international delegates **arrived _______** Heathrow Airport, the shuttle bus was already waiting. (`at` / `in` / `to`)
9. When the marathon runners **arrived _______** Boston, thousands of spectators cheered along the streets. (`at` / `in` / `at to`)
10. An **elevated** skybridge was constructed 15 meters _______ the busy **intersection** to improve **pedestrian** safety. (`above` / `under` / `onto`)
11. To protect the wooden floor during the **renovation**, the workers laid thick plastic sheets _______ the entire surface. (`above` / `over` / `among`)
12. Temperatures in the northern mountain valley dropped _______ zero shortly after midnight. (`under` / `below` / `between`)
13. The archaeologists discovered a hidden stone chamber buried deep _______ the ancient temple ruins. (`below` / `under` / `above`)
14. A narrow service road runs _______ the **residential** zone and the factory **boundary**, separating the two areas clearly. (`among` / `between` / `across`)
15. As soon as the technician stepped _______ the **elevated** platform, he checked all the safety cables. (`on` / `onto` / `into`)

### Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết (`across / through / along`, `opposite / in front of`, `between / among`, `into / in`)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Phân biệt ngữ cảnh 3.3–3.7 & Dấu hiệu động từ tĩnh/động.*

Điền một giới từ phù hợp từ khung gợi ý (có thể dùng lại): **`across` | `through` | `along` | `among` | `opposite` | `in front of` | `into` | `in`**

16. Every Sunday morning, residents take a peaceful **stroll** _______ the tree-lined **avenue** in their **suburb**.
17. Wait for the green signal before walking _______ the **pedestrian** crossing to reach the other side of the street.
18. Skilled drivers know how to **navigate** safely _______ the winding mountain tunnel even in thick fog.
19. From the balcony, we could barely spot the small cabin hidden _______ the tall pine trees in quiet **surroundings**.
20. The pharmacy is directly _______ the central hospital; you just have to cross the road from the main **entrance**.
21. Please move your motorbike! You have parked right _______ our garage door, and we cannot drive out.
22. The manager poured hot coffee _______ the ceramic mug and walked out into the **corridor**.
23. Passengers must keep their suitcases on the overhead rack, not blocking the **aisle** while they are _______ the train.

### Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục Bẫy đề thi & Ngoại lệ (cụm cố định, `premises`, `vicinity`, `proximity`, `corner`, `enter`, đảo ngữ).*

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C, hoặc D):

24. According to company policy, visitors are strictly required to wear ID badges at all times while _______ the **premises**.
    - A. in
    - B. on
    - C. into
    - D. inside of

25. Looking for affordable **accommodation** in close **proximity** _______ the subway station proved quite challenging.
    - A. with
    - B. at
    - C. to
    - D. for

26. There are three cozy cafés located _______ the **vicinity** of the historic **landmark**, making it popular with tourists.
    - A. in
    - B. on
    - C. at
    - D. among

27. _______ the dimly lit **corridor** hurried the night security officer when the alarm suddenly went off.
    - A. Into
    - B. Along
    - C. Below
    - D. Across to

28. She placed the tall floor lamp _______ the corner of the living room so it could light up the sofa.
    - A. at
    - B. on
    - C. in
    - D. to

29. When the keynote speaker _______ the crowded auditorium, everyone stood up to applaud.
    - A. entered into
    - B. entered to
    - C. arrived into
    - D. entered

30. The emergency exit is clearly marked _______ the back of the **aisle**, right next to the fire extinguisher.
    - A. at
    - B. into
    - C. across
    - D. opposite to

31. Our shuttle van will take guests directly _______ their final **destination**, whereas public buses only head _______ the city center.
    - A. toward / to
    - B. to / toward
    - C. into / at
    - D. at / onto
