# Bài tập Thực hành: Tường thuật Câu kể (Reported Statements)

> 📌 **Hướng dẫn**: Hoàn thành toàn bộ **50 câu hỏi** dưới đây (chia thành 7 dạng bài bám sát từng mảng lý thuyết). Chú ý vận dụng quy tắc `say`/`tell`, lùi thì, các trường hợp **không lùi thì**, đổi đại từ & trạng từ, cùng vốn từ vựng B1–C1 đã học.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Hoàn thành cấu trúc cơ bản với `say` / `tell`
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.1 & 2.2 (Công thức khẳng định, phủ định và phân biệt `say` vs `tell`).*

Điền **`say`**, **`said`**, **`tell`**, hoặc **`told`** vào mỗi chỗ trống cho phù hợp về ngữ pháp và nghĩa.

1. The **supervisor** ________ us that the **temporary** office policy would end next month.
2. Mia ________ to her **colleague** that she hadn't received the **confidential** file yet.
3. Please ________ me if there is any **misunderstanding** about the **deadline**.
4. The **witness** ________ that he had seen two men near the warehouse the night before.
5. Did the finance director ________ anything about the **substantial** cut in our **budget**?
6. My manager always ________ the truth, even when the quarterly results are disappointing.
7. Nobody ________ the new intern that the meeting had been **rescheduled** for Friday.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Chia động từ trong ngoặc (Lùi thì & Ngoại lệ KHÔNG lùi thì)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.3 & Cách dùng 3 (Quy tắc Backshift và 4 trường hợp không lùi thì).*

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng nhất trong câu tường thuật.

8. Lan said she ________ (work) on a **confidential** report at that moment.
9. Our astronomy professor explained that the Earth ________ (revolve) around the Sun.
10. The spokesperson **says** that the board ________ (allocate) more funds to research next year.
11. Tom told me that if he ________ (have) **reliable** **evidence**, he would contact the police immediately.
12. They admitted that they ________ (already / **postpone**) the launch twice before I joined the team.
13. The historian reminded us that World War II ________ (end) in 1945.
14. Anna told her **supervisor** that if she ________ (check) the **budget** carefully, she wouldn't have made that mistake.
15. David promised he ________ (call) me as soon as the **negotiation** finished.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Biến đổi Đại từ, Tính từ sở hữu & Trạng từ thời gian / nơi chốn
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2.4 & Cách dùng 4 (Chuyển đổi đại từ, sở hữu, thời gian và nơi chốn).*

Điền **từ/cụm từ chuyển đổi chính xác** vào chỗ trống dựa trên câu trực tiếp cho sẵn.

16. *Direct*: Minh said to Hoa, *"I will review **your** **statement** **tomorrow**."*
    *Reported*: Minh told Hoa that ________ would review ________ **statement** ________.
17. *Direct*: The staff told the manager, *"**We** submitted **our** expense claims **yesterday**."*
    *Reported*: The staff told the manager that ________ had submitted ________ expense claims ________.
18. *Direct*: Sarah said to me, *"I left **this** **confidential** folder **here** **two days ago**."*
    *Reported*: Sarah told me (when we were at a café) that she had left ________ **confidential** folder ________ ________.
19. *Direct*: *"I am **negotiating** a **permanent** contract **today**,"* Alex said last Monday.
    *Reported*: Last Monday, Alex said he was **negotiating** a **permanent** contract ________.
20. *Direct*: *"**My** team didn't receive **these** figures **last week**,"* Peter complained to Ms. Linh.
    *Reported*: Peter complained to Ms. Linh that ________ team hadn't received ________ figures ________.
21. *Direct*: *"We are going to **reschedule** the workshop **next month**,"* the organizers announced in April.
    *Reported*: In April, the organizers announced that they were going to **reschedule** the workshop ________.
22. *Direct*: *(At 9:00 AM today)* Nam said, *"I must finish the draft **today**."*
    *(At 3:00 PM the same day, you report to a friend)*: Nam said he had to finish the draft ________.
23. *Direct*: *"You can keep **mine** if **yours** is broken,"* Jenny told Ben.
    *Reported*: Jenny told Ben that he could keep ________ if ________ was broken.

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A, B, C hoặc D (B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4 (Dấu hiệu nhận biết, Ngoại lệ & Bẫy đề thi).*

Chọn phương án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D) cho mỗi câu sau.

24. The **witness** ________ the detective that she had never seen the suspect before.
    A. said
    B. told
    C. spoke
    D. talked

25. Dr. Patel explained that climate change is a complex ________ that ________ long-term global cooperation.
    A. phenomenon / required
    B. phenomena / requires
    C. phenomenon / requires
    D. phenomena / had required

26. The committee **has confirmed** that all members ________ **unanimously** to **postpone** the election.
    A. have agreed
    B. had agreed
    C. would agree
    D. were agreeing

27. When I asked about the **deadline**, my **colleague** said he ________ it to the **supervisor** the day before.
    A. already submits
    B. has already submitted
    C. had already submitted
    D. would already submit

28. Look at his pale face! The nurse said that the patient ________ in serious pain, so she called the doctor at once.
    A. had to be
    B. must be
    C. should have been
    D. could had been

29. During the meeting, the CEO stated that the new **budget** ________ **temporary** and added ________ the company would review it in Q4.
    A. is / Ø
    B. was / that
    C. has been / what
    D. had been / which

30. The lawyer warned his client that they shouldn't ________ anyone without ________ **evidence**.
    A. accuse / reliable
    B. accused / reliably
    C. accuse / reliably
    D. to accuse / reliable

31. Sarah mentioned ________ her team had **clarified** the **misunderstanding** before the clients arrived.
    A. to us
    B. us that
    C. to us that
    D. that us

## Part 5 (Câu 32–38): Tìm và sửa lỗi sai (Error Identification & Correction)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 (Các lỗi sai kinh điển người Việt hay gặp).*

Mỗi câu sau có **MỘT lỗi sai** được gạch chân (`A`, `B`, `C`, hoặc `D`). Hãy xác định chữ cái chứa lỗi sai và **viết dạng sửa đúng**.

32. My **colleague** <u>said me</u> (A) that the **confidential** contract <u>had been</u> (B) delayed <u>due to</u> (C) a minor **misunderstanding** <u>the week before</u> (D).
33. The director <u>told</u> (A) that the **substantial** **budget** <u>would be</u> (B) **allocated** to software upgrades <u>during</u> (C) the **following** quarter (D).
34. He <u>admitted</u> (A) that he <u>hadn't went</u> (B) to the **negotiation** <u>because</u> (C) his flight had been **rescheduled** <u>at the last minute</u> (D).
35. She <u>explained</u> (A) that if she <u>had had</u> (B) a **permanent** job at that time, she <u>would move</u> (C) to a larger apartment <u>near the office</u> (D). *(Câu gốc là ĐK loại 2: "If I had a permanent job now, I would move...")*
36. Our teacher <u>told us</u> (A) that water <u>boiled</u> (B) at 100 degrees Celsius <u>under</u> (C) standard atmospheric <u>pressure</u> (D).
37. The police officer <u>confirmed</u> (A) that they <u>had found</u> (B) <u>a **reliable** **evidence**</u> (C) at the scene <u>the previous night</u> (D).
38. John promised his **supervisor** <u>that</u> (A) he <u>will</u> (B) **clarify** the **statement** <u>before</u> (C) the **deadline** <u>the next day</u> (D).

### B. Dịch Việt–Anh ứng dụng (Câu 48–50)
Dịch các câu sau sang tiếng Anh, sử dụng đúng cấu trúc câu tường thuật và các từ vựng gợi ý:

48. Người giám sát bảo tôi rằng họ đã phải hoãn buổi đàm phán vì thiếu bằng chứng đáng tin cậy.
    *(Gợi ý: supervisor / postpone / negotiation / reliable evidence)*
49. Nhân chứng nói với cảnh sát rằng anh ta không hề buộc tội đồng nghiệp của mình về sự hiểu lầm đó.
    *(Gợi ý: witness / tell / accuse ... of / colleague / misunderstanding)*
50. Báo cáo gần đây nêu rõ rằng biến đổi khí hậu là một hiện tượng toàn cầu và chúng ta phải phân bổ ngân sách bền vững hơn.
    *(Gợi ý: state / phenomenon / allocate / permanent budget — chú ý thì động từ!)*
