# Bài tập thực hành: Tường thuật Mệnh lệnh, Yêu cầu & Lời khuyên (B1–B2)

> **Hướng dẫn chung:** Hoàn thành đầy đủ **50 câu hỏi** qua 7 dạng bài dưới đây. Đọc kỹ ngữ cảnh và chú ý các bẫy kinh điển (`say` vs `tell`, `offer` vs `invite`, vị trí của `not`, thiếu tân ngữ `O`). Không xem `answers.md` trước khi làm xong.

---

## Part 1 (Câu 1–7): Nhận diện & Chia dạng cơ bản
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 2 — Công thức & Cấu trúc chi tiết (Khẳng định / Phủ định / Câu hỏi chức năng)*

**Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng đúng (`to V`, `not to V`, hoặc `V-ing`) để hoàn thành câu tường thuật:**

1. The team leader told every intern ____________ (submit) their weekly progress report before 5 p.m.
2. Our supervisor warned us ____________ (not / leave) any confidential documents unattended on the printer.
3. Since I was struggling with the heavy equipment, my colleague offered ____________ (carry) the boxes to the conference room.
4. The safety inspector instructed all workers ____________ (take) every precaution before operating the new machine.
5. Local authorities have strictly prohibited factories from ____________ (release) untreated chemicals into the river.
6. My mentor encouraged me ____________ (not / hesitate) to consult a senior engineer whenever I faced a technical issue.
7. Before leaving for vacation, Ms. Hoa reminded her assistant ____________ (acknowledge) receipt of any urgent emails from clients.

## Part 2 (Câu 8–15): Áp dụng Cách dùng & Quy tắc biến đổi
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 3 — Cách dùng chi tiết & Mục 2.4 — Quy tắc biến đổi*

**Chọn động từ tường thuật phù hợp nhất trong hộp và điền cùng dạng đúng của động từ gợi ý để hoàn thành câu tường thuật (mỗi động từ tường thuật dùng một lần):**

> **Hộp động từ (chia ở quá khứ đơn):** `order` | `persuade` | `urge` | `invite` | `forbid` | `advise` | `offer` | `remind`

8. "Would you like to join our team for dinner to celebrate the project?"
   → The manager ________________ us ________________ their team for dinner to celebrate the project.
9. "Evacuate the building right now! There is a gas leak!"
   → The security guard ________________ everyone ________________ the building immediately.
10. "If I were you, I would revise the draft carefully before sending it."
    → My editor ________________ me ________________ the draft carefully before sending it.
11. "Shall I volunteer to supervise the trainees while you attend the workshop?"
    → David ________________ ________________ to supervise the trainees while I attended the workshop.
12. "Don't forget to postpone the outdoor event if the storm hits tomorrow."
    → She ________________ the coordinator ________________ the outdoor event if the storm hit the next day.
13. "Please, you really must meet the deadline tonight—our biggest client depends on it!"
    → The director ________________ the designers ________________ the deadline that night.
14. "You are strictly not allowed to use personal USB drives in this laboratory."
    → The head researcher ________________ the trainees ________________ personal USB drives in that laboratory.
15. At first I wanted to quit the course, but after talking for an hour, my teacher finally got me to stay.
    → My teacher finally ________________ me ________________ in the course.

## Part 3 (Câu 16–23): Phân biệt ngữ cảnh & Dấu hiệu nhận biết
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.1 — Dấu hiệu nhận biết & Ngoại lệ (không dùng máy móc)*

**Hoàn thành câu tường thuật dựa trên đúng chức năng giao tiếp của câu trực tiếp (phân biệt hỏi thông tin thật vs nhờ vả, mời đồ vật vs mời hành động, kể chuyện vs ra lệnh, `say` vs `tell`):**

16. "Could you speak Spanish before you moved to Madrid?" she asked me.
    → She asked me ________________ Spanish before I had moved to Madrid. *(Hỏi khả năng thật)*
17. "Could you reschedule our appointment to next Wednesday?" she asked me.
    → She asked me ________________ our appointment to the following Wednesday. *(Nhờ vả lịch sự)*
18. "Would you like a glass of iced tea after your long trip?" Linh said to Mark.
    → Linh ________________ a glass of iced tea after his long trip. *(Mời đồ uống)*
19. "Would you like to experience local hospitality at my family's house this weekend?" Linh said to Mark.
    → Linh ________________ local hospitality at her family's house that weekend. *(Mời tham gia)*
20. "The deadline is much stricter than last month," the manager told the staff.
    → The manager told the staff ________________ much stricter than the previous month. *(Kể thông tin)*
21. "Meet the deadline without making reckless mistakes," the manager told the staff.
    → The manager told the staff ________________ without making reckless mistakes. *(Ra lệnh/dặn dò)*
22. "Why don't you consult a legal specialist before signing?" he said to his partner.
    → He advised ________________ a legal specialist before signing. *(Lời khuyên)*
23. "Let me offer some assistance with those quarterly figures," Anna said to Tom.
    → Anna offered ________________ with those quarterly figures. *(Đề nghị giúp đỡ)*

## Part 4 (Câu 24–31): Trắc nghiệm chọn đáp án đúng A/B/C/D (Gài bẫy B1–C1)
*🎯 Nhắc lại lý thuyết: Mục 4.2 — Các bẫy đề thi kinh điển*

**Chọn đáp án đúng nhất (A, B, C hoặc D):**

24. The compliance officer ____________ all employees to comply with the updated data-protection rules.
    - A. said
    - B. instructed
    - C. suggested
    - D. offered

25. My father strongly warned me ____________ any reckless financial decisions without consulting an expert first.
    - A. not making
    - B. to not make
    - C. against making
    - D. not make

26. Seeing that the elderly passenger could barely lift her suitcase, a young man ____________ her carry it up the stairs.
    - A. offered to help
    - B. offered her to help
    - C. invited to help
    - D. said her to help

27. When I asked the HR manager about the extension, she ____________ me not to postpone submitting the application.
    - A. said
    - B. suggested
    - C. told
    - D. forbade

28. "Would you mind not sharing this confidential report outside the department?"
    → The director asked us ____________ that confidential report outside the department.
    - A. didn't share
    - B. not sharing
    - C. to not share
    - D. not to share

29. The coach encouraged ____________ for the community charity run even though the weather forecast looked uncertain.
    - A. to volunteer
    - B. us volunteering
    - C. us to volunteer
    - D. that we volunteer

30. The emergency team ____________ everyone in the coastal village ____________ before the typhoon arrived.
    - A. ordered / to evacuate
    - B. said / to evacuate
    - C. suggested / evacuating
    - D. offered / to evacuate

31. During the orientation, the professor ____________ the new students ____________ plagiarism in their research papers.
    - A. prohibited / to commit
    - B. warned / against committing
    - C. persuaded / not committing
    - D. asked / don't commit
